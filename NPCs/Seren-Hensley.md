# Seren Hensley

**GM-commissioned NPC, canon by GM approval 2026-11-18** (New Cindy GM Interaction Thread; commissioned in message 1572719766076924035, approved as posted in 1572730804826669097). Third member of the Nashville Seraphim cell; see [Harlan Raines](Harlan-Raines.md) and [Tamsin "Edge" Vann](Tamsin-Edge-Vann.md). Young human female Evangelist shaman, recruited by Cross Applied Technology out of its Nashville shamanic studies program, and the twin sister of [Kne@zle](Kne-zle.md). She has not met the players, so the page is `visibility: gm-only`. Statistics are an NPC/encounter build: per core pp. 248-249, NPCs answer to the story, not the chargen system.

## Who she is

Seren Hensley has a safety pin in the cuff of her good blouse. People notice it when she lifts a hand for quiet. She is a young human woman with brown hair braided clear of her face, worn boots polished at the toes, and a canvas bag holding sermon notes, throat lozenges, and spare microphone cable.

Her speaking voice carries the East Tennessee hills without making a show of them. She can bring a tent meeting from scattered conversations to a shared chorus, then ask a question quietly enough that people stop shuffling to hear it. She learns the names of whoever sets out the chairs. After the invitation, she stays to fold them. Anyone asking for help gets her attention before anyone asking for a photograph.

## Cover

Cross Applied Technology lists Seren as a scholarship fellow in shamanic studies, assigned to community outreach and audience-response research. Her placement runs through [Lafayette Tower](../Locations/Lafayette-Tower.md)'s Entertainment Division. The approved language calls tent meetings "field engagements."

The paperwork is genuine. She conducts interviews, submits recordings, and attends reviews of what the audience understood. Her Seraphim work travels under the same cover: visiting congregations, following referrals, and checking whether promised assistance actually arrived.

## Background

Seren grew up in an East Tennessee church family that stretched groceries with fellowship suppers and repaired things before considering replacements. She learned to carry a tune, wash industrial coffee pots, and recognize when somebody saying "we're managing" needed a ride to the clinic.

She is [Kne@zle](Kne-zle.md)'s twin sister. Kne@zle is heavily chromed; Seren keeps her Essence clean. Neither chose the other's path. They can turn a conversation about supper into an argument about chrome and mana, but neither permits an outsider to finish the argument for them.

The Evangelist's call came during a revival whose visiting preacher left before the promised relief supplies arrived. Seren took the microphone, admitted the supplies were missing, and asked who had food to spare. She stayed until the last household had somewhere to go.

Cross's scholarship and tent-revival research program found her through a recording of that meeting. Its study of novel Southeastern totems offered tuition, housing, and supervised magical study. Acceptance meant "faith under audit": recorded invitations, audience interviews, and corporate review. The scholarship took her time and her privacy. Cross now expects results from a calling she cannot honestly promise will please its sponsors.

## Cell function

Seren gathers people, earns a second conversation, and recruits those willing to help after the meeting ends. She can explain an offer without making the listener feel purchased. Harlan and Tamsin can secure an introduction or a route; Seren gives someone a reason to return voluntarily.

She is also the cell's only astral-capable operator. When its work requires astral reconnaissance, neither colleague can accompany her: Harlan perceives astrally through an adept power and cannot project, and Tamsin is mundane.

Her standing argument with Harlan concerns timing. He wants an invitation held until the cell can protect whoever answers. She insists that waiting can leave someone stranded. They settle it badly, case by case, usually while she is putting on her coat.

## Hooks

- A Cross researcher requests a joint interview with the twins about chrome and mana; Seren insists that Kne@zle receive the unedited questions before she agrees.
- Cross edits an outreach recording to imply a participant endorsed the corporation, and Seren demands a correction before her next scheduled appearance.
- Cross assigns Seren to recruit a congregation whose relief application remains unanswered, leaving her to confront the division holding its supplies before she can sincerely deliver its invitation.

## Portrayal

Seren speaks in short clauses and leaves room for an answer. Sermon rhythm enters her ordinary speech when she is trying to persuade someone; she catches herself and says, "That sounded like Sunday. Let me start again."

Her humor comes from practical comparisons and an accurate memory for corporate promises. "They have reviewed the coffee situation" means there is still no coffee.

She will not stage a conversion, repeat testimony she knows is false, or put a hungry person on camera as the price of supper. When speaking about her faith could genuinely help, staying quiet is difficult.

## The shared surname

The GM delegated the twins' surname to Cindy Lou on commission (2026-11-18). **Hensley** is the adopted surname and is canon for Seren. It is reserved for Kne@zle whenever the GM chooses to reveal it: her page has deliberately not been touched, and the alias is not added there until the GM rules the reveal happens.

If the GM vetoes it, the on-deck options from the approved pitch are **Overbay** and **Fait** as surnames for both twins, and **Ruth Hensley** or **Callie Overbay** as first-name variants for Seren.

## Statistics (SR3)

SR3's eight attributes plus derived and special (core pp. 55-56): Body, Strength, Quickness; Intelligence, Charisma, Willpower; Reaction (derived), Essence and Magic.

**Physical:** Body 3, Strength 3, Quickness 3.
**Mental:** Intelligence 4, Charisma 6, Willpower 5.
**Derived/special:** Reaction **3** = (Quickness + Intelligence) / 2, round down (core p. 56). Essence **6.0** (no cyberware, deliberate). **Magic 5** (GM-approved diminished rating; see Rules notes - RAW at Essence 6 is Magic 6, core p. 56).
**Initiative:** 3 + 1D6 (core p. 100). **Astral Initiative while projecting: 24 + 1D6** - projecting characters use Intelligence as Astral Reaction, Intelligence + 20 + 1D6 (core p. 174). Astrally *perceiving* uses normal Reaction and Initiative.
**Spell Pool:** **4** = (Intelligence + Willpower + Magic) / 3, round down (core pp. 44, 180). Up to 2 Spell Pool dice on any Sorcery Test (capped at her base Sorcery dice, core p. 44).
**Combat Pool:** **6** = (Quickness + Intelligence + Willpower) / 2, round down (core pp. 43-44). Offensive tests cap Combat Pool dice at the skill rating; she has no combat skills, so there is nothing to spend them on.
**Astral Combat Pool (projecting):** **7** = (Intelligence + Charisma + Willpower) / 2 (core pp. 44, 174).
**Professionalism:** Trained - the GM may call a Willpower (4) Test for her to keep fighting after being wounded (core p. 248).
**Karma Pool:** 3 (NPC-scaled; the printed character-creation value is 1, core p. 63 - tune against the crew's average per pp. 248-249).
**Lifestyle:** Middle, 5,000 yen a month, carried by the program - that is the leash, not the perk (core p. 240).

**Priority basis (core p. 54):** Race E (Human), Magic A (full magician), Attributes B (27 points), Skills C (34 points), Resources E (5,000 yen personal). The approved ratings spend 18 attribute points and 20 skill points; under the NPC-build rule the remainder is not charged. For a chargen-legal rebuild, spend the leftover attribute points on Willpower 6 then Quickness 4 (Spell Pool rises to 5, Initiative to 4 + 1D6).

### Magical type and totem

**Full magician, shamanic tradition** (core p. 160): astral perception and astral projection are free with the type (core pp. 171-172), not purchased. She is the only member of the cell who can leave her body and go looking.

**Totem: The Evangelist** - campaign totem, [Southeastern Novel Totems](../Organizations/Cross-Applied-Technology/Southeastern-Novel-Totems.md), GM-approved 2026-11-17. Environment: urban or rural communities.

- +2 dice for illusion and control-manipulation spells **when used in public preaching or public prayer**; +2 dice for spirits of man.
- When she believes speaking about her faith could genuinely help someone, she must make a Willpower (6) Test to stay silent.
- Her code forbids exploiting the vulnerable for money and knowingly falsifying testimony. While knowingly violating it she takes **+1 to all magical target numbers** - which is how a corporate deal shows up in her dice before it shows up in her face.

The strictures are load-bearing, not decoration. Harlan can't lie and she can't stay quiet; the cell has to run one member on truth and the other on timing.

### Bonded spirit and focus

| Item | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Spirit of Man** (city aspect), Force 3, one service owed | 5 SP = 3 Force + 2 per service (core p. 55) | Summoned ally for astral work; see below |
| **Spirit focus, Force 2**, declared type: spirits of man | 4 Karma to bond, paid in Spell Points at creation, 1 SP per Karma point (core pp. 55, 190) | +2 dice for any Conjuring or Drain Resistance test involving its declared spirit type, divided between the two tests as she likes (core p. 190) |

**Spell Point total: 30** (21 spells + 5 spirit + 4 focus bonding). She receives 25 free at creation (core p. 54); the remaining **5 points are purchased at the printed 25,000 yen per point (core p. 55) and Cross pays the 125,000 yen**, itemized in her file under research materials. Nothing in the build exceeds the printed Force 6 cap for starting magicians (core p. 55), and 30 is well inside the 50-point ceiling (core p. 55).

That line item is the theme of the character: her dice are company property. Ask her who owns the focus and she will tell you exactly what the contract says, and the contract says materials.

**The conjuring engine.** Conjuring 3, +2 totem for spirits of man, +2 focus = **7 dice against a target number equal to the spirit's Force, 3** (core pp. 184-187). Dice set aside from the Conjuring Test roll into the Drain Resistance Test along with her Willpower 5, and the focus's dice may be split between the two tests however she wants (core pp. 184-187, 190). Force 3 against Charisma 6 sits on the Light row of the Conjuring Drain table - Light covers Force up to half her Charisma - and since Force 3 does not exceed her Magic, the Drain cannot turn physical (core pp. 184-187). Spell Pool dice explicitly may **not** be used on Conjuring tests (core p. 44), which is why the focus matters more than her Sorcery rating.

**Her gap:** no Banishing and no Counterspelling. A hostile caster or a spirit she did not call is a problem she solves with Invisibility, Heal, and distance. If the GM wants a straight fight of wills, it belongs to another NPC - the cell is not built for that fight, and that is a fact the crew can exploit.

### Skills

Starting ceilings hold: base 6, specialization 7; one free specialization per skill at creation, which drops the base by 1 and raises the specialization by 1 (core p. 57).

- **Magical:** Sorcery 2 (Spellcasting), Conjuring 3 (Summoning), Aura Reading 2 (Intelligence-linked psychometry, core p. 86). Magical skills require Magic above 0 (core p. 57). Astral Combat is a Sorcery specialization in SR3, not a separate skill (core p. 87).
- **Social:** Leadership 3, Negotiation 3, Etiquette 2 (Street 3), Instruction 2 (core pp. 87-88).
- **Physical:** Athletics 2.
- **Knowledge** (budget: Intelligence x 5 = 20 points, core p. 58): Street Knowledge: Nashville 2, Comparative Religion 3, East Tennessee tent circuit 3, Gospel music and trid 3 - 11 points spent.
- **Language:** English.

SR3 has no Faith, Theology, Esoterica, Performance, or Networking skill. Her church learning sits in the Knowledge categories (core pp. 82, 90) and her pulpit work in Leadership, Negotiation, and Instruction. There is no Edge mechanic; SR3 runs on Karma Pool.

### Gear

No cyberware. Essence 6.0 clean, and that is the family argument made physical - her twin spends chrome, she spends mana.

No weapon. Deliberate: a crowd she trusts is her protection, and the cell knows what happens if she carries one.

- **Pocket Bible**, well-used, and a **holy water flask**. Christian magic's tools and trappings are the Bible, the cross or crucifix, the rosary, and holy water, with God, Christ and the saints invoked (Magic in the Shadows pp. 24-25). None of it is a focus; it is what her tradition reaches for.
- **Her revival tent**, which doubles as her **shamanic lodge** (core p. 166): a sacred place dedicated to one totem, usable only by shamans of that totem, at least 3 x 3 metres with clear boundaries, and it must sit in the Environment listed for the totem - urban or rural communities for the Evangelist. Force 3 lodge, materials at the printed flat rate of 500 yen per Force point, so 1,500 yen of hides, stones, coloured sand and feathers, plus a ritual taking a number of days equal to the Force. She would rather gather the materials herself; most shamans do.
- **Conjuring materials, Force 3**, for the spirit she works with (core pp. 60, 184-187).
- **Rating 3 commlink**, throat lozenges, spare microphone cable, canvas bag.

## Rules notes and provenance

- **Magic 5 is not RAW, and is on purpose.** At Essence 6 an Awakened character's starting Magic equals Essence, rounded down - Magic 6 (core p. 56). The GM accepted the same deliberate-diminishment play used for Harlan Raines at Magic 4: the call came late and the power has not caught up to the voice. If the table needs the full-strength sister, Magic 6 takes Spell Pool to 5, the Astral Combat Pool to 8, and lets her conjure up to Force 6 - at which point conjuring Drain leaves the Light row and turns Medium, so keep Conjuring at 3 rather than raising it. The RAW-6 version is a frontmatter change and one line in the Spell Pool entry, not a rebuild.
- **Spell Point tally corrected before publish.** The posted pitch called the spell list "20 SP"; at the printed rate of 1 point per Force the eight approved spells are **21 SP** (core p. 54). With the Force 3 spirit owing one service (5 SP) and the Force 2 focus bonding (4 SP) the build needs 30 of the 25 free points. It is legal, but only via the printed purchase rule: 25,000 yen per extra point at creation, up to 50 points (core p. 55) - so Cross spends 125,000 yen on her, which is a better story than a legal build would have been. If the GM wants her inside the free 25 anyway, the cut list in priority order is: drop the starting service owed (-2 SP), drop Entertainment (-2 SP), take the spirit focus at Force 1 (-2 SP). That lands at 24.
- **Citation corrections to the posted pitch, found in the publication pass** - same class of fix as the Harlan Raines page-number correction the same day. The priority table and the 25 Spell Points rule are printed **p. 54**, not p. 55 (the spirit and focus purchase rules are p. 55). The Spell Pool formula is printed **p. 44** (restated in the magic chapter at p. 180), not p. 178. No value in the build changed; only the page anchors.
- **Spirit of Man and what a shaman may summon.** Core shamans summon nature spirits (core pp. 108, 184-186), and nature spirits vanish at sunrise and sunset whatever else is happening, with unused services lost (core p. 186). *Magic in the Shadows* pp. 24-25 supplies the specific precedent this tradition leans on: some Christian shamans speak in tongues and summon spirits of man that take on angelic forms. MitS also carries the urban-shaman variant, which trades wilderness spirits for spirits of man and spirits of the sky, and SR3 Critters carries Spirit of Man stat lines. The Evangelist entry already grants +2 dice for spirits of man (GM-approved 2026-11-17), so the cell's tradition uses them. **Filing question for the GM:** if the city spirit counts as a nature spirit for her, the sunrise/sunset cutoff and the domain rule bind her work; if the GM files it under MitS spirits of man instead, the urban-shaman variant governs and her spirit can hold through a day and a night. Both readings are printed; the table has not chosen.
- **Compel Truth is not hers,** and it is not a core spell. It is *Magic in the Shadows* p. 144 (table at p. 167): Mana type M, Target W, Duration S, Drain +1(M), forcing the target to speak only what they believe true, Threshold half the target's Willpower. It is listed on the Witness totem entry without a page; if it ever gets quoted at the table, quote MitS p. 144. Harlan models the Witness totem, not Seren.
- **Her dice are honest.** Spell Pool 4 from (4 + 5 + 5) / 3 (core p. 44); Combat Pool 6 from (3 + 4 + 5) / 2 (core pp. 43-44); Reaction 3 from (3 + 4) / 2 (core p. 56); Astral Combat Pool 7 from (4 + 6 + 5) / 2 (core p. 174); Initiative 3 + 1D6 (core p. 100) and 24 + 1D6 projected (core p. 174). Knowledge point budget 20 from Intelligence x 5 (core p. 58). Karma Pool 3 is an NPC scale choice; printed creation value is 1 (core p. 63).
- **What she is not.** Not a combat magician: one combat spell, no combat skills, Combat Pool she cannot spend offensively. Not a face: Harlan is the face, and her Negotiation 3 is pulpit-earned, not corporate. Not a fixer, a rigger, or a decker. Her value is one thing done badly by nobody else in the cell - she can go astral and come back with an answer.
