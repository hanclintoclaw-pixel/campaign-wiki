# Imogene Viola

## Overview

Cross Applied Technology executive / shadowrun handler who hired the crew to recover stolen DNA sequencing and cloning equipment, and later confirmed she arranged the original Pixel Sticks takedown contract against the Matrix gang attacking CAT interests.

## Known Facts

- Imogene Viola presented herself as a senior director in forward agrarian strategy for Cross Applied Technology's CAS division.
- She showed the crew security footage of a clumsy imitation team stealing corporate equipment.
- She wanted the stolen hardware recovered and was prepared to bury the false accusation against the crew if they took the job.
- The gear in question involved high-end DNA sequencing and cloning technology.
- In Session 2026-08-27, Imogene met the crew near **[Lafayette Tower](../Locations/Lafayette-Tower.md)** / **[Little Chiba](../Locations/Little-Chiba.md)** after they brought evidence from the Pixel Sticks sponsor follow-up.
- She confirmed CAT had been attacked by the Pixel Sticks and that she arranged the original cleanup contract through the shadow pipeline.
- She paid the crew **10,000¥** for follow-up intelligence and utility samples, and indicated CAT may send future work directly rather than routing everything through **Ivan**.

## Relationships

- Linked to **Cross Applied Technology**.
- Linked to **6 Steel Strings** indirectly through the theft/impersonation aftermath.
- Linked to the **Pixel Sticks / Princeps** proxy-conflict thread as CAT's known crew-facing handler.

## Relevant Sessions

- 2024-02-01 — cloning-hardware recovery job setup.
- 2026-08-27 — accepted Pixel Sticks sponsor intelligence from the crew, paid **10,000¥**, and opened the possibility of direct CAT follow-up work.

## Open Questions

- How honest was Imogene about Cross Applied's stake in the hardware?
- What would Cross Applied have done with the technology if the crew had refused the job?
- What CAT response will Imogene pursue against Princeps after receiving the Pixel Sticks evidence?
