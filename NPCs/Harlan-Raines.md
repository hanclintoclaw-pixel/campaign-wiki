# Harlan Raines

**GM-commissioned NPC, canon by GM approval (2026-11-18).** Leader of the Nashville Seraphim cell. Page is `visibility: gm-only` because the crew has not met him and does not know the cell exists; flip sections to player-safe when the GM introduces him. Statistics are an NPC/encounter build: per core pp. 248-249, NPCs answer to the story, not the chargen system.

## Overview

Street contacts call him **Switchboard**. Outsiders call him "the Deacon"; he neither chose nor confirms it. Human man, mid-50s, graying hair cut close above the ears. Well-kept suits with resoled shoes. Carries a small clothbound hymnal with handwritten names inside the cover. Looks at whoever is speaking, including the waiter.

He is the face of the Nashville Seraphim cell: an intelligence officer whose weapon is conversation, running collection and access through entertainment-industry relationships rather than force.

## Cover identity

Director of Community and Talent Relations, Cross Applied Technology's North American Entertainment Division, operating out of Lafayette Tower. He handles troublesome introductions, artist disputes, and relationships with religious broadcasters. [Imogene Viola](Imogene-Viola.md) stays the recorded local representative; his office exists in the org chart as a liaison function, and nobody in CAT facilities questions it.

## Background - the Open Table

Raised in eastern Tennessee's independent revival churches - his people call it the **Open Table** revival: testimony, congregational singing, an invitation nobody should be pressured into accepting. He learned to arrange meals, reconcile angry relatives, and ask prosperous congregants for money without humiliating anyone. Entertainment work brought him to Nashville; shadowrunning taught him why introductions sometimes need witnesses. Cross recruited him from its retained runners.

His gifts show in conversation and attention, not displays of force; he treats them as an obligation to listen better, not proof that God endorses his employer. Before difficult business he opens the hymnal and sings a verse aloud, quietly but audibly, and carries it even when that complicates security screening.

## Seraphim cell - role and hooks

The cell works the Southeast from CAT's entertainment footprint: introductions, auditions, revival circuits, broadcaster calendars - collection disguised as hospitality. The "Southeast Division" signature on the [Seraphim File 7-AUGUST](Claude-August-CAT-Dossier.md) is a standing embarrassment: Seraphim organize by function and priority out of L'Assomption, Quebec (State of the Art 2064), not by region, and Harlan cannot authenticate that signature. He wants to learn who benefits from the forgery before he challenges its provenance publicly.

- The cell's current tasking customer sits **outside** Lafayette Tower, so Viola can employ the crew without knowing everything Harlan has been ordered to learn about the Princeps entanglement.
- He suspects the shamanic studies program (see [Southeastern Novel Totems](../Organizations/Cross-Applied-Technology/Southeastern-Novel-Totems.md)) is becoming recruitment machinery, so he quietly helps participants tell consent apart from promises made under corporate pressure.
- Whether Harlan knows whose signature really tasks him is **GM inventory** - see Open Items.

## Portrayal

Cultivated eastern Tennessee voice, courteous without going folksy on demand. Lets you finish, then asks the practical question everyone avoided. Faster while negotiating logistics, slower when discussing faith. Assigns responsibility by surname. Repeats a qualification as a question - "Only until closing?" Replaces an abstract promise with a concrete example. Use those selectively, not as catchphrases.

### Adept - Totem Way (MitS p. 22)

Totem Way adepts model a totem as an ideal, take geasa on their powers that mimic the totem's modifiers, and observe the totem's behavioural restrictions (Magic in the Shadows p. 22). Harlan models **Witness** from the approved Southeastern totems page: he never knowingly lies, including by omission in testimony, and must publicly confront a known lie when it matters - Willpower (6) to resist, or Willpower (8) when the lie is oppressing someone present and he can act. He is a professional intelligence officer bound not to lie; the cell works around it by careful framing and by letting the target confess to themselves.

Powers - **4.0 Power Points, equal to Magic** (core p. 168; maximum level in any power equals Magic Rating). Geasa follow MitS p. 33: a voluntary geas on a power costs 75 percent of standard, rounded up to the nearest quarter point, and cannot be removed; breaking a geas switches that power off without breaking the others.

## Rules notes and provenance

- **Magic 4 is deliberate.** RAW starting Magic for an Awakened character equals Essence rounded down (core p. 56), so an uncybered starting adept would carry Magic 6 and 6 Power Points (core p. 55). The GM accepted the design rationale for Magic 4 at approval (2026-11-18): Face first, cell leader not cell heavy, and the diminished rating is a story hook - a veteran who lost potency somewhere. If a table scene needs the full-strength version, the verified Magic 6 / 6.0 PP loadout is: Astral Perception 1 @ 1.5 (Incantation geas), Enhanced Perception 3 @ 1.25 (Talisman geas), Mystic Armor 3 @ 1.25 (Talisman geas), Body Control 4 @ 2.0.
- **No adept dice on social skills.** The printed Improved Ability cost table covers Physical and Combat skill categories only (adept powers, core pp. 168-173); Social skills are not eligible. His conversational uncanny is Perception 5, Charisma 6, and skill ratings. The printed route to formalize it is the **Centering** metamagic (MitS p. 72) via initiation: a Willpower-linked Centering Skill using a creative skill - singing is the obvious pick, and it pairs with the hymnal if the GM ever initiates him.
- **No Edge.** SR3 has no Edge mechanic; Karma Pool is the real resource (core). "Edge" returns zero hits as a rules term in the core corpus.
