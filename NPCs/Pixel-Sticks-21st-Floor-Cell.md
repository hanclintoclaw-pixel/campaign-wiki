# Pixel Sticks 21st-Floor Cell

## Overview

The **Pixel Sticks 21st-floor cell** was the physical crew operating from the upper floors of the [Vernon Winfrey Arkoblock](../Locations/Vernon-Winfrey-Arkoblock.md). The cell had controlled access, cameras, decker hardware, host equipment tied to the [Happy Cat Public Storefront Host](../Tech/Matrix/Happy-Cat-Public-Storefront-Host.md), and at least one extremely dangerous scorpion-like combat drone.

The crew disrupted the cell in [Session 2026-07-23](../Sessions/2026-07-23.md), recovered used utilities for the Johnson-side handoff, and left the floor in chaos while the drone fought [Chunky Sparkles](Chunky-Sparkles.md). In [Session 2026-08-27](../Sessions/2026-08-27.md), the crew questioned surviving members in Nashville PD custody and confirmed the cell had been recruited as a proxy against **[Cross Applied Technology](../Organizations/Cross-Applied-Technology.md)**.

## Known Members / Handles

- **Highbrow** - decker/leader voice encountered through an orc-barbarian Matrix icon on local display hardware; rejected Mevin's warning and threatened to have the crew thrown out.
- **Pop Onomata** - handle recovered from the member-list / route-board context and used as bluff material.
- **Ernie Pollock** - brash young orc member present on the scorpion drone's whitelist; confirmed the dead-drop / anonymous-benefactor account under Mevin's friendly questioning.
- **Ziggy Hill** - heavily chromed elf decker present on the scorpion drone's whitelist; confirmed Ernie's core account after Curtis used intimidation.
- **[Lizzo Wells / Handy-Sandy](Lizzo-Wells-Handy-Sandy.md)** - young woman associated with Happy Cat and present on the scorpion drone's whitelist; probable otaku / self-described technoshaman, Grid Overwatch person of interest, and temporary handler of Chunky Sparkles / Mr. Rainbow.
- **Unnamed guards / goons** - physical door security; one was killed by Valgaut during the breach.
- **Sickly elf decker** - found lying in or near the equipment area, status unresolved in the public notes.

## Capabilities / Resources

- Controlled 21st-floor access point with guards.
- Camera coverage and display hardware for Matrix-icon intimidation / remote speaking.
- Host terminal and Matrix infrastructure connected to the Happy Cat laser-array path.
- Used military-grade utilities recovered by the crew.
- Additional encrypted utility/paydata material retained by Mevin for later research.
- Access to or operational contact with the [Pixel Sticks Scorpion Drone](../Vehicles/Pixel-Sticks-Scorpion-Drone.md).
- The sponsor package arrived through a Matrix contact, a dead drop, and a transponder tied to the drone; the prisoners did not know how to reproduce or control the sponsor's full capability.

## Current Status

Disrupted. The crew breached the floor, disabled or intimidated several members, recovered used utilities, and triggered the scorpion drone as escape-cover. Surviving members are in Nashville PD custody and being adapted into coerced gray-hat work under Herrick's oversight. Their sponsor now appears strongly tied to Princeps / Claude August, but proof remains circumstantial.

## Open Questions

- Was Highbrow the actual leader or only the active decker on shift?
- What unfinished job was supposed to create the cell's major payday?
- Who supplied Lizzo Wells / Handy-Sandy with Chunky Sparkles / Mr. Rainbow?
- Are any Pixel Sticks outside the captured 21st-floor cell still active?
