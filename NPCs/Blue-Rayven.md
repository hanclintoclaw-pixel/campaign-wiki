# Blue Rayven

## Overview

A dealer in high-end Matrix hardware who became a key logistics lead in the early Cindy investigation.

## Known Facts

- Blue Rayven operated as a source/fence for serious Matrix hardware.
- The crew tracked him to a warehouse on the south side of town.
- He had sold enough gear to set up a "cradle" for Cindy's housing/support environment.

## Relationships

- Linked to **[Cindy Lou Jenkins](Cindy-Lou-Jenkins.md)** through support-hardware tracing. (Early records misname her **Cindy Liu Thompson**; see the name authority ruling on the [character page](Cindy-Lou-Jenkins.md#name-authority-gm-ruling-2026-11-10).)
- Linked to the broader Cindy / knowbot / influence-engine arc.
