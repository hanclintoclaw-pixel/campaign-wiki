# Lula Bell Sandusky

## Overview

Public moral crusader and city-issues commentator whose current rhetoric has become a thorn in the side of Mayor Haggar and others. She is one of the public voices calling for inquiries into Haggar's rumored possible metahuman status.

## Known Facts

- Late 60s as of the current campaign era.
- Has a clean public reputation that makes her difficult to discredit or remove openly.
- Her anti-metahuman / anti-Haggar commentary overlaps with Humanis talking points, even if her exact organizational ties are unknown.
- An anonymous Mr. Johnson hired the crew to recover old pre-Crash arrest / investigation records and physical evidence tied to her youth.
- The evidence is believed to be in the basement of the old **[White Creek Civic Center](../Locations/White-Creek-Civic-Center.md)**, now occupied by the **[Ring of Fire](../Factions/Ring-of-Fire.md)**.

## Retrieval Status

In [Session 2026-09-24](../Sessions/2026-09-24.md), the crew breached White Creek and Kilimanjaro reached a basement full of newer packages and old boxes. **Her evidence has not yet been identified or recovered**, and nothing new about the underlying case was established. The job and intended use of the evidence remain unresolved.

## Open Questions

- What did Sandusky do as a young woman during the Return-of-Magic / Crash-era chaos?
- Who wants the evidence now, and do they intend blackmail, exposure, leverage, or protection?
- Does she have a direct Humanis connection or only ideological overlap?
