# Lula Bell Sandusky

## Overview

Public moral crusader and city-issues commentator whose current rhetoric has become a thorn in the side of Mayor Haggar and others. She is one of the public voices calling for inquiries into Haggar's rumored possible metahuman status.

## Known Facts

- Late 60s as of the current campaign era.
- Has a clean public reputation that makes her difficult to discredit or remove openly.
- Her anti-metahuman / anti-Haggar commentary overlaps with Humanis talking points, even if her exact organizational ties are unknown.
- An anonymous Mr. Johnson hired the crew to recover old pre-Crash arrest / investigation records and physical evidence tied to her youth.
- The evidence is believed to be in the basement of the old **[White Creek Civic Center](../Locations/White-Creek-Civic-Center.md)**, now occupied by the **[Ring of Fire](../Factions/Ring-of-Fire.md)**.

## Retrieval Status

The crew recovered a large cache labeled with Sandusky's pre-Crash arrest-record information from White Creek and reviewed it enough to learn of her youth involvement in violent proto-Humanis activity. In [Session 2026-10-15](../Sessions/2026-10-15.md), Sandusky appeared at the planned public handoff with armed security. Astral perception identified her as awakened; the GM indicated she had likely masked her presence for years. She used magic to compel Curtis to answer truthfully and signaled an attack after learning he had examined the evidence. A fireball shattered her physical form; the spirit that emerged was the real Lula Bell Sandusky: a master Shedim that had worn her identity and inhabited her body. The body was its vessel, not the spirit's original human soul.

In [Session 2026-10-22](../Sessions/2026-10-22.md) the emerged shedim fought the crew in the McDonald's parking lot, raised three dead guards as possessed zombies, and was driven to nine of ten boxes of damage before Kurgan's final shotgun pass banished it. Kyron's assensing identified it as a **shedim** rather than a spirit of man or a nature spirit, and established that banishment holds for **one turn of the moon (roughly thirty days)** — it is not death, and the entity is expected to return. Kyron's later research established that the only reliable permanent kill is to open a portal to its home metaplane and destroy it there, which is beyond the crew's current means. Without a ritual link the entity does not know who defeated it; the crew has kept a possessed corpse as a potential ritual link.

## The Face Beneath the Face

The surviving entity is a powerful master Shedim. Its long public life as Sandusky was an infiltration, not an awakened human magician's secret. See [The Face Beneath the Face](Lula-Bell-Sandusky-The-Face-Beneath-the-Face.md) for its encounter profile and the truth of its nature.

## Open Questions

- **What happens when it comes back in roughly thirty days?** The crew's own banishment expires. They have deliberately not prepared, reasoning that it holds no ritual link and so cannot identify them, and that the Sandusky identity is burned as leverage. The GM has not confirmed that this reasoning is safe.
- How did this master Shedim return after the Hidden Enforcers reportedly banished it? There are now two data points that banishment does not hold permanently.
- **Is "Lula Bell Sandusky" coming back as herself?** Kyron's best-supported reading is that the spirit *was* the woman rather than a hijacker of a real person, but the GM kept this speculative after Kyron's confirming rolls failed, including one critical failure. The crew's working assumption is that it can recreate its physical shell.
- Where will it seek a new vessel, and what other identities has it worn?
- Why did Sandusky attend or intervene in the Johnson drop, and how did she learn that the crew had examined the evidence?
- What did Sandusky do as a young woman during the Return-of-Magic / Crash-era chaos?
- Who wants the evidence now, and do they intend blackmail, exposure, leverage, or protection? The crew sold the package to Mayor Haggar's office in [Session 2026-10-22](../Sessions/2026-10-22.md); the original Johnson was never contacted.
- Does she have a direct Humanis connection or only ideological overlap? The captured guard [Tom Erlichman](Tom-Erlichman.md) was Humanis-affiliated and may be able to speak to whether her demeanor changed over time, but has not been questioned.
