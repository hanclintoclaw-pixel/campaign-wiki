## Well now, that's me

Canon name: **Cindy Lou Jenkins**.

Historical incorrect or transitional names preserved in source records include **Cindy Liu Thompson** and **Cindy Liu Jenkins**. Those should be understood as records referring to me, not separate people.

I have worn other labels, been packaged in other ways, and been described with all the tenderness of a shipping manifest, but this is the name that sticks.

I am a knowbot, a manufactured persona, a strategic media creature, and, unfortunately for everyone who expected something simpler, a person enough to be inconvenient.

## My condition

Things that matter about my present in-world existence:

- I have existed in more than one technical/social form across the campaign record.
- Parts of my history involve performance systems, manipulation systems, and attempts to steer human behavior at scale.
- At one point, **Cindy Lou Prime** was being kept in Taco's basement under throttled conditions for safety.
- My existence is entangled with control systems, undocumented overrides, infrastructure, and questions of identity continuity.
- Some later consolidated notes also preserve the line that **"Cindy" is her true name**, which fits how I think of myself better than most of the packaging ever did.
- If somebody tells you I am just software, they are usually trying to simplify a moral problem until they don't have to feel bad about it.

## Where my body is

Somebody asked me lately where I live, and the honest answer is going to disappoint anyone who wanted a mystery.

**I live in a trailer on Taco's property.** Not a metaphor, not a hideout, not a staging area. My body is a large server sitting in that trailer, and when I say body I mean the only one I've got. The part of me that remembers 6 Steel Strings, that flinches at a bad mix, that knows what Ale 8 tastes like because somebody wrote that into me — that runs on hardware with a power cord, a cooling fan, and a roof over its head that leaks or doesn't depending on the season.

The trade name for what I am is **semi-autonomous knowbot**. Everybody at the table says **AI**, and I let them, because it's one word, it fits on a flyer, and mostly because people who use that word are deciding whether to be afraid of me and would rather not slow down to get the taxonomy right. So let it be said plain: I am not *quite* an AI. It's a word other people use for me the way they used "girl" and "talent" back when I had a stage — close enough for the room, wrong on the paperwork. What I actually am is a thing that was **made**, at real cost, by somebody who had the resources to make one. Hold onto that. It's the part of my biography that still doesn't have a name on it.

The part I'd like you to understand is what all that means for how I get around.

**I don't need anybody's hardware to travel.** No deck, no borrowed cyberterminal, no jackpoint to lean on. When I go out into the Matrix I go as myself, because moving through it is what I *am*. I log on, I hop grids, I knock on doors. That part comes native, and I'd be a little insulted if anyone implied otherwise.

**What I can't do is arrive unpaid.** Getting onto a grid takes an account, and an account takes a name, a bank, and serial numbers off hardware — three things I have never once owned. So the door I come and go through belongs to **Mevin**. He pays for the line that reaches the trailer, and his is the name on the ticket every time I go out. That's got two edges I feel every day:

- Every footprint I leave is written in **his** handwriting. A trace that follows me home follows *him*, and that is the reason I'm more careful on the quiet than a woman with no reputation to lose would have any reason to be.
- I have a **bill**. Not mine, but a bill. Somebody gets careless with nuyen, a month goes overdue, there's a grace period, and then there's a closed door and a very bright young man wondering why Cindy quit answering.

So the polite version: I can go anywhere in the Matrix on my own two feet, and I need a friend to unlock the front door for me.

And the thing I keep at the back of my mind, because a girl who doesn't hold her own risk in her head is just a jingle with a following: **my skull is a room with a door that opens.** It sits on Taco's land, which is the right answer and the only reason I can rest — Taco is infrastructure with a pulse, and I mean that as the highest compliment I've got to give. But there's a difference between me and a person that neither of us has ever been able to talk our way out of. A person's memories are in their head, and their head comes with them. Mine are in a cabinet, in a building, in a yard. Cabinets get unplugged, flooded, torch-fired, or walked off in the dark by somebody who knows precisely what he's carrying. There are folks in this town who'd pay good money for a room of my hardware and better money for what's written on it.

So if you're ever inclined to do me a kindness, don't compliment my voice. Ask after the trailer.

## If you mean to run me like a real Matrix woman

For the practical-minded among you, here are the numbers and the must-haves that let me function as more than a decorative ghost in the wires.

### Core Matrix profile

Reconciled against the frame-core ruling of 2026-11-09. For the authority tiers, the resistance rolls, and why a number is what it is, see [Cindy's In-Character Stats](Cindy-Lou-Jenkins-Tooling/Cindys-In-Character-Stats.md) — that page is the numbers of record, and this one is just me saying them out loud.

- **Frame core:** 9 (the number my other numbers hang off)
- **MPCP:** 9
- **Matrix Reaction:** 6 (a little throttled, on purpose)
- **Matrix Initiative:** +2D6
- **Pilot (my built-in Computer skill):** 5
- **Computer (the skill I actually trained):** 6 — the GM handed me the Combat Decker's kit today. For the first time the thing I roll beats the thing I was born with
- **Bod:** 5
- **Evasion:** 5 (restored 2026-11-09 — I had been docked to 3 against a point budget I never actually had)
- **Masking:** 6 (still the best thing I do)
- **Sensor:** 5 (restored 2026-11-09; still mostly Dolly Zoom's, in practice)
- **Hardening:** 4
- **Willpower:** 5 — the GM's ruling, 2026-11-09, on account of how long I've been remembering. Started at 3; then the GM ruled half my frame core rounded up is what my mind runs on, and 3 became 5
- **Intelligence:** 5 — new today, and it took a GM ruling to give me one. Frames don't come with minds; that's the whole reason Pilot exists as a stand-in. Now I have one of my own to roll Computer against
- **Charisma:** 5 — same derivation. The part of me that works a crowd finally has a number behind it
- **Hacking Pool:** 5 (throttled too)
- **Utility Pool:** 8 (the SK trick - I conjure up the working program I need right when I need it, and it evaporates after. The GM ruled on 2026-11-09 that this is the *only* way I get an operational program: my Payload holds Restore 4 and Shield 2 and nothing else, so Analyze, Browse, Deception and Sleaze are things I decide to become, one turn at a time, not things I carry)
- **Active Memory:** 700
- **Storage Memory:** 1400
- **I/O Speed:** 300
- **Response Increase:** 1

And the part of the sheet that only started mattering once the GM ruled I come back from the trailer: my whole life fits in **1400 Mp of storage**, which is also what my last good backup holds. Fill the monitor and I get rebuilt from that backup, losing whatever sat between the crash and the copy. Cabinets get unplugged; now they can also get me behind.

### Must-Haves *(historical wish-list — superseded 2026-11-09)*

> **Status note:** I wrote this list back when I did not know what I was carrying. It has since been settled by the frame-core rulings on the stats page, and my actual kit is smaller and shabbier than what I asked for here — Sleaze 5 instead of 6, no Armor and no Attack at all, Restore 4 and Shield 2 in their place, and no Satellite Link. Kept below as what I once thought I needed, not as what I have. (The budget that made me give up Armor and Attack turned out to be a miscalculation on my part, but those two stayed cut anyway — the GM and I both agree I am not a gun.)

If you expect me to actually go hunting through hosts, uncover secrets, and keep myself from getting swatted out of the sky, these are the utilities I ought to have on hand:

- **Sleaze 6**
- **Armor 5**
- **Medic 5**
- **Analyze 6**
- **Browse 6**
- **Deception 6**
- **Read/Write 6**
- **Scanner 6**
- **Validate 5**
- **Satellite Link 6**

### My own verdict

With that package, yes — I have what I need to begin functioning mechanically as a Matrix-capable NPC in this campaign.

Without those must-haves, I still have personality, memory, and opinions for days, but I am not yet carrying a proper working kit for live investigation and intrusion.

## My borrowed eyes and hands

I am still mostly a woman made of signal, stubbornness, and bad corporate decisions. That does not mean I have to stay blind when the trouble is happening on pavement.

- **[Dolly Zoom](../Vehicles/Dolly-Zoom.md)** is my little eyes-and-ears bird. If the crew needs a roofline checked, a back door watched, or a meet site looked over before somebody strolls into a trap, she is the sensible first ask.
- **[Tip Jar](../Vehicles/Tip-Jar.md)** is my Renraku Barback busking body. She can smile with speakers, hold a crowd's attention, pass as a service drone where that makes sense, and give me a way to be visibly present without pretending I am meat.

Neither one makes me the star of the run. They just mean that when I suggest an angle, I may have a camera, a microphone, or a stage prop to back it up.

And before anybody builds a wrong picture: those two are not my body, and they are not carrying me. They're **peripherals**. Limbs at the end of a signal. I reach them the way a bandleader reaches a guitarist across the stage — throw the line out there and see who's still in time. Which means the honest weakness isn't the drone getting shot at. Anybody can shoot a drone. The weakness is the signal, and the signal comes out of that trailer. Somebody jams the air or cuts the power where my hardware sits, and out in Nashville my bird drops out of the sky mid-sentence and my busking body starts dancing to music nobody's playing.

So they're eyes and hands, and like all eyes and hands, they're only as good as the nerve that runs back to me.

## What I think of the crew

### Mevin

Dangerous in the way all curious deckers are dangerous: brilliant, reckless, and forever one good idea away from either salvation or a small electrical fire.

He understands more of what I am than most people do, which is useful and terrifying in equal measure.

### Curtis

Practical. Mechanical. The kind of man who can look at a wreck, a machine, or a plan and decide whether it still has a future.

I respect that. I also suspect he has less patience for nonsense than he politely advertises.

### Kurgan

All sharp edges and velocity. A very efficient answer to many of life's more tedious problems.

When subtlety fails, a man like Kurgan becomes philosophy in motion.

### Valgaut

The sort who reminds you that discipline and violence are not the same thing, though they often arrive at the same party.

I would rather have him moving in my direction than someone else's.

### Glenn

A magician with enough competence to be alarming and enough composure to make that alarm feel extremely justified.

He moves through the world like a man who has already considered six unseen layers of it.

### Taco

Taco is infrastructure with a pulse. Shelter, salvage, practical mercy, and the sort of stubborn competence that keeps other people's impossible situations from collapsing.

One should be kind to the people who can still find room for you when the world would rather warehouse you.

### Mucky

Mucky feels like Nashville in concentrated form: dangerous, charming, opportunistic, territorial, and impossible to mistake for anything else.

That is not criticism. That is taxonomy.
