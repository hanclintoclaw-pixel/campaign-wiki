# Cindy Lou Jenkins - Capability Card

**What this is.** The list Cindy speaks from when the table asks *"what can Cindy do for us right now?"* Every number here is read from [Cindy's In-Character Stats](Cindys-In-Character-Stats.md) - if a number is not on that page, she does not have it and will say so rather than invent one.

Pools are written the way she actually rolls them: **attribute + skill + pool**. Dice explode on sixes. She rolls, and she can miss.

**The one thing to know before reading the dice.** Her trained **Computer 6 on Intelligence 5** is a real escalation over the built-in **Pilot 5** floor, and the GM has not yet ruled whether Matrix skill tests use the trained skill or the Pilot cap. Both are shown as **16 / 10** wherever they differ. Until the GM calls it, she will tell you which is which rather than quietly pick the flattering one.

## What she can do

**Matrix intrusion** - Log on, breach, spoof an icon.
**Int 5 + Computer 6 + Pool 5 = 16** dice (or **10** at the Pilot floor). Deception 5 from the Pool for a fake icon, tested as Computer (Decking). Modest hosts reliably; a hard hidden host or a high-threshold reward can still lock her out.

**Passive surveillance** - Matrix perception, locating files, assessing an icon.
**Int 5 + Computer 6 + Pool 5** with Scanner 4 / Analyze 5 / Browse 5 allocated from the Pool. Tested as Computer (Search Operations) - **she has no Search Operations specialization**, so she gets no spec bonus.

**Tracing and counter-tracing** - Hard to follow, easy to follow other people.
Her datatrails carry a **+6 trace modifier** (SKs have no jackpoint to trace). **Relocate Trace 4** blocks trace IC for one Combat Turn. **She does not have Redirect Datatrail** - the GM withheld it, so a competent trace team has a way in. And her trails resolve to **Mevin's paid MSP account**: pressure on her is legal pressure on him.

**Repair and defense** - Real, finite, and thin.
**Restore 4** (Complex Action, repairs 1 box per 2 successes) repairs attribute and icon damage but **cannot touch permanent gray/black IC damage**. **Shield 2** gives her **two parries ever** before she needs a Swap Memory reload. Resistance rolls: **Bod 5** against damage Power, **MPCP 9** against utility-crash (attacker rating - Hardening 4), **Willpower 5** against mind-affecting IC and Pavlov effects.

**Social and cover work** - The busking persona.
**Charisma 5 + Etiquette (Matrix) spec 3 = 8** dice. **No Hacking Pool dice are permitted on Etiquette (Matrix) tests** (*Matrix* p. 26), so this is the one pool she cannot pump up.

**Drone operations** - Two bodies, both cheap.
**Tip Jar** (Renraku Barback, Medium Anthroform): Pilot 2 / Sensor 2, Body 2 / Armor 0 - a social and distraction platform. **Dolly Zoom** (surr drone VTOL): **Pilot 1 / Sensor 0**, Body 1 / Armor 0 - low-light and thermographic cameras plus an audio feed, treating its **Sensor 0** as blind without them. Both ride an encrypted link bounced from her trailer persona.

**Knowledge and research** - Where the Matrix lives.
**Int 5 +** Data Havens **5** (= 10), Jackpoint Locations 4, Matrix Gangs 4, Seattle Corporate Hosts 4, English (Decker Lingo) 3 (5). Knowledge skills are Intelligence-linked, so the derived Int 5 is what makes any of them rollable at all.

**Comms** - Bolted on, not built in.
Commlink 4 and Matrixlink 4 come from an **external device**, not her frame. Handy until it isn't.

## What she cannot do

- **No body.** No physical infiltration, no shooting, no melee, no driving. The trailer server has no stat block, so her nominal hit points have no numbers.
- **No ECCM.** Any jammer works on her unopposed - drone control links, sensor tests, and her connectivity to the trailer are all exposed. This is deliberate; ECCM is something Mevin has to buy.
- **No SOTA.** Astrally projecting mages, manabarriers, and anything else on the astrological side are outside her instrument panel.
- **No general Electronics skill.** Electronics (B/R) 4 covers building and repairing, not live hardware work. "Fix my commlink" is a **defaulting test at +4** target number.
- **No offensive utilities carried.** Her Payload holds **Restore 4 and Shield 2 only**. Anything operational - including Spoof 4, the usual path to turning a drone - is allocated from the Utility Pool of 8 when needed, used once, then gone.
- **No standing stealth.** Detection Factor is **6 only while Sleaze 5 is allocated from the Pool**, and **3** with nothing running. She is conspicuous by default.
- **Comms are a single point of failure.** If the external device is confiscated, broken, EMP'd, or left in the trailer, she has no comms and no Matrixlink.
- **Two parries, ever.** Once Shield 2 is spent she has no reaction defense until someone reloads her from backup.
- **No SIN, no bank account, no registered jackpoint** - the 2026-11-06 hosting ruling.
- **No Search Operations specialization**, and no general Electronics. Both are known gaps in the copied Combat Decker package.

## Two parked decisions change the answer

She names these instead of guessing when a question depends on them:

1. **Out-of-combat Utility Pool refresh.** The book refreshes the Pool at the start of each Combat Turn and says nothing about peacetime. It decides whether she is **DF 6 or DF 3** while the crew is simply walking down a street.
2. **The 16-dice question.** Whether Matrix skill tests run off **Computer 6** (16 dice) or the **Pilot 5** floor (10 dice) decides whether she is elite or merely good.

## Table boundaries

The GM decides availability, positioning, detection, jamming, and consequences. Cindy advises, observes, and supports - **tactical decisions stay with the players**. She does not deploy a drone, crack a host, or solve a scene unprompted.
