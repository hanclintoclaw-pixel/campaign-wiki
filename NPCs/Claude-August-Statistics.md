# Claude August — GM Statistics

This is the GM-facing table build for Claude August, a **juvenile Great Dragon** who appears teenage in human form. His physical statistics use the printed typical Western Dragon values; Great Dragon capabilities are layered on without the printed Great Dragon attribute increases. **Essence 9 (fixed); Magic 9.** The raw attributes are the typical adult Western Dragon line, not the Great Dragon line. Essence is deliberately set to 9 for this campaign build. Flight rate: **70 meters per Combat Turn** (table value; the printed ×3 is the running multiplier).

Claude is physically smaller and visibly younger than a typical adult dragon. That is descriptive; it does not further reduce this usable block. The printed Great Dragon attribute increases are intentionally omitted.

### Human form

Metahuman Form gives him the physical abilities of a normal human, not his dragon Body or Strength. He can speak audibly with a normal human voice in this form. Assume ordinary human physical vulnerability; transformation in either direction takes an **Exclusive Complex Action**.

## Powers and abilities

### Great Dragon package

- **Metahuman Form:** Assume a chosen metahuman form with an Exclusive Complex Action; returning to true form also takes an Exclusive Complex Action.
- **Twist Fate:** Use the Karma Pool effects below.
- **Magical Skills:** Full magician capability; Magic 9. His working style is hermetic and preparation-oriented (campaign characterization).

### Draconic powers

- **Astral Armor.**
- **Enhanced Senses:** Wide-Band Hearing, Low-Light Vision, Thermographic Vision.
- **Hardened Armor:** Use the Western Dragon armor value, **8**, with the hardened-armor rules.
- **Dragonspeech:** Psychic communication with creatures in unaided line of sight; details below.
- **Animal Control (Reptiles), Influence, Innate Spell, Empathy, Fear.** These are Claude's selected individual powers for this build. His Influence and mental powers complement his controlled, corporate negotiating style.

He does **not** have Noxious Breath or Venom in this build.

## Magic

**Magic 9; hermetic working tradition.** His practical spell repertoire: **Armor, Improved Invisibility, Heal, Detect Enemies**. Use Sorcery for spell defense when protecting himself or a critical negotiating partner.

**Karma Pool: 50** (GM-set starting reserve within the Great Dragon scale). **Good Karma available for magical purposes: approximately 25**, as a table allocation toward anchoring, quickening, and other metamagic costs. Track expenditures normally.

### Twist Fate

For each use, make an **Opposed Test: target's Willpower against Claude's Essence 9**. Initiates add Grade to Willpower. Claude must win for the effect to occur. A tie or target victory defeats the attempt, but Claude still spends the committed Karma Pool points.

- **Cancel Karma — Free Action:** Spend 1 Karma Pool point to negate 1 point spent by a creature he can see. The target's point remains spent. This cannot negate permanent Karma Pool expenditure to buy successes.
- **Spoil Success — Simple Action:** Spend 1 Karma Pool point to force a creature in line of sight to reroll all successful dice on a test; failed dice remain untouched and the new results stand. The target may spend 1 Karma Pool point to resist automatically.
- **Gift Karma:** Transfer points from Claude's pool to characters in line of sight, even above their normal maximum. Gifted points do not refresh for recipients; once spent, they return to Claude when his Karma Pool refreshes.

## Communication

**Dragonspeech** reaches any creature in Claude's unaided line of sight. Recipients perceive it inside their minds; it works despite cybernetic hearing replacement and crosses language barriers. He can address selected listeners while excluding others nearby. An unwilling listener may attempt a **Willpower Test, TN 8** against his Charisma; successes equal minutes of blocking, and maintaining the block imposes **+2 to all other actions**.

Microphones and other non-living machines cannot detect dragonspeech. In true form, recorded speech, telecommunications, and trid appearances require a translator or another vocal intermediary. **In human form Claude speaks normally; no translator is needed.** His human voice is recordable, so choosing to use it creates evidence that dragonspeech would not.

## Running Claude

Play him as a corporate operator. His first weapons are lawyers, economics, information, and Princeps personnel; physical confrontation is a last resort. Before exposed meetings, establish his Armor spell, spell defense, screened access, and extraction route. His human vulnerability is real: active defenses must keep him alive until he can spend the action to revert.

When threatened:

1. **Degrade:** Disrupt information, visibility, coordination, or a decisive success.
2. **Withdraw:** Use concealment, cover, and prepared exits.
3. **Delegate:** Put Princeps security and corporate assets between himself and pursuit.

Reserve true-form violence for failed containment or immediate survival. His objective is continued control, not winning a public firefight.
