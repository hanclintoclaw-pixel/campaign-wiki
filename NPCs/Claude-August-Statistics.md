## Reading the notation

- **B:** Body followed by armor; that armor functions as both ballistic and impact armor.
- **Q:** Quickness followed by the running movement multiplier. Other movement multipliers are listed under Notes; the source block does not provide a flying multiplier here.
- **C:** Charisma is listed for intelligent creatures capable of social interaction.
- **I:** The first figure governs Intelligence and spell resistance; a second figure governs Perception Tests. The western-dragon entry supplies no separate Perception figure.
- **E:** **(2D6)Z** indicates Essence determined by **2D6**; **Z** marks a dual-natured being.
- **R:** Reaction, used for physical attacks.
- **INIT:** Reaction plus initiative dice. Initiative also caps Combat Pool dice usable in a single Combat Phase.
- **Attacks:** Physical damage unless otherwise stated, followed by Reach.
