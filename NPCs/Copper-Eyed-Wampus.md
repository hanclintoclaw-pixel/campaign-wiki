# The Copper-Eyed Wampus

## Overview

The Copper-Eyed Wampus is a custom dual-natured wildcat critter, inspired by Appalachian catlike cryptid stories reported in East Tennessee. This campaign version is a powerful, low-slung cat with copper-reflective eyes and a cry that carries unnaturally far. Folklore versions vary; this entry does not assign it a single origin story or claim a traditional identity for any Indigenous community.

It stalks the margins of roads, livestock clearings, and old logging cuts. It is more likely to scare trespassers away than to hunt metahumans, but it fights fiercely if cornered.

## SR3 Stat Block

The physical ratings use the *Critters* **Cat, Wild** baseline. The custom changes are dual nature (E 6Z) and the listed power package; physical attributes are otherwise unchanged.

**Powers:** Adaptive Coloration, Enhanced Senses (Low-Light Vision, Smell), Sonic Projection.

Each power is from the SR3 *Critters* power list (Adaptive Coloration p. 7, Enhanced Senses p. 9, Sonic Projection p. 13). Their combination, copper-eye description, and dual-natured wildcat are campaign homebrew. Essence-based powers use Essence 6.

## Encounter Use

- **Sign:** A copper flash low in the brush, then a scream that seems to come from the wrong ridge.
- **Disposition:** It avoids open ground and usually retreats after its cry creates an opening.
- **Limits:** Adaptive Coloration follows the book's normal limits; it does not make the Wampus invisible to scent, sonar, or other nonvisual senses.
