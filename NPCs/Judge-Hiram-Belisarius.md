# Judge Hiram Belisarius

## Overview

**Judge Hiram Belisarius** is a corrupt Nashville-area circuit judge and gambling addict targeted in the completed **Byron Cedar / Judge Belisarius** rush job.

## Known Facts

- Belisarius is an older human male and long-time city lawyer turned circuit judge.
- He is described as moderately corrupt by the standards of the corporate figures around him.
- He has accepted payoffs from **Byron L. Cedar's** side before.
- Cedar needed him to miss court on 2066-05-21, but specifically did not want him dead or permanently removed from the docket.
- Belisarius is a bachelor.
- His vices include greasy diner food, cheap booze, and problem gambling.
- He often eats at a grimy diner near the edge of Nashville's high-security downtown district.
- He regularly gambles with **8th Street**, a minor alley crew that appears to fleece him.
- He owes money around town and believed he was due for a hot streak on the night of Session 2026-09-03.
- He is a protected corrupt figure valuable enough to local underworld interests that he felt safe despite his debts and habits.
- His personal bodyguard is **Renaud Dupree**.
- His court-assigned city security is **Officer Davis Holbrook**, who generally keeps more distance outside work.
- **Kilimanjaro** successfully planted a personal comm unit on Belisarius during the diner/alley approach.
- In Session 2026-09-10, Belisarius was kidnapped from Mucky's after **Mevin** applied a tranquilizer patch and **Kilimanjaro** carried him out during the extraction.
- **Cindy Lou** tapped Belisarius's cyber-eye, which sent a local tracking / vision feed; Mevin inferred **Renaud Dupree** was the likely recipient, but this is not proof of a remote server or recording archive.
- Mevin secured Belisarius, found the planted personal comm unit, blocked the cyber-eye feed, and located the transmitter hardware switch. The crew then used Cindy's spoofed signal as cover for a debt-shakedown story rather than proving the tracking hardware was permanently neutralized.
- Belisarius was hidden through the next court day, then released alive near 8th Street Cafe with a staged beating and drunken gambling-debt collection story.

## Relationships

- Target of [Byron L. Cedar](Byron-Cedar.md)'s completed court-delay job.
- Protected after hours by [Renaud Dupree](Renaud-Dupree.md).
- Associated with [8th Street](../Factions/8th-Street.md) through gambling debts and alley dice games.
- Kidnapped from [Mucky's Place](../Locations/Muckys-Place.md) with Mucky's cooperation during the completed court-delay job.
