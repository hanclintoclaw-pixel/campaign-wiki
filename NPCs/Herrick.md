# Herrick

## Overview

Police-linked recurring contact and leverage node for the crew. Older consolidated campaign material renders the same figure as **Harac**, and these appear to be the same entity.

## Known Facts

- Herrick / Harac is a police-linked figure who manages shadowrunner opportunities and challenges.
- He functions as a recurring operational contact and job-giver.
- Mevin is specifically noted as the crew member with the key relationship to Herrick.
- Older campaign logs show Mevin planning to ask Herrick/Harac for insight about why the Federal Matrix Investigators were interested in local gun running.
- Older campaign material also suggests Herrick/Harac can help source reliable identity/documentation contacts.
- Historical Wordpress run-note material specifically shows Krom approaching Herrick at Atlas's to source reliable burner identities for operational use.
- During the Pixel Sticks aftermath, Herrick confirmed the scorpion drone broke the police cordon under active camouflage, headed south toward old I-65 go-gang territory, and represented high-end corporate/military-grade trouble he wanted kept quiet from federal attention.
- Herrick also confirmed investigators believed the Pixel Sticks had a sponsor and that unknown actors later removed the high-end utilities.
- In Session 2026-08-27, Herrick granted the crew access to surviving Pixel Sticks prisoners after Mevin framed the inquiry as a public-safety / containment issue.
- Herrick listened in but stayed mostly hands-off, and made clear that Nashville PD is deliberately avoiding the CAT / Princeps corporate fight because the city has effectively been paid to stay out unless it threatens city business.

## Relationships

- Linked to **Mevin Kitnick** as a key contact.
- Connected to the crew as a law-enforcement-adjacent liaison.
- Linked to **[Pixel Sticks](../Factions/Pixel-Sticks.md)** fallout through the escaped / recovered scorpion drone.
- Holding or overseeing surviving Pixel Sticks prisoners now being adapted into coerced gray-hat work.

## Relevant Sessions

- 2023-03-23 — older consolidated references to Herrick/Harac as an information/contact node.
- 2026-02-27 — authoritative roster update and relationship clarification.
- 2026-08-06 — provided police-side information on the escaped scorpion drone and Pixel Sticks sponsor cleanup.
- 2026-08-27 — enabled crew interviews with the surviving Pixel Sticks prisoners while avoiding deeper police entanglement in the CAT / Princeps conflict.

## Open Questions

- What is Herrick's exact formal role within Nashville PD / liaison structures?
- How exposed is the crew if Herrick's relationship to them becomes public?
- How much can Herrick keep the Pixel Sticks scorpion drone fallout from attracting federal attention?
- How far will Herrick tolerate crew operations around a corporate conflict Nashville PD is being paid not to investigate?
