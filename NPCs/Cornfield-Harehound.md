# The Cornfield Harehound

## Overview

The Cornfield Harehound is a custom dual-natured dog critter of the Red River valley. Its campaign image—a large black hound with a pale, hare-like head—borrows loosely from strange-animal accounts associated with the Bell Witch legend. This is an Awakened animal for the campaign, not the Bell Witch itself and not a claim that the legend describes a real species.

It is usually solitary and avoids a fair fight. It circles field edges, answers distant animal calls, and slips from sight when pursued through broken rows or brush.

## SR3 Stat Block

The physical ratings use the *Critters* **Dog, Large** baseline. The custom changes are dual nature (E 6Z) and the listed power package; physical attributes are otherwise unchanged.

| Rating | Value |
|---|---:|
| Type | Awakened dog; dual-natured |
| Body / Armor | 3 / 0 |
| Quickness / movement | 4 / ×5 |
| Strength | 3 |
| Charisma | — |
| Intelligence | 2 / 4 (spell resistance / Perception) |
| Willpower | 2 |
| Essence | 6Z |
| Reaction | 4 |
| Physical Initiative | 4 + 1D6 |
| Physical attack | 6M |
| Combat Pool | 5 (maximum 4 dice on attacks) |

**Powers:** Enhanced Senses (Low-Light Vision, Hearing, Smell), Fear, Mimicry (Animal Calls).

The powers themselves are standard SR3 critter powers (*Critters*, pp. 9–10, 12); this combination and the dual-natured dog are campaign homebrew. Fear and other Essence-based powers use Essence 6.

## Encounter Use

- **Sign:** A dog answers from the cornfield in a voice too far away to locate; the hound's pale head is visible for only a second between rows.
- **Disposition:** It is territorial around a den or litter, but otherwise prefers to misdirect intruders and escape.
- **Limits:** It has no immunity to bullets, no spirit Materialization, and no shapechanging power. The legend-inspired appearance is descriptive, not a disguise ability.
