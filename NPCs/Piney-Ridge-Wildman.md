# The Piney Ridge Wildman

## Overview

The Piney Ridge Wildman is a custom Tennessee-region Sasquatch variant, inspired by Appalachian accounts of a towering, red-eyed wildman. It is an animal-like, sentient critter—not a spirit—and is not intended to replace or define any Indigenous tradition. Its name and local range are campaign fiction.

Usually solitary, it keeps to steep wooded ridges and avoids settlements. Its calls are excellent imitations of local wildlife; when cornered, its stare and sudden scream can break an opponent's nerve long enough for it to escape.

## SR3 Stat Block

This variant uses the published *Critters* **Sasquatch** ratings without changing its physical or mental attributes. The additional Fear power is a campaign-homebrew addition; the book lists Mimicry (Animal Calls) for Sasquatch.

**Powers:** Mimicry (Animal Calls), Fear [homebrew addition].

Fear is a listed SR3 critter power (*Critters*, p. 10); the custom choice to add it to this Sasquatch variant is homebrew. Fear and other Essence-based powers use Essence 6. The book notes that some Sasquatches have Magical Skills; this individual is not assigned any.

## Encounter Use

- **Sign:** A familiar animal call repeats from a ridge where no animal could be standing; red points of reflected light vanish when directly watched.
- **Disposition:** It is curious but cautious, and turns violent only when trapped, attacked, or protecting its young.
- **Limits:** The creature cannot astrally project without magical skills. Its call-mimicry does not grant speech, human knowledge, or the ability to create a false scene by itself.
