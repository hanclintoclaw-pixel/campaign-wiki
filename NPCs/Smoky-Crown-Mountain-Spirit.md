# The Smoky Crown

## Overview

The Smoky Crown is a Force 4 **mountain spirit** of a high, rocky stretch in Tennessee's Great Smoky Mountains. It materializes as a broad, stone-backed silhouette with a crown of drifting cloud and spruce-shadow. The name, appearance, and persona are campaign flavor; its mechanics use the SR3 mountain-spirit template without added powers.

## Domain and Behavior

Its home domain is a defined high-elevation mountain area of the Smokies: steep rock, ridgeline, and the immediate forested slopes around it. It cannot leave that domain, and its powers apply only there. It is patient and difficult to hurry, more likely to guard a pass or redirect intruders than to pursue them downhill.

## Encounter Use

- **Sign:** Loose stones settle into a deliberate trail marker; fog parts around one ridge and closes behind it.
- **Disposition:** It may protect a pass, shelter a creature within its domain, or make a trespasser's route confusing and exhausting.
- **Mechanical note:** No special weakness, attack, or power beyond the SR3 template is assigned.
