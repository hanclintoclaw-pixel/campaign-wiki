# Ziggy Hill

## Overview

**Ziggy Hill** is a heavily chromed elf decker of the **[Pixel Sticks 21st-Floor Cell](Pixel-Sticks-21st-Floor-Cell.md)**. He was on the [scorpion drone](../Vehicles/Pixel-Sticks-Scorpion-Drone.md)'s whitelist and was the crew's second source on the anonymous sponsor who armed the gang against **[Cross Applied Technology](../Organizations/Cross-Applied-Technology.md)**.

## Known Facts

- Ziggy was one of the four named people on the scorpion drone's control list, alongside **[Ernie Pollock](Ernie-Pollock.md)**, **[Lizzo Wells](Lizzo-Wells-Handy-Sandy.md)**, and two goons.
- He was jacked in during the crew's 21st-floor breach in [Session 2026-07-23](../Sessions/2026-07-23.md) and woke up in police custody.
- He was held with the other surviving cell members, who were being adapted into coerced gray-hat work for Nashville PD.
- In [Session 2026-08-27](../Sessions/2026-08-27.md), **[Curtis](../PCs/Curtis.md)** opened the interview with a whip strike meant to scare rather than maim; the intimidation went badly.
- Ziggy confirmed the same core account Ernie gave: Matrix contact, dead drop with the utilities and transponder, drone delivered later.
- His appearance suggested heavy headware and a low-Essence, disconnected affect.
- The crew found he had little or no additional sponsor information beyond Ernie's.

## Relationships

- Decker for the [Pixel Sticks](../Factions/Pixel-Sticks.md) and their [21st-floor cell](Pixel-Sticks-21st-Floor-Cell.md).
- Questioned alongside [Ernie Pollock](Ernie-Pollock.md) and [Lizzo Wells / Handy-Sandy](Lizzo-Wells-Handy-Sandy.md).
- Held through [Herrick](Herrick.md) / [Nashville PD](../Organizations/Nashville-Police-Department.md) arrangements.
