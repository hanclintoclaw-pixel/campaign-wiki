# Byron L. Cedar

## Overview

**Byron L. Cedar, Esquire** is a corporate lawyer for **HEMP Global Agronomics** and the employer behind the completed Judge Belisarius rush job.

## Known Facts

- Cedar has previously hired **Kilimanjaro** for low-risk surveillance, personal security, and related corporate-side work.
- In Session 2026-09-03, Cedar contacted Kilimanjaro in a panic with an emergency evening job.
- Cedar needed **Judge Hiram Belisarius** kept out of court the next day, but did not want him killed, permanently removed from the docket, or replaced as the assigned judge.
- The offered job budget was **60,000¥ total**.
- In Session 2026-09-10, the crew completed the contract: Belisarius was hidden through the next court day, then released alive near 8th Street Cafe with a staged beating and drunken gambling-debt collection story.
- Cedar was surprisingly positive about the result despite the public kidnapping and shootout around the extraction.
- Cedar's panic implies the next day's court matter is important to HEMP Global Agronomics, but the exact case has not yet been revealed.

## Relationships

- Corporate lawyer for [HEMP Global Agronomics](../Organizations/HEMP-Global-Agronomics.md).
