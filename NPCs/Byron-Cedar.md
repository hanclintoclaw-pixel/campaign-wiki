# Byron L. Cedar

## Overview

**Byron L. Cedar, Esquire** is a corporate lawyer for **HEMP Global Agronomics** and the employer behind the active Judge Belisarius rush job.

## Known Facts

- Cedar has previously hired **Kilimanjaro** for low-risk surveillance, personal security, and related corporate-side work.
- In Session 2026-09-03, Cedar contacted Kilimanjaro in a panic with an emergency evening job.
- Cedar needs **Judge Hiram Belisarius** kept out of court the next day.
- Cedar does not want Belisarius killed, permanently removed from the docket, or replaced as the assigned judge.
- The offered job budget is **60,000¥ total**.
- Cedar's panic implies the next day's court matter is important to HEMP Global Agronomics, but the exact case has not yet been revealed.
