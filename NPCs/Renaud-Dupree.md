# Renaud Dupree

## Overview

**Renaud Dupree** is a heavily cybered Cajun troll who serves as **Judge Hiram Belisarius's** private after-hours bodyguard.

## Known Facts

- Dupree is an impressive-looking troll with obvious cyberware.
- Visible ware includes dermal armor, eye replacement, and reinforced or possibly fully replaced arms.
- He is protective of Belisarius and warns him that his gambling habits will get him hurt.
- He immediately noticed the alley disturbance after Kilimanjaro's flash grenade scattered 8th Street.
- He was initially skeptical of Curtis's offer to lead Belisarius to a better game, but Curtis's strong etiquette check put him at ease for the moment.
- He was the main immediate physical obstacle when the crew incapacitated Belisarius.
- In Session 2026-09-10, Mevin inferred Dupree was the likely recipient of Belisarius's local cyber-eye tracking / vision feed; this is an inference, not proof of remote server access or recording.
- During the getaway, Dupree pursued in an armored reinforced vehicle that revealed a pop-up LMG and missed Curtis's drone.
- The Finisher failed to shoot out the pursuing vehicle's tire, then chipped it with SMG fire.
- Kilimanjaro's Ares Alpha underslung HE grenade caused severe engine damage and a crash.
- Dupree explicitly survived the crash; neither Dupree nor his vehicle is confirmed captured.

## Relationships

- Private security for [Judge Hiram Belisarius](Judge-Hiram-Belisarius.md).
- Opposing obstacle in the completed [Byron Cedar / Judge Belisarius Rush Job](../Arcs/Byron-Cedar-Judge-Belisarius-Rush-Job.md).

## Relevant Sessions

- **2026-09-03** - introduced as Belisarius's bodyguard and followed the judge toward Mucky's.
- **2026-09-10** - pursued the crew after Belisarius's kidnapping, survived the vehicle crash after Kilimanjaro's grenade disabled the engine.
