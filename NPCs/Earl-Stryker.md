# Colonel Earl Stryker / Dead Soldier

## Overview

**Colonel Earl Stryker** is the named historical lead behind the **Scarred Man** that **Core 7** remembers as a Matrix bogeyman. Public/archive legwork tied Stryker to a CAS military unit involved in a major misappropriation scandal and to an abandoned North Nashville civic-construction expansion. Shadowrunner legwork later connected him to the handle **Dead Soldier**.

## Known Facts

- Stryker was a CAS military colonel tied to a unit of unspecified duties.
- That unit was involved in a major misappropriation scandal: hundreds of millions of dollars were allegedly funneled into its budget.
- Stryker claimed the money moved without his knowledge, but could not convincingly explain how it happened.
- One associated project was an expansion of civic construction in North Nashville that was abandoned when some equipment proved too dug-in to remove.
- Stryker was forced to resign from the military in disgrace.
- Draco Foundation-adjacent legwork confirmed rumors of a pre-Crash CAS military effort to use aquatic mammals' human-adjacent brain architecture, shut down after scandal or disaster.
- Core 7 remembers the associated Matrix presence as the **Scarred Man**, a hideous mechanical skeletal icon/profile that followed or hunted him in the Matrix.
- Core 7 last saw that Matrix signature roughly **15 years** ago.
- Shadowrunner old-heads identify **Dead Soldier** as Stryker's later handle.
- Dead Soldier worked as an independent solo operator doing physical security, facility security, host security, and Matrix surveillance work around the CAS.
- His reputation was reliable and competent rather than legendary: a workhorse operator who did not survive by blowing contracts, but was not remembered as a spectacular runner.
- No one in the queried shadowrunner circles had heard from Dead Soldier in the last couple of years.
