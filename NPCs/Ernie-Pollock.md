# Ernie Pollock

## Overview

**Ernie Pollock** is a brash young orc member of the **[Pixel Sticks 21st-Floor Cell](Pixel-Sticks-21st-Floor-Cell.md)**, the youth Matrix gang that ran military-grade utilities out of the [Vernon Winfrey Arkoblock](../Locations/Vernon-Winfrey-Arkoblock.md). His name was on the [scorpion drone](../Vehicles/Pixel-Sticks-Scorpion-Drone.md)'s control list, and he is the prisoner whose account gave the crew the Pixel Sticks sponsor story.

## Known Facts

- Ernie was one of the four named people on the scorpion drone's whitelist, alongside **Ziggy Hill**, **[Lizzo Wells](Lizzo-Wells-Handy-Sandy.md)**, and two goons.
- He was present on the 21st floor during the crew's breach in [Session 2026-07-23](../Sessions/2026-07-23.md) and was taken into custody with the other survivors.
- Surviving cell members were adapted into coerced gray-hat work for Nashville PD under **[Herrick](Herrick.md)**'s oversight.
- In [Session 2026-08-27](../Sessions/2026-08-27.md), **[Mevin Kitnick](../PCs/Mevin-Kitnick.md)** interviewed him with a friendly, bragging approach rather than pressure.
- Ernie confirmed the recruitment story: an anonymous Matrix contact, a dead drop holding the high-end utilities and a transponder, and the later arrival of the scorpion drone inside the gang's headquarters.
- He described the benefactor's Matrix presence as a high-resolution custom draconic humanoid icon, rude and condescending.
- He admitted the gang was handed equipment it did not know how to handle, overused the utilities, and did not expect further resupply.

## Relationships

- Member of the [Pixel Sticks](../Factions/Pixel-Sticks.md) and their [21st-floor cell](Pixel-Sticks-21st-Floor-Cell.md).
- Interviewed alongside [Ziggy Hill](Ziggy-Hill.md) and [Lizzo Wells / Handy-Sandy](Lizzo-Wells-Handy-Sandy.md).
- Held through [Herrick](Herrick.md) / [Nashville PD](../Organizations/Nashville-Police-Department.md) arrangements.
