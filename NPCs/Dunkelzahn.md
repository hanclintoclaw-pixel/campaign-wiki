# Dunkelzahn

*What this is: a reference page for a deceased public figure, not an active scene NPC. Nothing on this page has been witnessed at the table. The entry exists because the GM commissioned it on 2026-11-15 and because the campaign already leans on the institution his death created.*

## Canon Status (GM commission, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-15)

Dunkelzahn is adopted as the **same printed entity** as the SR3/FASA great dragon Dunkelzahn, in the manner of the 2026-11-10 ruling applied to the [Draco Foundation](../Factions/Draco-Foundation.md) and other printed bodies. The commission licenses the page; it does not by itself settle which of the printed secrets are true at this table. Everything below is therefore drawn from printed sources and labelled by evidence tier, and the disputed items are listed as disputed rather than resolved.

Dunkelzahn is dead, his will was read, and the **Draco Foundation** exists to execute it. Those three facts are already load-bearing in this campaign through [Wyrmwatch Circle](../Factions/Draco-Foundation/Wyrmwatch-Circle.md), whose public cover is "post-Dunkelzahn testamentary scholarship," and through the [Cairo Testament Annex](../Locations/Cairo-Testament-Annex.md).

## Overview

A great dragon who ran for and won the presidency of the UCAS on an openly pro-Awakened, pro-metahumanity platform, and was assassinated minutes after his inauguration on **9 August 2057**. He was killed in the presidential limousine leaving the Watergate Hotel in Washington, FDC, and the astral rift that opened above the death site 109 seconds later is still there. His last will and testament distributed a great dragon's hoard in a long series of bequests to individuals, mid-size corporations, and causes, with the Draco Foundation named as sole executor. Nine years on, he is a civic saint, a political brand, and an open wound in the UCAS.

## Known Facts

**Can on the public record** (printed SR3 / FASA, corroborated across two books):

- Great dragon; campaigned for the UCAS presidency in 2057 and won a narrow victory on 7 August 2057, declared at 11:23:34 PM EST.
- Sworn in alongside Vice-President **Kyle Haeffner** on 9 August 2057 in Washington, FDC. Assassinated at 10:23:08 PM EST that night, in the presidential limo leaving the **Watergate Hotel**.
- An **astral rift opened above the death site** at 10:24:57 PM EST. It has never closed. It sits next to the Watergate, in the FDC, and the **Army Corps of Thaumaturgists** keeps watches on it. Locally it is called the **Dunkelzahn Rift**; official correspondence calls it the **Watergate Rift**.
- More than **500 claims of responsibility** reached the FBI within minutes. The first, from a group called Red Dawn, was written off as a hoax because the caller claimed to have "fired the fatal bullet." The killers were never established.
- The **Draco Foundation** was named sole executor of the will and took on that role in late 2057, with **Nadja Daviar** as its chair.
- His first documented public appearance was at **Cherry Creek Lake State Park, Denver** — the Mile High City still treats itself as the city where he first showed himself to the world.
- He was widely understood to like metahumans. The phrase used in the printed record is a dragon "with a soft spot for metahumanity."

**Disputed on the public record** — and the campaign should keep it that way:

- **How he died.** The SR3 record says an **explosion** killed him and that the rift opened in that explosion. The FASA-era assassination timeline describes a shooting in the limousine, and the hoax caller's "fatal bullet" line is treated as a tell. Public sources do not agree. No ruling has been made for this table.
- **Who killed him.** Nothing is established. The Scott Commission, formed on 10 August 2057 under Chief Justice Richard Scott, heard the case publicly and produced no settled answer.
- **Whether the will is complete.** Public sources acknowledge unlisted and conditional bequests, including gifts that go "to the first party to" accomplish something.

## Campaign Relevance Now

The campaign is in **2066**, nine years past the assassination. What that means in practice:

- The **Children of the Dragon** religion, founded directly in response to his 2057 assassination, holds its major anniversary vigils at the DeeCee rift and has a main temple on Prince Edward Island. Its membership grew after his death rather than fading.
- Bequests still surface. One well-documented case: he willed the lands and mineral rights of a volcanic island to **Federated Boeing**, specifying an eruption date of 3 October 2060. The island appeared on schedule, 301 kilometres off Petrolia. A will that predicted a geological event to the day is the reason nobody treats the remaining bequests as settled.
- The **Ghostwalker** conspiracy — that the Denver dragon is Dunkelzahn returned — circulates because Ghostwalker's astral form was first seen emerging from the Dunkelzahn Rift itself on Christmas Eve. It is conspiracy, not fact, and the printed record is explicit that Ghostwalker is a different dragon.
- Locally, his death matters mostly through institutions. The Foundation's nearness to the CAS makes the **Cairo Testament Annex** the natural place for any testamentary thread to touch Nashville without a UCAS scene.
