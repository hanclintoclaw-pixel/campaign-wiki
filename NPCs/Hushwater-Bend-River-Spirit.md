# The Hushwater Bend

## Overview

The Hushwater Bend is a Force 4 **river spirit** tied to a stretch of the Tennessee River at Chattanooga. When it materializes, it resembles a long-limbed figure made of dark water, foam, and reflected bridge lights. This identity and appearance are campaign flavor; its mechanics use the SR3 river-spirit template without added powers.

## Domain and Behavior

Its home domain is a chosen bend and adjoining banks of the Tennessee River near Chattanooga. Like other nature spirits, it cannot leave its home domain, and its powers apply only within it. It watches the current, submerged debris, and traffic on the water. It is usually indifferent to passersby, but it reacts strongly to violence or pollution in its stretch.

## Encounter Use

- **Sign:** The current briefly runs against itself; a voice seems to travel over the water without a visible speaker.
- **Disposition:** It may reveal or conceal something carried by the river, but it has its own priorities and is not automatically friendly to people on shore.
- **Mechanical note:** No special weakness, attack, or power beyond the SR3 template is assigned.
