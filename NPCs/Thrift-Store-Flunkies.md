# Thrift-store Flunkies

## Overview

Two unnamed associates who maintain the storefront at the crew's **[Thrift Store Flophouse](../Locations/Thrift-Store-Flophouse.md)**. In [Session 2026-09-24](../Sessions/2026-09-24.md), the GM explicitly recorded them as **Level 1 contacts of [Mevin Kitnick](../PCs/Mevin-Kitnick.md)**, not automatic personal contacts of Curtis.

## Known History

- The session's retrospective account identified them as members of a hapless rival runner group involved in an old bank job. The crew took over the thrift-store/pawn-shop operation afterward; Glenn previously lived upstairs while they ran the shop below.
- The exact bank, played date, and individual names were not supplied. This is recovered table history, not a newly dated session.
- They agreed to act as out-of-town delivery men for the White Creek operation after Mevin's successful contact call and a promise of Double Downs.
- Their handoff succeeded: Ring of Fire members collected the disguised package and carried it into the civic center. The flunkies were not sent inside with the assault team.
- No courier casualty, cash payment, or confirmed fulfillment of the food promise was recorded.

## Open Questions

- What are their names, and how much did they understand about the package?
- Has the promised food been provided?
- Can their involvement be traced back to the safehouse or Mevin?
