## RESULT 1 - SPONSORED

**THE DRACO FOUNDATION - OFFICE OF PUBLIC INFORMATION**
*Entry 7-DKZ. Submitted and maintained by the Foundation. Last refreshed 2066-08-09.*

### Dunkelzahn
**1947 (unconfirmed) - 9 August 2057**
*President of the United Canadian and American States. Great Dragon. Philanthropist.*

The Foundation marks today's ninth anniversary of the President's death by inviting the public to remember what he built rather than how he was taken from it.

**A dragon who chose our side.** Dunkelzahn first presented himself publicly to the world at Cherry Creek Lake State Park in Denver, and from that first appearance onward he made no secret of where his interests lay. He backed metahuman self-determination, he backed the Awakened, and he backed the mid-size corporation against the old entrenched giants. When he stood for the presidency of the UCAS in 2057 he did so as a dragon, openly, and asked to be judged on the platform.

**The election and the assassination.** The President was declared winner on 7 August 2057 and sworn into office with Vice-President Kyle Haeffner on 9 August, in Washington, FDC. At 10:23 PM that evening, as the motorcade left the Watergate Hotel following inauguration celebrations, President Dunkelzahn was killed. He had held office for a matter of hours. He was, by every account that matters, the most popular head of state the UCAS has ever had.

**The will.** The President's final document distributed the bulk of a great dragon's accumulated wealth across hundreds of named individuals, causes, mid-size companies, and institutions. It was written to shape the future rather than to honour the past, and it very largely has. The Draco Foundation was named sole executor and has carried out that charge for nine years without interruption, under the chairmanship of Nadja Daviar, who served the President as chief personal aide through the campaign.

**The Foundation's work.** Grant-making, magical research sponsorship, cultural custody, and disaster response. The Dunkelzahn Institute of Magical Research funds the work he said he wanted funded. Where the will named a recipient, we deliver. Where the will set a condition, we hold to the letter of it. That is the whole of our public promise and we have kept it.

**Bequest claims.** Any party presenting a legitimate interest in an unlisted, conditional, or contested bequest should file through the nearest Foundation office. Claims are reviewed on documentation, not on sentiment. Appointments are required.

> **FOUNDATION NOTE ON THE RIFT:** The Foundation does not conduct programming at the FDC death site and advises the public that the area is monitored by the Army Corps of Thaumaturgists as an active astral hazard. The Foundation has never endorsed pilgrimage to the site, and the vigils held there each August are held by private religious groups without Foundation participation, funding, or endorsement.

> *The Foundation welcomes correction of the factual record. Send documentation, not opinion.*

## GM notes

This is a **player-facing prop**. It is what a Matrix search returns, and it is a curated set of narrators rather than the truth. Six things to know before you run with it.

**The Foundation entry is PR, and it is doing four specific jobs.** It foregrounds what he built and asks the reader not to dwell on how he was taken, which is exactly what a memorial office is for. It **denies any connection to the rift vigils** - and note that this denial is *true*, the vigils are the Children of the Dragon's own, so the Foundation is being honest while managing a liability. It says "hundreds of named individuals" and "unlisted, conditional, or contested bequests" without ever committing to a count, because the count is the part that matters and the Foundation does not want to fix it in public. And the claims paragraph is not decoration: the Foundation is openly inviting bequest claims. That paragraph is an adventure hook and it is meant to be read by players.

**Two genuinely useful facts are sitting in plain sight and can be spent.** The Foundation will accept bequest claims through a public records office **two hours north of Nashville in Cairo, Illinois**, appointment required, one visit will not settle it. And the will has a **track record of conditional gifts coming true on schedule** - the Federated Boeing island erupted on the exact date the will specified. A crew that finds a scrap of the will's public schedule is holding something with a real precedent behind it.

**What is wrong, or at least unestablished, in the prop:**

- **The explosion-versus-shooting conflict is real in the sources and stays unresolved here on purpose.** Wire copy says explosion, the Foundation's own language implies a shooting, and the hoax caller's "fatal bullet" line is treated as a tell by nobody official. If you want one answer for the table, that is a ruling to make, not a fact to look up. The prop will support either.
- **The Foundation's "1947 (unconfirmed)" birth line is an invention of the sponsored copy.** No printed source fixes a birth year. It is the kind of plausible precision a memorial office manufactures and the kind a careful player will notice is unsourced. Treat it as false-unless-ruled.
- **"The most popular head of state the UCAS has ever had" is Foundation copy, not a finding.** So is the soft, frictionless picture of the campaign. The same record shows a race that turned ugly enough to produce riots, self-immolations, and ethnic-targeted gang violence in the weeks after.
- **The third result is not evidence of anything.** The Ghostwalker-is-Dunkelzahn theory is documented as a conspiracy, the printed record explicitly says Ghostwalker is a different dragon, and the submitter has had twelve submissions removed for fabricating documents. It is included because a real search would surface it and because it tells you who is still inside the old campaign machinery and unhappy. The one line worth keeping in your pocket is the insider claim that people were "already in place hours before" — that is a lead about a *person*, not about a dragon.
- **The 200+ bequest schedule offered free by the memorabilia shop is the single most valuable object in this handout,** and it is being sold as a novelty item. Whether the "unexpurgated public schedule" is accurate, incomplete, or padded is yours to decide; the shop is not checking, it is shipping.

**Deliberate omissions, so nobody thinks the prop contradicts canon.** The prop does **not** mention the Wyrmwatch Circle. Under its Secrecy stricture, Foundation contact can be acknowledged publicly; Wyrmwatch membership, its rites, its archives and its secured sites cannot, so a Matrix result would not name it even though the Annex behind the counter is Wyrmwatch's intake site. The prop also does not mention any Nashville bequest holder, any local Foundation office, or any live Dunkelzahn material, because the campaign has established none.

**If the crew acts on this, the realistic path is boring before it is interesting.** They get the Cairo address, they get an appointment, they get a forms check, and someone at the counter decides their documentation is insufficient. The interesting version is what happens when they come back with better paperwork - and who else has been filing claims.
