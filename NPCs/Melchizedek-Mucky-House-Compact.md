# The House Compact of Melchizedek and Mucky

![The House Compact of Melchizedek and Mucky](../assets/images/Melchizedek-Mucky-House-Compact-2026-09-04.png)

_A working agreement, understanding, covenant, compact, memorandum of proper respect, and/or entirely reasonable hospitality arrangement between Memphis Menthol Mephistopheles Muckmeyer, hereinafter **the Proprietor**, and Melchizedek, hereinafter **the Resident Divinity**, **the Little Goat Man**, **the House**, or **that hovering son of a bitch in Room Whatever**._

## Preamble

Whereas the Proprietor owns, influences, intimidates, protects, profits from, or otherwise has every practical right to speak for the gambling hall, hotel, rooms, kitchens, service passages, roof access, feed lines, back office, cash cage, and associated profitable confusion hereinafter called **the Establishment**;

Whereas the Resident Divinity has taken a special liking to the Establishment, including but not limited to its walls, rooms, threshold habits, bad carpeting, human desperation, snacks, televisions, cable boxes, Matrix feeds, room-service wrappers, and excellent position for watching people behave poorly;

Whereas the Proprietor is not in the charity business, the temple business, the babysitting business, or the business of letting supernatural freeloaders squat in profitable square footage without a return;

And whereas the Resident Divinity has demonstrated the ability to make the Establishment into his own little kingdom, ask the building questions, open roads to places the Proprietor would prefer not to name in front of paying customers, throw around household luck and misfortune, find hidden things, hide obvious things, and produce wealth in a manner that is mysterious, shiny, and best accepted without theological argument;

The parties agree as follows.

## 1. Consideration Furnished by the Proprietor

The Proprietor shall provide the Resident Divinity with regular and sufficient access to fast food, diner food, fried food, boxed food, greasy food, sugar water, coffee-adjacent stimulants, and other metahuman dietary mistakes as the Resident Divinity may reasonably demand and unreasonably enjoy.

The Proprietor shall provide functional cable, trid, local feeds, remote feeds, illicit feeds when convenient, Matrix access sufficient for observation and entertainment, and such splitters, repeaters, remotes, adapters, hacked hotel menus, and shouted technical assistance as may be required to keep the Resident Divinity from treating signal interruption as a theological insult.

The Proprietor shall not be responsible for taste, accuracy, signal morality, product placement, or any resulting opinions the Resident Divinity forms about metahuman culture, except where such opinions can be monetized.

## 2. Consideration Furnished by the Resident Divinity

The Resident Divinity shall recognize the Establishment as his preferred house and shall keep his special attention upon it. This attention shall include the gaming floor, cash movements, guest rooms, private tables, service corridors, parking approaches, count rooms, back-office doors, and any other place where a fool might believe the Proprietor is not looking.

The Resident Divinity shall, when asked by the Proprietor or when offended by stupidity, consult the house itself regarding matters touching the Establishment. Such consultation may include staring through wallpaper, listening to the mood of a room, tasting the air around a crooked hand of cards, asking the carpet what it remembers, or otherwise drawing conclusions from the habits of walls, thresholds, and the desperate mammals between them.

The Resident Divinity shall use these house-born insights to identify cheaters, thieves, false winners, advantage players too proud of themselves, dealers shaving the count, guests hiding weapons where no respectable guest hides weapons, spirits trespassing without paying rent, magicians doing subtle nonsense, and any other party attempting to separate the Proprietor from money in a manner not approved by the Proprietor.

Whenever practical, the Resident Divinity shall provide these findings in a useful form, such as pointing, whispering through an ice machine, making a chandelier hum over the guilty table, causing a television to briefly show the relevant face, or appearing personally to say something ominous and actionable.

## 3. Expected House Conduct

The Resident Divinity shall maintain the Establishment as a place where the Proprietor's paying guests feel watched only in the ordinary predatory commercial sense, unless the Proprietor requests otherwise.

The Resident Divinity may conceal things the Proprietor wants concealed, including rooms, doors, guests, cash, evidence, exits, meetings, wounds, nervous associates, and the Proprietor himself, provided such concealment does not prevent collection of debts.

The Resident Divinity may confuse, misdirect, inconvenience, trip, startle, delay, sour, distract, or spiritually wrinkle persons acting against the Proprietor's interests. The preferred order of operations is embarrassment, financial correction, physical removal, then ominous miracle. The Proprietor reserves the right to skip steps.

The Resident Divinity may guard the Establishment and its chosen occupants from fire, panic, unlucky accidents, angry outsiders, and preventable stupidity, except that preventable stupidity caused by the Proprietor's own plans shall be treated as an ordinary cost of doing business.

The Resident Divinity may search the Establishment and its margins for missing persons, hidden goods, planted devices, concealed weapons, unpaid debts, lost remotes, secret doors, cursed items, hidden recordings, and customers who said they were just stepping outside but in fact were not.

The Resident Divinity may use household magic, forceful conversation, sudden silence, or other suitable pressure to keep order, provided the result does not permanently reduce the earning capacity of a useful room without prior approval.

## 4. Wealth, Coinage, and Proper Gratitude

At regular intervals acceptable to the Resident Divinity and profitable to the Proprietor, the Resident Divinity shall furnish flat wealth payments to the Proprietor as rent, tithe, house blessing, tribute, vig, nuisance fee, or whatever term causes the least whining from lawyers.

Such payments shall be rendered in gold coins whenever possible. Gold coins are preferred because they are pretty, stackable, hard to charge back, easy to understand, and make the correct sound when poured onto a desk.

If gold coins are not practical, the Resident Divinity may provide equivalent wealth in other forms, provided the Proprietor is not required to explain to tax men, fences, bankers, angry magicians, or his own people why the monthly house share arrived as antique teeth, bearer chips, wet gems, or a duffel bag full of foreign commemorative currency.

The Resident Divinity shall not substitute visions, prophecies, riddles, blessings, vibes, lucky feelings, unpaid future favors, or educational commentary for wealth unless the Proprietor expressly agrees in writing while sober or while winning.

## 5. Doors Best Left Polite

The Resident Divinity may maintain or access strange roads, back ways, library doors, viewing domes, and other passages not found on the Establishment's fire map.

The Resident Divinity shall not open such roads into public guest areas during peak business unless doing so solves a problem more expensive than screaming.

The Proprietor may request special passage, consultation, witness, hiding, or disposal through such roads. The Resident Divinity may object on grounds of metaphysical taste, but taste shall not overrule a direct threat to the Establishment, a major opportunity for profit, or a situation where the Proprietor says, with feeling, that this is important.

No guest, employee, enemy, creditor, cop, spirit, magician, tourist, family member, bounty hunter, journalist, or door-to-door preacher shall be allowed to wander into the Resident Divinity's deeper holdings by accident. If they do, they are presumed to have trespassed creatively.

## 6. Exclusivity and Priority

The Resident Divinity acknowledges that the Establishment is a Mucky house first, last, sideways, and through the drywall. Any spiritual claim, hearth claim, domestic claim, house claim, or goat-man claim exists inside the Proprietor's broader practical claim, which is backed by ownership, reputation, violence, paperwork if available, and long-standing community expectation that Mucky is not to be tested for free.

The Resident Divinity shall not contract with rivals, road trash, crooked managers, ambitious dealers, outside gangs, visiting magicians, or charming liars against the Proprietor's interests.

The Resident Divinity may enjoy metahuman drama, but he shall not create unprofitable drama merely to observe it unless the Proprietor has been given first chance to sell tickets.

## 7. Breach, Remedies, and Tone of Voice

Should the Proprietor fail to provide food, cable, feeds, or reasonable entertainment, the Resident Divinity may complain, sulk, rearrange remotes, haunt the vending machines, or make one room unpleasant. He shall not escalate to blood, fire, structural damage, mass panic, or theological spectacle until the Proprietor has been notified and given a fair opportunity to blame an employee.

Should the Resident Divinity fail to catch cheaters, produce wealth, guard the Establishment, respect the Proprietor's interests, or refrain from making business weird in an unprofitable direction, the Proprietor may restrict feed access, alter menu privileges, impose remote-control custody, relocate favorite chairs, bring in a priest, bring in a magician, bring in someone worse, or speak sternly in a manner calibrated to produce compliance.

Both parties agree that most disputes shall be resolved privately, loudly, and with minimal witnesses.

## 8. Mutual Understanding

The Proprietor understands that the Resident Divinity is not an employee, pet, mascot, slot-machine charm, security camera, snack disposal unit, or novelty act, despite the many ways he resembles several of those things during normal operations.

The Resident Divinity understands that the Proprietor is not a worshipper, supplicant, temple steward, ceremonial butler, appointed food priest, or soft-hearted patron of supernatural television addiction, despite the many ways he may be required to behave like one to keep the house profitable.

The arrangement survives because both parties get what they want: the Resident Divinity gets a warm little kingdom full of food, signal, noise, vice, and endlessly instructive metahuman foolishness; the Proprietor gets a house that watches back, a cheat-catching oracle in the wallpaper, a supernatural security problem pointed outward, and regular gold on the desk.

Signed, marked, grumbled at, hovered over, revised in the Proprietor's favor, and understood well enough to be enforceable by appetite, profit, and consequences.
