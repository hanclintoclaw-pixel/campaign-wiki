# Sleazy Tabloid Reporter

This is a published Shadowrun 3rd Edition contact archetype from the book's “It's Who You Are: Contact” material, **not a campaign NPC**. Adopting a specific reporter at the table needs a GM ruling; this page does not establish that the group knows one or that one has appeared in play. (p. 41)

## Why it's here

The campaign's other press texture so far comes from *Shadowbeat*, a 2nd Edition book whose mechanics are quarantined from rules rulings here. This published SR3 contact is a rules-legal archetype to use instead, without importing another edition's mechanics.

## Voice and interaction

> “The story is all important. Whatever it takes to get it, I'll do it. Don't get all smug and sanctimonious with me—people want to read this stuff. If they didn't, they wouldn't buy the rag in the first place.” (p. 41)

He is always looking for lurid, steamy, controversial or provocative material. He would rather get the real story, but will stretch the truth on slow news days. He wants a scoop; runners can win his favor by taking him along to witness events and take pictures, though most runners will not take the relationship that far. (p. 41)
