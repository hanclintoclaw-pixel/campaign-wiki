# Tamsin "Edge" Vann

**GM-commissioned NPC, canon by GM approval 2026-11-18** (message 1572712597453803618, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread). Second member of the Nashville Seraphim cell; see [Harlan Raines](Harlan-Raines.md). Has not met the players: player-facing reveals are GM-licensed.

## Who she is

Fast cyber samurai, wholly mundane, late thirties, from Kingsport, Tennessee. The handle predates her conversion; Harlan later gave it a second meaning from the sword-of-the-Spirit passage, which she tolerates - "still not engraving it on anything."

Tailored work jackets, immaculate boots, lipstick checked in any reflective surface. Up close the polish can't hide the reconstruction - articulated hands, artificial eyes, seams disappearing under her collar. She moves abruptly enough that people sometimes stop talking. The katana has a plain repaired scabbard and a grip she rewraps herself; the smart pistol rides under the jacket. Neither gets displayed to settle an argument.

Faith changed whom she works for, not what powers her body. At services she takes an aisle seat; some congregants hesitate before touching her hand. She notices. She also notices who eventually stops hesitating.

## Cover

Touring-equipment and backstage-access coordinator for [Cross Applied Technology's](../Organizations/Cross-Applied-Technology.md) Nashville Entertainment Division. Freight manifests, loading arrangements, missing credentials, explaining why a stage prop can't use the passenger elevators. She genuinely likes the work; crews trust her because their gear arrives and their breaks happen.

## Background

Her mother Leesa worked southeastern concert security and trained at a strip-mall dojo between tours; the katana was Leesa's saved-for-years extravagance. A loading-dock shooting killed her, and the hunt for the shooter was Tamsin's introduction to paid violence.

Corporate protection work paid for successive surgeries until employers stopped asking and started booking appointments - core p. 45 notes that employers "are not against removing 'ware installed in former employees," which is exactly where one hook lives (see Hooks). The [Open Table](../Organizations/Cross-Applied-Technology/Southeastern-Novel-Totems.md) found her at a church-basement supper after one of those employers abandoned her with unpaid clinic bills: a woman serving beans asked her to carry a sticking folding table, Tamsin snapped she wasn't hired help, and the woman said "Neither am I. Grab that end." She came back the following week.

[Harlan Raines](Harlan-Raines.md) recruited her after a frightened witness showed up at supper with a tracker stitched into his coat - she spotted the altered lining, borrowed sewing scissors, and the tracker left on an outbound catering van.

## Cell function

Edge makes Harlan's meetings survivable: routes, service entrances, credible reasons to enter restricted spaces, separating frightened sources from pursuers. She handles the coercion Harlan won't attempt. Their standing argument is how long an invitation stays open when someone is approaching with a gun.

## Hooks

- A former employer produces a service agreement claiming ownership of several implanted components. She suspects it is genuine.
- A touring musician recognizes Leesa's sword and offers information about the loading-dock shooting in exchange for passage out of Nashville.
- An Open Table regular asks her to frighten an abusive relative - and wants someone else present when she answers.

## Portrayal

Brisk and conversational; the mountain accent thickens only when she is annoyed. Jokes about backstage disasters, never about anyone's surgical scars. Moves her chair before sitting so nobody has to squeeze behind her. Delights in clothes compliments, dislikes "impressive hardware" ones. When Harlan sings before difficult business, she checks the exits - then quietly joins the last line.

## Rules notes and provenance

- **Combat Pool 7 and Reaction 8 both check out.** Pool formula printed on the Dice Pool Table (core p. 44); Reaction = (Quickness + Intelligence) / 2, round down (core pp. 55-56), then wired reflexes' printed +2 Reaction per level (core pp. 301-302). Wired reflexes raise Reaction and Initiative, not Quickness, so they do not move the Combat Pool.
- **Initiative 8+3D6 is the fast-guy math.** Average 18.5, reliably acting before an uncybered crew twice a turn. Deliberate at approval; if it outspends the table, Wired Reflexes 1 (alpha E1.6, 110,000 yen; core p. 302) is the one-line nerf - Initiative 6+2D6, Reaction 6.
- **If the GM wants her meaner:** Muscle Replacement 1, alpha E0.8 / 40,000 yen (core p. 302; rules text core p. 301: add the rating to Strength and Quickness, "this change does not affect Reaction," maximum 4) takes Essence to 1.68, Quickness and Strength to 6, katana damage to (6+3)M.
- **Spurs are core bodyware, not cyberlimb-only** (correcting my pitch): Retractable Spur E0.3 / 11,500 yen base, alpha E0.24 / 23,000 yen, Damage (STR)M (core p. 302). Bone-mounted, no cyberlimb required. Left out of the base build to keep Essence visible at roughly 2.5; add it if the table wants a hidden answer to grapples.
- **Essence 2.48 is the design** - visible seams, the aisle seat, the hesitation. The core's own Street Samurai sensibility runs far lower (core p. 45), so 2.48 is still humane by print standards. Magic 0: she is mundane (core p. 56); the Open Table changed her allegiances, not her anatomy.
- **No Edge.** SR3 runs on Karma Pool; the name "Edge" is a handle, not a mechanic (core pp. 242-248).
