# Memphis Menthol Mephistopheles Muckmeyer

![Mucky](../assets/images/Mucky.jpg)

## Overview

Usually called **Mucky**. A major Nashville criminal figure, wolf shapeshifter, and physical adept who is allied to the crew through existing contact networks.

## Known Facts

- Mucky is an NPC.
- He is a wolf shapeshifter and physical adept.
- He is described as a major criminal figure in Nashville.
- He is considered an active contact.
- His night club is recorded as [Mucky's Place](../Locations/Muckys-Place.md), where the crew completed the Byron Cedar / Judge Belisarius rush job extraction.
- In Session 2026-09-10, Mucky knowingly cooperated with the Belisarius kidnapping for **10% of the 60,000¥ take**.
- Mucky offered booze and distraction through his troll bouncer **Big Lou**; the transcript also has a casual variant of **Big Blue**.
- Kilimanjaro covered Mucky's cut from Kilimanjaro's own share, leaving Curtis and Mevin paid at **20,000¥ each**. Kilimanjaro's remaining balance after expenses is not explicit.

## Relationships

- Allied to the crew through **Glenn**.
- Part of the broader Nashville criminal/contact ecology around the party.
- Linked to [Melchizedek](Melchizedek.md) through the night club that serves as Melchizedek's Personal Domain.
- Cooperated with [Kilimanjaro](../PCs/Kilimanjaro.md), [Curtis](../PCs/Curtis.md), and [Mevin](../PCs/Mevin-Kitnick.md) during the Belisarius extraction.

## Relevant Sessions

- 2026-02-27 — authoritative roster/contact update.
- 2026-09-03 — the crew chose Mucky's night club as the lure destination for Judge Belisarius and Renaud Dupree.
- 2026-09-10 — knowingly hosted and assisted the Belisarius extraction for a 10% cut.
