# The Broadway Chord

## Overview

The Broadway Chord is a Force 4 **city spirit** whose home is the Lower Broadway entertainment district in Nashville. It takes shape as a tall figure of neon reflections, rain-dark pavement, and brass-band echoes. The appearance and name are campaign flavor; its mechanics use the SR3 city-spirit template without added powers.

## Domain and Behavior

Its domain is the crowded, neon-lit Lower Broadway corridor: honky-tonks, alleys, sidewalks, and the immediate street-front spaces that make up its home. It cannot leave its domain, and its nature-spirit powers work only there. It is curious about crowds and performances, protective of the district's familiar rhythms, and quick to confuse or frighten people who threaten its home.

## Encounter Use

- **Sign:** Neon signs flicker in time with a song no band is playing; alley reflections move a beat late.
- **Disposition:** It may misdirect a pursuer or conceal someone who treats the district respectfully, but it is not a dependable ally.
- **Mechanical note:** No special weakness, attack, or power beyond the SR3 template is assigned.
