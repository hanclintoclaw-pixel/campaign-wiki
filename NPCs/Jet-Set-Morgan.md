# Jet Set Morgan

## Overview

Active surveillance target in the April 9 session.

He is a vendor of illicit and obscure sim-sense recordings and BTLs, working as a
distributor for an employer the crew has not identified. (GM ruling, New Cindy GM
Interaction Thread, 2026-11-16)

## Known Facts

- The crew chose to tail Jet Set Morgan rather than grab him immediately.
- Kurgan followed him on foot while Curtis supported with drone surveillance.
- Another unknown drone also appeared to be following him.
- He moved through public streets and into alleyways, increasing the risk profile of the tail.
- After capture, he was revealed to be a gig-economy stooge working for the same employer behind the street-promoter / Darla-distribution side of the operation.
- His van contained a second satellite receiver, and his cyberterminal was root-kitted in a way that let a controller exert deep influence over his life.
- The crew ultimately released him and treated him as a potential **Level 1 contact for Kurgan** rather than a pure disposable target.
- His merchandise is experience, not goods: illicit and obscure sim-sense recordings and BTLs, the hard-to-get end of the trade rather than catalog releases. (GM ruling, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-16)
- He knew Cindy Lou Jenkins during her Swiftwing Records years, before the save, so he is a contemporary witness to the manufactured persona rather than a fan who only met the brand later. (GM ruling, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-16)

## Relationships

- **Attributes and derived dice pools are NOT yet set.** The GM must pick a body/quickness cluster before Combat pools are used. Black Book attribute rows are unreliable in the local scan; do not reconstruct Morgan's attributes from them.
