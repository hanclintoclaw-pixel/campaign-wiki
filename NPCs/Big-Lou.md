# Big Lou / Big Blue

## Overview

The troll bouncer / security presence at **[Mucky's Place](../Locations/Muckys-Place.md)**. The session transcript renders his name as both **Big Lou** and **Big Blue**; the spelling is not settled, and these are treated as one scene participant rather than separate NPCs.

## Known Facts

- Works for **[Mucky](Mucky.md)**, who presents him as the club's visible security muscle and makes clear that trouble with the bouncer means trouble with Mucky first.
- In [Session 2026-09-10](../Sessions/2026-09-10.md), Mucky sent him to distract **[Renaud Dupree](Renaud-Dupree.md)** while the crew extracted **[Judge Belisarius](Judge-Hiram-Belisarius.md)**.
- He successfully drew Dupree to the other side of a doorway, but not as far away as the crew hoped; the runners used the rear exit instead.
- Was laughing and following the furious Dupree as the bodyguard rushed back to his vehicle.
- No full stat block, independent agenda, or subsequent injury was established.
