# Paul Hardcastle

## Overview

Humanis-linked smuggling figure operating north of Nashville. He leads or fronts the **[Ring of Fire](../Factions/Ring-of-Fire.md)**, whose individual members are called Torchmen.

## Known Facts

- Active around **[White Creek Civic Center](../Locations/White-Creek-Civic-Center.md)**, the old police station / town hall that the Ring of Fire uses as a fortified base.
- Known to Nashville shadow / smuggling circles as a Humanis figure of note on the north side of the city.
- The gang operates as a smuggling waypoint for traffic coming overland from the UCAS / Kentucky side while avoiding federal attention.
- During [Session 2026-09-17](../Sessions/2026-09-17.md), Kilimanjaro saw Hardcastle arrive in a souped-up hot rod and carry supplies into the building.
- Described in the transcript as cybered/chromed and wearing a rough leather vest.

## Open Questions

- How directly does Hardcastle answer to Humanis leadership versus operating as an affiliated contractor?
- Can the crew spoof his voice or orders well enough to move Torchmen away from the site?
- Does he know the Sandusky evidence exists in the basement?
