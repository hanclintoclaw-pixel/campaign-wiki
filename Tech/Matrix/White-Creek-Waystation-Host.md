# White Creek Waystation Host

## Overview

The thin logistics host Mevin actually accessed during the **[White Creek evidence retrieval](../../Arcs/Humanis-Evidence-Retrieval.md)**. It serves the **[Ring of Fire](../../Factions/Ring-of-Fire.md)** at **[White Creek Civic Center](../../Locations/White-Creek-Civic-Center.md)**.

This page records **table-discovered information only**. The earlier plan pointed toward the [Humanis Nashville Hidden Host](Humanis-Nashville-Hidden-Host.md), but the GM resolved this intrusion with live rolls and described a low-tech waystation. The generic profile's deeper files, ratings, and IC are not established discoveries or confirmed properties of this site.

## Discovered Information

- An unreliable, lossy connection apparently spliced into a fiber trunk explains the site's intermittent Matrix signal.
- Files mainly cover expected shipments, arrival times, and pickup work orders.
- Poorly closed personal-message sessions exposed dissatisfaction among the men.
- At least twenty men use the base; long and overnight patrols collect packages for later pickup by people from the city.
- The operation appears substantially independent; the recovered material does not establish its wider Humanis command links.
- No connected cameras or on-site security controls were found.
- Downloaded records refer to a secure holding area somewhere in the building. The successful analysis did not locate it precisely or identify Sandusky's evidence.

## Session 2026-09-24 Intrusion

Mevin's first entry attempt failed; after a day or two of probing he succeeded and recovered the logistics information. He later re-entered and successfully forged an existing pickup order, moving it a day early and substituting the crew's delivery. The gang accepted the shipment and carried the package inside.

This was a logistics deception, not a completed evidence theft or a Hardcastle voice impersonation. No persistent administrator access, remote building control, trace outcome, or additional hidden-host discoveries were established.

## Open Questions

- What was the legitimate shipment whose schedule was changed?
- Will anyone notice the amended order or trace the delivery?
- Where is the referenced secure holding area, and does it contain the requested old evidence?
