# Reputation: Optional Rules

These are optional SR3 rules from a gamemaster companion, not core SR3 rules. Cross-check the core rulebook before presenting them as default rules. Reputation has three separate scores: Street Cred, Notoriety and Public Awareness. (pp. 120-121)

## Street Cred

Street Cred represents lifetime accomplishments in the shadows. It equals Total Karma earned divided by 10, rounded normally; the book's example gives 34 Total Karma a Street Cred of 3. The GM may award additional points for exceptional accomplishments. (p. 120)

For applicable Social Skill Open Tests, Street Cred supplies its full modifier. For regular Social Skill Tests, it supplies Complementary skill dice. These benefits apply only when the other party knows the character's reputation. (p. 120)

## Notoriety

Notoriety represents the negative side of a character's reputation and starts at 0. The book lists these modifiers: Bad Reputation (+its Value), Braggart (+1), Good Reputation (−its Value), Liar (+1), Police Record (+3) and Uncouth (+2). The GM may award additional points. (p. 120)

For intimidation against people who know the notorious reputation, add Notoriety to Street Cred. A character can reduce Notoriety by sacrificing 2 Street Cred for each point reduced. (p. 121)

## Public Awareness

Public Awareness normally equals **20 − Street Cred − Notoriety**. The book's example gives Street Cred 11 and Notoriety 2 a Public Awareness of 7. It is the target number for appropriate Knowledge Skill Tests to determine whether someone outside the shadows has heard of a runner and what they know. (p. 121)

The GM may adjust the score for low-profile efforts or media exposure. Under the campaign's ruling, lower Public Awareness means easier recognition: the printed statements that 15+ is well known and 20+ is a household word are an editorial error and are not used. (p. 121; GM ruling, 2026-11-16)
