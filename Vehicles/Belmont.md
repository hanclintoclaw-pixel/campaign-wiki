## Role

A combat crawler drone in Curtis's active rigger stable. During the Happy Cat investigation, Curtis used Belmont for roof surveillance and captured images of suspicious communications equipment later identified as part of a laser modem link into the neighboring arkoblock.

## White Creek Deployment

In [Session 2026-09-24](../Sessions/2026-09-24.md), Belmont deployed from Grandpa under Curtis's Captain's Chair orders. Its opening rifle attack forced a guard into cover without hitting him. It then crossed the broken defenses into the parking lot and fired **one incendiary grenade**, which struck above a window and ignited exterior cladding. It remains in the parking-lot assault position at the pause. No damage, loss, or permanent stat change was recorded.
