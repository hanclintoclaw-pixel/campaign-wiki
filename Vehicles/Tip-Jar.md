# Tip Jar

![Tip Jar, Cindy Lou Jenkins's Renraku Barback busking avatar](../assets/images/Tip-Jar-Header-2026-08-15.png)

*Tip Jar gives Cindy Lou a visible meat-space busking body: charming enough for a street corner, mechanical enough that nobody mistakes her for flesh.*

## Overview

**Tip Jar** is Cindy Lou Jenkins's customized **Renraku Barback**: an anthroform service drone repurposed as a meat-space busking avatar, social decoy, and public-facing performance body.

In stock form, the Renraku Barback is built to replace metahuman bartenders, wait-staff, and bus-staff in clubs, restaurants, casinos, and wealthy private homes. Cindy's version keeps the service-industry chassis but points it toward street-corner charm, acoustic-stage presence, and the occasional very deliberate distraction.

Part of the campaign's dedicated **Drones / Vehicles** reference layer rather than the general location index.

## Cindy Lou Customization Package

These are campaign-use hooks for Cindy's Barback rather than separate canon SR3 vehicle entries.

- **Busking speaker/amp package:** lets Cindy perform, speak, or play saved voice lines through the drone.
- **Microphone and local pickup:** captures nearby speech, crowd noise, and performance feedback.
- **Tip credstick reader:** lets the avatar plausibly collect street tips, club fees, or cover-charge theater.
- **Instrument handling profile:** uses the Barback's two Strength 6 mechanical arms for guitar case handling, percussion props, gear setup, or staged service work.
- **Performance disguise kit:** clothing, wig, cosmetic shell plates, or ColorChange-style accent panels can change the presentation without pretending the drone is metahuman on close inspection.
- **Safe-service behavior:** defaults to non-combat public-facing movement unless the GM explicitly authorizes a riskier use.

## Role in Cindy Lou Initiative Plans

Tip Jar gives Cindy a reason to suggest plans that involve being seen.

Good uses include:

- busking near a target site to provide a social reason for Cindy's local presence;
- drawing eyes, microphones, or security attention away from the crew;
- acting as a polite public face when a Matrix ghost needs to talk to someone in meat-space;
- holding a doorway, managing a crowd beat, moving a small prop, or creating a noisy distraction;
- giving Cindy a visible avatar for scenes where a voice from a van would feel too abstract.

Bad uses include frontline combat, impersonating a real person under close scrutiny, or pretending the Barback is subtle in places where a medium anthroform service drone is out of context.
