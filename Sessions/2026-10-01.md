# Session 2026-10-01

## Summary

**The crew recovered Lula Bell Sandusky-labeled records from the basement of the White Creek Civic Center and escaped the burning building with additional concealed paydata and an old terminal. The Humanis evidence-retrieval job is not yet complete: the crew still needs to identify the material's significance and make the promised handoff.**

Kurgan and newly introduced runner Kyron joined Curtis, Mevin, and Kilimanjaro as the previous breach continued. The site was already burning and filling with smoke and lingering knockout gas. After Kilimanjaro, Kurgan, Curtis, and Mevin converged on the interior search, the crew located the Sandusky material, loaded it onto two hand trucks and Curtis's carrier bin, and removed an old terminal with its attached monitor. A further search uncovered several pieces of hidden paydata whose contents and value remain unknown.

Two fire elementals were seen accelerating the blaze. Kyron's astral perception also found a powerful great-form tainted spirit of man over the building; its summoner and purpose were not established. The team chose to leave rather than engage. They made it outside with their recovered material and intended to return to their vehicles and flee, potentially with something in pursuit.

**No Karma, nuyen payout, or job-completion award was recorded.** The requested window was approximately **8:10-10:05 p.m. Central**. The available Discord transcript begins around **8:15 p.m. Central** and the session-end marker lands around **10:04 p.m. Central**; the thread closed moments later.

## Major Scenes

- **Kurgan's return and new runner:** Kurgan arrived with Kyron, a new magical associate, while the prior team was still working inside White Creek. The group exchanged quick introductions and joined the existing communications network.
- **A burning, gas-filled site:** Curtis, Mevin, and Kilimanjaro continued the interior operation amid smoke, heat, and the earlier knockout gas. The building's fire-suppression system was not functioning; Mevin advised the crew to watch for usable extinguishers.
- **Basement recovery:** Kilimanjaro and Kurgan searched and moved material while Curtis used his mechanical arms and carrier bin. The GM identified a large cache labeled with Lula Bell Sandusky's pre-Crash arrest-record information. The desired evidence was recovered from the site, though its contents and significance were not fully examined.
- **Kurgan's encounter:** Kurgan found a ganger choking on the knockout gas and killed him with a shotgun. He took the man's original-run Dukes of Hazzard commemorative medallion; the victim's identity was not established.
- **Old terminal and extra paydata:** Mevin located and removed an old terminal and its attached monitor. The crew also found several concealed pieces of paydata among the other material; they did not identify or appraise it during the escape.
- **A magical threat at the fire:** Kyron's detection registered hostiles near the fire and an astral presence above. The visible attackers were two fire elementals methodically feeding the blaze. Astral perception identified the overhead presence as a powerful great-form tainted spirit of man; it stared down at Kyron, but no direct attack or identity was established.
- **Withdrawal:** Rather than fight the elementals or delay in the structure, the crew carried out the recovered records, terminal, and paydata. The GM described the building's front fully engulfed and a tall column of black smoke. The crew planned to return to their vehicles and flee, leaving any pursuit unresolved.
