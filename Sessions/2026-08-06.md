# Session 2026-08-06

## Summary

With only **Curtis** and **Kurgan** at the table, the crew chose a player-driven follow-up to the loose **Pixel Sticks scorpion drone** problem rather than starting a new contract. Curtis wanted to find the drone before its whitelist / biometric data became leverage against the crew, while Kurgan saw both a city-security concern and a chance to pull on the sponsor thread behind the Pixel Sticks' military-grade gear.

Initial legwork confirmed the aftermath of the Vernon Winfrey arkoblock fight: the police cordon had been broken by a fast-moving drone with active camouflage, several Pixel Sticks leaders had been taken into custody, and **Chunky Sparkles** had slipped away from magical containment. **Glenn** confirmed the Hidden Enforcers had been called because of the powerful awakened creature. **Herrick** confirmed the drone had fled south along the old I-65 corridor, toward go-gang territory, and that its active camouflage looked like expensive ruthenium polymer rather than ordinary gang hardware.

Curtis and Kurgan traced the damaged scorpion drone into **South Side Highwaymen** territory, found it hidden under debris with failing cloak and silver road-disks deployed around it, and decided not to simply walk up to it. Curtis identified that a remote-control encryption/decryption module could potentially let them talk to the old Pixel Sticks control channel, then bought one through **Taco** and waited about forty-eight hours near the site while Kurgan maintained a close stakeout and Curtis kept **Grandpa** hidden nearby.

The waiting period ended when three armed go-ganger vehicles returned. Curtis launched **The Finisher** and **Belmont** while Kurgan stayed concealed near the disabled scorpion. Between Curtis's drones, the damaged scorpion drone's remaining weapons, and the go-gangers' poor luck, the Highwaymen vehicles were wrecked or driven off. The scorpion drone survived but was left badly damaged and no longer cloaked. Curtis chose to haul it away as a long-term rebuild / nuyen-sink project rather than an instant upgrade.

## Major Scenes

- Curtis proposed using the player-driven night to track the military-grade scorpion drone that had escaped the Pixel Sticks / Chunky Sparkles fight with the crew's whitelist data still inside it.
- The table confirmed Curtis's backpack-arms build was complete, and Curtis wanted to wait before restarting daily project pings until the next major drone project existed.
- Kurgan and Curtis reviewed public aftermath from the arkoblock fight: police response, a dramatic news cycle around Chunky Sparkles fighting a scorpion robot, and likely surviving Pixel Sticks prisoners.
- Kurgan called **Glenn**, now firmly tied to the **Hidden Enforcers**, to ask about the magical side of the aftermath.
- Glenn confirmed the Hidden Enforcers had been called in because of the powerful awakened creature; they failed to contain **Chunky Sparkles**, who slipped away.
- Kurgan and Curtis established that several Pixel Sticks leader figures were taken into custody.
- The crew clarified that the Pixel Sticks used an "idiot box" style wireless control interface for the scorpion drone, while Curtis and Mevin had added crew biometrics / whitelist entries without wiping every original administrative route.
- Kurgan reached for the City Hall / police side through **Herrick** rather than escalating directly to **Joseph Neumann** or **Mayor Haggar**.
- Herrick confirmed someone sponsored the Pixel Sticks with high-end Matrix utilities, and that unknown raiders later removed those utilities.
- Herrick also had a lead on the drone: after the battle, it broke through a police cordon under active camouflage, then headed south toward old I-65 go-gang territory.
- Curtis and Kurgan identified the active camouflage as **ruthenium polymer**, a brittle and very expensive military/corporate-grade coating that should be far beyond normal youth-gang resources.
- The discussion around robot brains established that the scorpion drone could plausibly be smart enough to hide itself under physical debris while damaged.
- Curtis and Kurgan used drone surveillance and local knowledge to narrow the drone's likely resting place to an old broken-down area in the southern corridor.
- Kurgan approached on foot while Curtis hid **Grandpa** and used aerial / sensor support.
- Kurgan found the scorpion drone under a tree / debris pile, heavily damaged, hot in uneven places, and showing failing ruthenium cloak.
- Kurgan noticed several clean, featureless silver disks set on nearby roadways and holes on the drone's back where more disks may have been stored.
- Curtis and Kurgan treated the disks as likely munitions, mini-drones, or sensor / denial hardware rather than harmless debris.
- Kurgan identified local tags as the **South Side Highwaymen**, a go-gang controlling or taxing the nearby route.
- Curtis picked up a beacon / call-home signal from the damaged scorpion, suggesting it had been calling for help for days without rescue.
- Cindy Lou provided SR3 gear guidance that pointed the table toward a remote-control encryption/decryption module as the best match for trying to talk to the drone's old command channel.
- Curtis bought the needed module through **Taco**, succeeded well enough to get it in roughly two days, and the pair chose to stake out the site instead of leaving the drone unattended.
- Kurgan and Curtis concealed **Grandpa** in the brush and hid through the waiting period without damaging the vehicle or drawing attention.
- During the stakeout, Kurgan watched the scorpion drone reposition itself as three armed go-ganger vehicles approached.
- Curtis launched **The Finisher** as a stealthy intercept and prepared **Belmont** as a heavier gun platform.
- The returning go-gangers were likely following up on the prior chase / firefight rather than operating as the drone's owners.
- The damaged scorpion drone attacked the vehicles, including with its remaining cyber-disks / road-denial hardware, while Curtis's drones opened fire from supporting angles.
- Belmont and The Finisher helped wreck the go-ganger vehicles while Kurgan remained hidden and was not spotted.
- The scorpion's cloak was effectively ruined by the end of the fight; it slumped or shut down rather than cleanly escaping.
- Curtis chose to haul the scorpion drone away, with the table agreeing it should become a long-term staged project rather than an immediate combat upgrade.

## Clues Gained

- Several Pixel Sticks leaders survived the arkoblock battle and were taken into custody.
- The Hidden Enforcers were called in specifically because of Chunky Sparkles, but the creature escaped.
- Someone sponsored the Pixel Sticks with high-end utilities; someone later raided or cleaned out those utilities.
- The scorpion drone broke the police cordon under active camouflage rather than being destroyed at the arkoblock.
- The drone fled south toward old I-65 / 840 go-gang territory.
- Its camouflage appears to be ruthenium polymer: extremely expensive, brittle, and much more consistent with corporate / military supply than youth-gang improvisation.
- The drone likely had enough robotic intelligence to camouflage itself physically with debris after the ruthenium coating failed.
- The drone had been broadcasting or calling for help for days without obvious owner recovery.
- The clean silver disks found around it may be munitions, sensor extensions, or other road-denial hardware; they are not confirmed safe.
- The scorpion drone's original owner or sponsor either could not recover it quickly, chose not to, or was willing to abandon an extremely expensive asset.
- The South Side Highwaymen appear to have fought or chased the damaged drone after it entered their territory.

## Decisions Made

- Curtis and Kurgan prioritized recovering or neutralizing the scorpion drone over immediately pursuing the broader sponsor identity.
- Kurgan used Glenn for the magical aftermath and Herrick for the police / drone aftermath rather than escalating to the mayor.
- Curtis and Kurgan did not assume the crew's whitelist data made a direct approach safe.
- Curtis bought an encryption/decryption module through Taco and waited for delivery instead of risking a low-information retrieval.
- The pair chose a forty-eight-hour stakeout, keeping Kurgan close to the drone and Grandpa hidden nearby.
- Curtis deployed The Finisher and Belmont when the South Side Highwaymen returned, preserving the scorpion as salvage where possible.
- Curtis took the damaged scorpion drone away as a staged rebuild project / long-term nuyen sink.
