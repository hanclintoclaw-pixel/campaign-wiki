# Session 2026-07-30

## Summary

After the Pixel Sticks payout, the crew chose a player-driven follow-up on the old **Core 7 / Site 7** problem rather than taking a fresh contract. **Curtis** pushed the question of who originally built the dolphin-and-hurricane-seal facility, whether more old military technology might still be recoverable, and whether the mayor or the Draco Foundation should eventually know about a rogue awakened-animal project operating around Nashville.

The search led the crew back to the abandoned Site 7 ravine. **Kurgan** found the old vehicle access path and old **Pyramid** cigarette debris tied to CAS military culture, then found much fresher Pyramid packets suggesting someone with the same habit had checked the site within the last month or two. **Mevin**, **Cindy Lou**, and the Site 7 hardware confirmed the project used old CAS military-standard reinforced computer cabinets and cases. **Valgaut** detected only a low background count and no remaining hidden magical creature at the ravine.

The crew then questioned **Core 7** through a predator-to-predator framing that Valgaut helped shape. Core 7 revealed that the dolphins had tricked the "green-skin humans" into digging or forgetting the hole, admitted through fear and deception that someone may still be looking for him, and gave Mevin a Matrix profile / icon memory of the **Scarred Man** who once hunted Core 7 in the Matrix. Follow-up Matrix and contact work identified that figure as **Colonel Earl Stryker**, a disgraced CAS officer tied to a pre-Crash aquatic-mammal wetware project and later known in shadowrunner circles as **Dead Soldier**.

## Major Scenes

- Curtis pitched a group follow-up on the dolphin enclosure / Site 7 mystery: identify who built it, whether similar abandoned sites or technology exist, and whether a rogue military element is still active around Nashville.
- The crew agreed the hook mattered to multiple characters: Curtis wanted salvage and answers, Kurgan saw a city-security concern, Valgaut saw a Draco Foundation / awakened-creature abuse angle, and Mevin had a Matrix architecture problem to chase.
- Mevin asked Cindy Lou to help profile the Site 7 architecture. Cindy rolled **2 successes** on Computer 5 versus TN 5, helping reduce Mevin's Matrix search difficulty.
- Mevin's first detailed Matrix search failed and consumed time, leaving the physical team to inspect the old site while he continued archival work.
- The crew returned to the Site 7 ravine beyond the stripped warehouse zone. Valgaut detected no active magical creature, but did find a low background count in the ravine bottom.
- Kurgan found an overgrown access road / work cut that likely brought the enormous digging machine into the ravine years ago.
- Kurgan found old **Pyramid** cigarette material near the construction / work area. Curtis recognized Pyramid as a CAS military-associated cigarette brand still wildcat-produced for expat veterans and black-market customers.
- Curtis also spun a probably-unreliable but memorable theory that Pyramid cigarettes might once have carried a combat-drug effect.
- Mevin fed the CAS/Pyramid lead back into the search. Cindy rolled **3 successes** on Computer 5 versus TN 5, and the team confirmed the Site 7 hardware and recovered cabinets matched CAS military-standard cases from about 25 years earlier.
- Kurgan found much fresher Pyramid cigarette packets, probably discarded within the last month or two, matching a similar clue the crew had once found near the old Franklin art-gallery site.
- Curtis and Mevin considered whether a long-duration surveillance drone could have watched the Site 7 area, but no hard physical trace was found.
- The team returned to Core 7 and used careful dolphin/predator framing instead of a threat posture.
- Core 7 described tricking the "green-skin humans" into digging a big hole and forgetting the hole was there, leaving Core 7 safe in the "bright ocean."
- When Curtis asked whether anyone was looking for him now, Valgaut sensed fear and Curtis read the dolphin's denial as a lie.
- Mevin and Valgaut reframed the question as apex predators seeking worthy prey, which persuaded Core 7 to describe the **Scarred Man** and the way Core 7 erased the "song" / trace of where he had gone.
- Core 7 transmitted a Matrix-side memory/profile of the Scarred Man through CLJ: a hideous mechanical skeletal icon and enough profile data for Mevin to parse.
- Mevin's Matrix Security Procedures roll identified the profile as a very early Matrix primitive icon, a Matrix bogeyman that had last hunted or followed Core 7 roughly **15 years** ago.
- Mevin and Valgaut made contact-legwork inquiries through Dr. Bruce Gallstone and Valgaut's Draco Foundation researcher contact about awakened/aquatic animal trafficking and old military interest.
- Valgaut's contact confirmed rumors of a CAS military unit, before the Crash, trying to exploit human-adjacent aquatic mammal brain architecture; a scandal or disaster shut that unit down.
- Mevin searched news/archive references to the scandal and identified **Colonel Earl Stryker**.
- Kurgan asked around shadowrunner haunts, especially Atlass, and learned that Stryker later operated under the handle **Dead Soldier**, doing reliable physical security, facility security, host security, and Matrix surveillance work around the CAS.
- The session ended with Mevin able to spend several days trying to trace Stryker's Matrix icon/profile if Stryker is still out there.

## NPCs Introduced / In Play

- **Colonel Earl Stryker / Dead Soldier** - disgraced CAS officer tied to the old aquatic-mammal wetware project; later a reliable but not legendary shadow operator specializing in physical/security and Matrix surveillance work.
- **Core 7** - cybered dolphin / Site 7 wetware intelligence; supplied the Scarred Man Matrix profile but lied from fear when asked whether anyone is still looking for him.
- **Dr. Bruce Gallstone** - confirmed that awakened-animal trafficking or acquisition is heavily regulated and dangerous, especially in the CAS.
- **Valgaut's Draco Foundation researcher contact** - confirmed rumors of a pre-Crash CAS aquatic-mammal brain-architecture project and its shutdown after scandal or disaster.

## Clues Gained

- Site 7's remaining/recovered computer hardware matches old CAS military-standard reinforced cabinets and cases from roughly 25 years ago.
- The ravine looks artificially cut or expanded by the enormous digging machine; the machine may have dug its own grave or been abandoned in place after excavation work.
- Old Pyramid cigarette debris links the original worksite to CAS military culture; fresher Pyramid packets suggest someone with the same brand habit visited the area within the last month or two.
- Pyramid is no longer broadly mainstream but is still black-market / wildcat-produced for people who want that old soldier-associated brand.
- The same Pyramid clue pattern connects back to a cigarette packet found near the old Franklin art-gallery site.
- Core 7 says the dolphins tricked the "green-skin humans" into digging or forgetting the hole; the exact meaning remains partly filtered through dolphin communication and player interpretation.
- Core 7 is afraid of someone still looking for him, even though he denied it aloud.
- Core 7 remembers the Scarred Man as a Matrix bogeyman who hunted or followed him; Core 7 last saw that signature roughly 15 years ago.
- The Scarred Man's Matrix icon/profile is a traceable lead, even if the icon has changed, because the old deck/profile leaves recognizable Matrix resonance.
- CAS military rumors point to a pre-Crash effort to use aquatic mammals' human-adjacent brain architecture, shut down after scandal or disaster.
- Public/archive material names **Colonel Earl Stryker** as the disgraced officer tied to a major misappropriation scandal and a North Nashville civic-construction expansion that was abandoned when equipment proved too dug-in to remove.
- Shadowrunner old-heads identify **Dead Soldier** as Stryker's later operator handle, with a history of reliable security and surveillance work.

## Decisions Made

- The crew chose the Core 7 / Site 7 follow-up as the night's player-driven work.
- The crew treated the old habitat as an investigation scene rather than a salvage target, since Curtis had already stripped most valuable hardware during the rescue/relocation work.
- Kurgan pursued the physical trail and later committed to watching the site for several days to see if anyone returned.
- Curtis chose not to push Core 7 too hard once the dolphin lied from fear, preserving Core 7's sense of control and safety.
- Mevin asked for Core 7's memory of the Scarred Man as a Matrix profile/icon rather than merely a human name.
- The crew did not immediately reveal everything to Valgaut's initiatory group; Valgaut kept the Draco Foundation contact inquiry narrower and lower-key.
- The table left Mevin's trace of Stryker's Matrix icon/profile as a follow-up blue-book / next-session hook.
