# Session 2026-08-13

## Summary

With **Valgaut** preparing to step more fully toward the Draco Foundation's **Wyrmwatch Circle**, the crew chose another player-driven follow-up instead of taking a prepared contract: locate **Chunky Sparkles**, document or recover the anomalous awakened creature, and use the work as Valgaut's service to his initiatory group. The Wyrmwatch Circle offered a modest bounty for actionable information and a larger interest in securing the creature, while the crew also wanted to clean up the loose end before Chunky Sparkles became a threat to Curtis, Core 7, or the city.

The crew combined Mevin's Matrix research, Kurgan's old memory of the first Chunky Sparkles incident, Curtis's drone surveillance, Taco's sheepish admission that the creature had escaped from an improvised field, and Herrick's access to surviving Pixel Sticks prisoners. The key handling plan became simple rather than violent: use nutrient-rich equine food, avoid hostile intent, and exploit a short set of command words that **Lizzo Wells** had been taught for Chunky Sparkles.

The trail led to **Radnor Lake**, now an overgrown and strangely clear awakened-wild area south of Nashville. The crew found signs of loose mana, blood ticks, an aquatic awakened predator, and eventually Chunky Sparkles bedded down under a collapsed tree. At dawn, Valgaut and Kurgan moved in with the bait while Curtis watched through drone overwatch and Mevin supported the plan from Grandpa. When the lake predator moved on Chunky Sparkles, the crew intervened: Chunky Sparkles fired a depleted lightning blast, Curtis and Kurgan helped bring the creature down, and Valgaut used boosted strength and his axe to finish the regenerating gator before it could rise again.

After the fight, Chunky Sparkles responded positively to the rescue, the food, and the recovered command phrases. The crew lured it back to **Grandpa**, and the table hand-waved the final handoff/drop-off as complete enough for the Wyrmwatch follow-up. The group split **20,000¥** four ways and each character received **4 Karma**.

## Major Scenes

- The table recapped the prior scorpion-drone recovery and confirmed the recovered drone was in catastrophic shape.
- Curtis's scorpion-drone salvage path was clarified: the ruined drone can provide the equivalent of a Rating 3 robot-brain core that may be scavenged into a couple of Rating 2 robot brains, while any true rebuild remains a long, expensive, customized project.
- Valgaut reported the anomalous Chunky Sparkles encounter to the Draco Foundation / Wyrmwatch Circle as part of his initiatory relationship.
- Wyrmwatch treated the matter as a qualifying deed-style service: ideally secure the creature, but at minimum locate, document, and research it.
- The crew chose the player-driven Chunky Sparkles follow-up rather than a prepared contract.
- Kurgan remembered the older Chunky Sparkles incident and Taco's earlier attempt to befriend / contain the creature.
- Mevin assisted Valgaut with Matrix research on how to lure or handle a partly equine awakened creature without escalating to violence.
- The research pointed to nutrient-rich equine feed, varied food incentives, and avoiding hostile intent.
- Cindy Lou was asked what Chunky Sparkles had been eating at Happy Cat; the table recovered that it was hay and oats mixed with a high-calorie slurry of junk food, ground candy, and corn syrup.
- Taco admitted he had planned to build a pen but had only kept Chunky Sparkles in a field; the creature eventually flew away and Taco had no way to track it.
- Curtis reasoned that Chunky Sparkles probably did not travel far past the first sufficiently comfortable wilderness / water source, narrowing likely locations toward Radnor Lake or Percy Priest.
- The crew chose **Radnor Lake** as the first lead because it lay upstream from the city and offered cleaner water, abandoned wilderness, and cover.
- Valgaut spent **500¥** on specialty hay / equine food and junk-food bait before the crew left Nashville.
- The crew loaded into **Grandpa**, with Curtis preparing drone overwatch, Kurgan and Valgaut ready for field approach, and Mevin joining the support team.
- Curtis launched a surveillance drone around Radnor Lake while Kurgan watched for signs of Chunky Sparkles' destructive lightning.
- The lake appeared overgrown, suspiciously clear, lightly inhabited at a distance, and quietly maintained by an unknown party or force.
- Valgaut sensed loose mana, blood ticks, an aquatic awakened presence near the waterline, and a broader malevolent hostility from the lake.
- Kurgan contacted **Herrick**, who reached a surviving Pixel Sticks prisoner and learned that **Lizzo Wells** had been given commands and incentives for handling Chunky Sparkles.
- Herrick passed along a short command set, including halt, come, attack, and flee, in an unfamiliar language.
- The crew waited until dawn rather than trying to move Chunky Sparkles at night.
- Curtis's morning drone sweep found Chunky Sparkles under a collapsed tree, oversized awakened insects nearby, and an alligator-like aquatic predator lurking just offshore.
- Kurgan guided Valgaut down a safer approach path while Curtis stayed with Grandpa and drone overwatch.
- When Chunky Sparkles moved toward the water, the crew recognized that the gator-like predator might be hunting it.
- Chunky Sparkles fired a lightning blast, but Valgaut's magical read suggested its internal power reservoir was severely depleted.
- Curtis and Kurgan helped bring the gator down; Kurgan's shot collapsed it after accumulated damage.
- Valgaut recognized the regeneration risk and used boosted Strength 10 with his axe to bisect the prone gator, ending the magic in it before it could recover.
- The blood ticks scattered and other magical creatures fled after the superior lake predator died.
- Chunky Sparkles returned to grazing, then responded favorably to the crew, the food, and the command words.
- The crew lured Chunky Sparkles back toward Grandpa; it followed by flying up onto Grandpa as the vehicle pulled away from Radnor Lake.

## NPCs / Factions Introduced or In Play

- **Chunky Sparkles** - recovered from the Pixel Sticks / Happy Cat fallout; confirmed depleted after prior violence but still dangerous and responsive to food / command incentives.
- **Draco Foundation - Wyrmwatch Circle** - Valgaut's initiatory group; sponsored the follow-up and treated it as service toward his Foundation path.
- **Lizzo Wells** - surviving Pixel Sticks prisoner; provided, through Herrick, the command/incentive details previously used to handle Chunky Sparkles.
- **Herrick** - used his police-side access to reach a Pixel Sticks prisoner and pass the command set to Kurgan.
- **Taco** - admitted Chunky Sparkles escaped from his improvised field containment because he had not yet built a real pen.
- **Radnor Lake awakened predator** - an alligator-like magical or awakened predator with regeneration, killed by the crew.
- **Blood ticks** - oversized awakened carnivorous insects around Radnor Lake, likely drawn by the local loose mana.

## Clues Gained

- Chunky Sparkles can be influenced by food and command phrases rather than only force.
- The preferred / known bait mixture is hay and oats plus a grotesque high-calorie slurry of junk food, ground candy, and corn syrup.
- Lizzo Wells was given commands and incentives for Chunky Sparkles, suggesting the Pixel Sticks did not merely improvise its handling.
- The command set included at least halt, come, attack, and flee, in an unfamiliar language.
- Taco never truly secured Chunky Sparkles; it flew away from his field before he built a proper pen.
- Radnor Lake is overgrown, unusually clear, and awakened-active, with blood ticks, an aquatic predator, and a hostile magical feel.
- Loose mana at Radnor Lake likely drew Chunky Sparkles there.
- Chunky Sparkles' lightning was still dangerous but noticeably depleted after its recent ordeals.
- The Radnor Lake gator-like predator had regeneration or a similar recovery trait; finishing it after collapse mattered.
- Wyrmwatch has enough interest in anomalous awakened animals to pay for field follow-up and likely custody.

## Decisions Made

- The crew chose the player-driven Chunky Sparkles / Wyrmwatch follow-up instead of a prepared run.
- Valgaut took point because the deed supported his Draco Foundation / Wyrmwatch path.
- The team prioritized luring and rescue / containment over trying to kill Chunky Sparkles.
- Mevin supported the research angle rather than making the run a direct Matrix scene.
- Kurgan used Herrick for prisoner-side information, accepting some political visibility risk because of his growing mayoral-security role.
- Curtis kept Grandpa and drone overwatch positioned rather than entering the woods directly.
- The crew waited for dawn before moving on Chunky Sparkles.
- Valgaut and Kurgan approached on foot with bait and command phrases while Curtis prepared drone/fire support.
- The crew intervened against the gator-like predator, partly to keep Chunky Sparkles alive and partly to earn its trust.
- Valgaut finished the regenerating predator instead of leaving it down but recoverable.
- Valgaut split the Wyrmwatch money evenly with the crew.
