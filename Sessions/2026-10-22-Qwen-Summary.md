## Summary

**The night resumed in the middle of a firefight outside a Nashville McDonald's and ended with the crew rolling away paid: the shedim that had emerged when Lula Bell Sandusky's body was destroyed was banished, the last possessed corpse was put down, a living guard and a dead host rode back in Grandpa, and the evidence—now including Buzz's video of the transformation—was sold to the mayor's office.**

Kilimanjaro's opening burst killed a living security guard. The spirit raised three dead guards as possessed shamblers. Kyron wounded it with a mana spell; Kurgan's shotgun and Kilimanjaro's grenade added damage. Curtis shook off the lingering charm, took control of Belmont, and drove the mini-tank through the restaurant. Two interior guards fled and Curtis shot them down. The RV withstood the shamblers' attacks while Mevin took cover behind the counter.

The team eventually banished the shedim. Kyron identified it and explained that it would be barred from returning for about thirty days, not permanently destroyed. Kurgan captured a surviving guard alive; the crew also recovered a possessed host body for study. At Taco's compound, the captive was identified as Tom Erlichman and secured, while the corpse went into the deep freeze. Cindy Lou wiped the restaurant's security footage before police arrived.

Buzz's surveillance video captured the explosion and the monster emerging from Sandusky's body. The crew added it to the evidence file. A call to Princeps brought denial and hostility; the mayor's office remained the buyer under the previously agreed ¥60,000 deal. The GM awarded ¥12,000 and 8 Karma to each runner; Kilimanjaro's nuyen share was set aside for a future character.

- **Belmont breaks through.** Curtis used Karma on his Willpower test to shake off the compulsion that made him overlook Sandusky's actions. With Curtis's consent, the crew transported the corpse in Grandpa for study. Cindy Lou erased the McDonald's security footage after the GM called for a successful Computer check.
