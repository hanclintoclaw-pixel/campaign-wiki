# Session 2026-09-24

## Summary

**Curtis, Mevin, and Kilimanjaro continued the Humanis evidence retrieval, turning their White Creek reconnaissance into a successful delivery deception and an assault on the civic center. The contract remains unfinished: the crew reached the basement, but has not identified or recovered Lula Bell Sandusky's evidence.**

Mevin breached the Ring of Fire's thin, unreliable waystation host and found shipment schedules, poorly secured personal communications, and references to a secure holding area. Rather than impersonating Paul Hardcastle's voice, he altered an existing pickup order to occur a day early. Mevin's thrift-store flunkies handled the delivery, and the gang carried the crew's disguised Neuro-Stun VII package inside its own base.

The ensuing breach scattered or incapacitated several Torchmen. Grandpa pushed through the vehicle barricade without damage; Kilimanjaro took the roof and then entered through its unlocked hatch, while Curtis directed Belmont and The Finisher from the RV. Two exterior guards were killed, several occupants were incapacitated, the building's exterior caught fire, and one ganger fled on foot without pursuit. Buzz was safely recalled to Grandpa.

**The session stopped with Kilimanjaro inside a crowded basement storage room, Curtis and Mevin heading toward the building in gas masks, and The Finisher guarding Grandpa. No Karma, nuyen payout, or completion award was granted.**

## Major Scenes

- **Back in Nashville:** The crew resumed from the previous session's shopping and Matrix-preparation pause. Kurgan was absent again; the active PCs were Curtis, Mevin, and Kilimanjaro.
- **Buzz's recordings:** Curtis recovered conversations involving Paul Hardcastle and other Torchmen. The recordings confirmed the site as a waystation moving people and material for Humanis-affiliated groups; no additional named gang members were established.
- **Host intrusion:** An initial failed attempt cost Mevin a day or two of probing before a successful retry. He found a lossy connection apparently spliced into a fiber trunk, shipment and arrival records, and carelessly open personal-message sessions. He found no connected cameras or on-site security controls.
- **Operational read:** At least twenty men used the site as a headquarters, with many away on long or overnight pickup patrols. The available records suggested substantial independence from other Humanis groups and dissatisfaction among the men, not a fully mapped command hierarchy.
- **Secure storage lead:** Mevin downloaded material and combined police/corporate-procedure knowledge to identify a secure holding area somewhere in the building. Its exact location was not established by that roll.
- **Supplies and contacts:** Kilimanjaro used an unnamed arms contact to buy four Neuro-Stun VII grenades, bought rope, and confirmed a second hand truck. Curtis confirmed Grandpa's existing hand truck and arranged hard-shell carrying gear. The final approach used fictional knockout gas rather than the earlier proposed explosive delivery.
- **Thrift-store help:** Mevin contacted the two flunkies associated with the crew's thrift-store safehouse. The GM explicitly established them as his Level 1 contacts. They agreed to handle the out-of-town delivery for promised Double Downs; they were not sent into the civic center with the attack team.
- **Forged pickup:** After breaching the host again, Mevin successfully used Forgery to repurpose an existing work order, moving it a day early and inserting the crew's delivery into ordinary gang business.
- **Technical preparation:** Curtis and Mevin spent a few hours preparing the disguised gas package. The GM confirmed all three runners had gas masks. No gas-handling skill chip was bought.
- **Return to White Creek:** Buzz showed Hardcastle present and roughly ten gang members on site, including two regular exterior guards; about half the gang was away. Grandpa waited concealed nearby. Mevin traveled with the crew, with the GM warning that out-of-town Matrix connectivity would be unreliable despite the RV's retrofit.
- **Delivery and breach:** A gang vehicle returned with the package, and a Torchman carried it through the side door. Curtis activated it after entry and brought Grandpa through the barricade, deploying the combat drones. The GM explicitly ruled that the reinforced RV took no damage.
- **Roof attack:** Kilimanjaro scaled his familiar approach and fired two fragmentation grenades from the roof. The first destroyed the near guard's sandbag cover; the second killed the farther guard and collapsed his pillbox. The Finisher then killed the exposed near guard.
- **Drone recovery:** Curtis recalled Buzz to dock aboard Grandpa and moved Belmont into the parking lot over the broken defenses.
- **Upper-floor entry:** Wearing his gas mask, Kilimanjaro opened the rusty roof hatch, which was unlocked on this visit. He entered the second-floor stairwell and found three men rousing from their sleeping quarters. His flashbang put them down amid the gas; no final death determination was made for those three.
- **Fire and broken windows:** Belmont fired one incendiary grenade toward a window. It struck above the opening and ignited exterior cladding rather than landing inside. After checking Kilimanjaro's position over comms, Curtis had The Finisher shoot out the west-side second-floor windows.
- **Basement reached:** Two men emerging at ground level succumbed to the gas, while another tried to flee. Kilimanjaro descended to the basement and entered a room crowded with newer packages and moldering old boxes. He radioed that he had reached it and brought his hand truck into play.
- **Stopping point:** Curtis put on his gas mask and invited Mevin to come with him into the building, leaving The Finisher on guard around Grandpa. Curtis explicitly let the fleeing ganger escape on foot. The GM saved the board with the interior search still to play.

## Clues Gained

- White Creek's intermittent Matrix connection is a thin, unreliable waystation connection, apparently tapped into a fiber trunk. The intrusion revealed no remote camera/security system to take over.
- Shipping records and exposed personal communications support an operation moving people and goods, with at least twenty associated men and frequent overnight pickups. About ten were present when the crew returned.
- Existing work orders could be altered convincingly; the forged pickup succeeded. A Hardcastle voice impersonation was discussed but never executed.
- A secure holding area is mentioned in downloaded records, but it was not definitively located by the Matrix investigation. The basement room subsequently reached contains a large mix of new packages and decaying older boxes; the desired evidence has not been singled out.
- Hardcastle was present before the attack. None of the narrated casualties or the fleeing man was identified as him.
- The roof hatch had been noted as maglocked in the prior reconnaissance, but was explicitly unlocked when Kilimanjaro returned. This records the observed change, not a successful lock bypass.
- The basement search now takes place in a building affected by gas and an exterior fire. The upper floor was not comprehensively searched or declared clear.

## Decisions Made

- Do the meaningful Matrix work from Nashville before returning to unreliable rural connectivity.
- Use a forged routine pickup and Mevin's known flunkies, rather than hire random couriers or rely on a spoofed Hardcastle emergency.
- Use Neuro-Stun VII in the delivered package; keep incendiary fire under separate drone control.
- Split the approach between Kilimanjaro's roof entry and Curtis's drone-backed ground approach, with Mevin initially sheltered in Grandpa.
- Retrieve Buzz once its surveillance role was complete.
- Coordinate drone fire with Kilimanjaro's position instead of firing blindly into his floor.
- Leave one fleeing ganger unpursued and continue toward the evidence search.
