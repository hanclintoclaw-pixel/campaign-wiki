# Southeastern Novel Totems

**GM direction, 2026-11-17:** One major local project Cross Applied Technology is running in Nashville is a shamanic studies program focused on Christian shamans, studying **novel totems found only in the American Southeast**. The program and the four totem files below are table-created content, approved by the GM as posted on 2026-11-17 (New Cindy GM Interaction Thread); Witness was approved in a second pass the same day. They are not printed SR3 options; SR3 print supports the framing, as recorded below.

## What SR3 print supports

*Magic in the Shadows* pp.24-25 ("Christian Magic") states that liberal and "revivalist" theologies produce shamanistic magicians, including worshippers who speak in tongues and summon spirits of man that take on angelic forms; conservative believers may deny they practice "magic" at all, and religious tools run to the Bible, the cross, the rosary and holy water. *State of the Art 2064* pp.122-123 ("Magic and Christianity") covers the Catholic Order of St. Sylvester and its exorcists, the Westphalian Theurgists, and the Orthodox Exarchs. *Threats 2* pp.102-109 supplies the reborn Order of the Temple and the Order of St. Sylvester as Catholic militant orders. None of these is a Protestant revivalist tradition; the printed coverage leaves room for a Southern tradition of the table's own making, which is what this page records.

Real-world texture note: altar calls, tent revivals and gospel music are common features of Southern revivalism; speaking in tongues and prayer for healing belong particularly to Pentecostal and Charismatic practice; serpent handling is a rare Appalachian "Signs Following" Holiness observance, not a general evangelical custom. The Serpent-Bearer totem below draws on that rare tradition and does not represent Southern Christians generally.

## The Cross shamanic studies program

A megacorp does not fund religion; it funds a "research program" and lets the religion keep it company. The program runs scholarships, tent-revival field sites, and a dean who wears a chaplain's collar and carries a stock-option plan. Its stated object of study is the set of totems that appear only in the American Southeast; its unstated object is what those totems can be made to produce. Cross's intelligence branch, the Seraphim, is printed SR3 background (State of the Art 2064, pp.26, 33); the program's interest in them is this campaign's invention.

These shamans retain the standard shamanic mask like any SR3 shaman (SR3 core p.163): feathers, a dove's watchful eyes, a preacher's raised voice. Cross can photograph the mask. Whether it understands what answered is another question.

## Totem files

### Dove - comfort in the aftermath

Dove comes to the bedside, the flooded house, and the family that has run out of words. She hears the prayer that never gets past somebody's teeth and stays until somebody else can hear it too. Her shamans mend flesh, sit beside hospital beds, and sing when ordinary speech gives out. They may treat glossolalia as prayer or as a medium for cryptic omens, without assuming every ecstatic utterance is a message; the GM may use tongues as omen delivery and leave interpretation to the congregation.

Environment: Urban or rural communities.

Advantages: +2 dice for health spells; +2 dice for spirits of man, which may appear angelic to Dove's shamans.

Disadvantages: -1 die for combat spells. Before striking first in a confrontation, the shaman must succeed at a Willpower (6) Test. They cannot refuse a sincere request for comfort or healing without good reason.

CAT hook: Cross Biomedical offers scholarships and clinical placements to Dove's healers, then asks who owns the treatment records, and whether bedside prayer is part of a proprietary protocol. Cross Biomedical is printed SR3 material (Man and Machine, pp.64, 89); the trials and the paperwork are campaign inventions under this program.

### The Serpent-Bearer - faith without a safety net

The Serpent-Bearer carries death without pretending it has become harmless. He stands in the hollow church where the hymn keeps going after the box is opened, demanding faith without a guarantee that anybody goes home. His followers belong to the rare Signs Following tradition, reading Mark 16:17-18 as a present challenge rather than a safely distant promise. This Idol does not make every Southern Christian a snake handler, and his favor does not make venom cease to be venom.

Environment: Rural hills and mountain communities.

Advantages: +2 dice for health spells used against toxins and disease; +2 dice for spirits of the land, which may manifest in serpentine forms.

Disadvantages: -1 die for detection spells; their eyes remain fixed on what they carry. When confronted with a venomous serpent or an untested deadly poison, the shaman must handle it bare-handed unless they succeed at a Willpower (6) Test. This stricture is dangerous by design; the GM should treat it as a compulsion, not proof that the practice is safe.

CAT hook: Cross recruits Serpent-Bearer shamans for antivenom and toxicology work, where an unusual healing specialty can earn a clean laboratory and an ugly contract. The program's "faith under audit" screening makes the ethical problem explicit: someone in personnel has started treating a religious compulsion as a recruitment instrument.

### The Evangelist - the invitation that must mean something

The Evangelist raises the tent before the town has decided it needs saving. He knows the sermon, the altar call and the sawdust aisle, but judges the preacher by who gets helped after the last chorus. His shamans must speak where silence would leave someone stranded, whether the audience fills an auditorium or a bus shelter. A fine voice can gather a crowd; keeping faith when the collection comes up short is harder.

Environment: Urban or rural communities.

Advantages: +2 dice for illusion and control-manipulation spells when used in public preaching or public prayer; +2 dice for spirits of man.

Disadvantages: When the shaman believes speaking about their faith could genuinely help someone, they must succeed at a Willpower (6) Test to remain silent. Their personal code forbids exploiting the vulnerable for money or knowingly falsifying testimony; while knowingly violating it, they suffer +1 to all magical target numbers.

CAT hook: Lafayette Tower's Entertainment Division invites Evangelists into media and audience-response research. The corporation wants a revival that tests well; the Idol expects the invitation to be sincere.

### Witness - the truth that will not sit down

Witness takes the stand after the courtroom, town hall, or mob has already reached its verdict. This is the believer dragged from the pew to speak when silence would be safer, Sunday shoes planted square against the aisle. Fervent and unignorable, Witness carries truth as a weapon and calls its followers to muster like a militia. Its compulsion magic lands like testimony under a bare courthouse bulb, not a card slipped up somebody's sleeve.

Environment: Urban.

Advantages: +2 dice for control-manipulation spells used to expose or compel truth, including Compel Truth, Influence, Control Emotion, and Control Thoughts. +2 dice for spirits of man serving as witnesses or bearing witness.

Disadvantages: Witness shamans must never knowingly lie, including by omission in testimony. They must publicly confront a known lie when doing so would matter, unless they succeed at a Willpower (6) Test. If the lie is oppressing someone present and the shaman can act, resisting that obligation requires a Willpower (8) Test. They receive -1 die for illusion spells: Witness does not hide anything, including the shaman.

CAT hook: Cross's Nashville Entertainment Division wants Witness shamans as auditing instruments: live lie detectors for executive sessions and contract negotiations. But the company's "faith under audit" screening has started auditing Cross, too; the witness chair faces both ways.

## Epistemic notes

The totems' dice modifiers and strictures follow the SR3 core totem format (SR3 core, pp.162-163); the totems themselves are table-created content, GM-approved 2026-11-17. Whether the spirits that answer Dove or the Serpent-Bearer are angelic, man-spirits, land spirits or something Cross will want to instrument is a faith question and a table portrayal question, not settled by this page.
