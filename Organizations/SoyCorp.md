# SoyCorp

## Overview

**SoyCorp** markets **Bebop Cola** and is the parent behind **SoyCorp Meatlets**. (GM ruling, 2026-11-17, recorded in [Cindy's back catalog](../NPCs/Cindy-Lou-Jenkins-Back-Catalog.md))

## What the crew knows

- Cindy recorded jingles for SoyCorp Meatlets; the GM established these placements as both viral marketing and subliminal influence work. ([Back catalog](../NPCs/Cindy-Lou-Jenkins-Back-Catalog.md), GM ruling, 2026-11-16)
- The warehouse at [612 Wharf Avenue](../Locations/612-Wharf-and-Site-7.md) distributed counterfeit Meatlets and Bebop Cola. This identifies counterfeit stock, not SoyCorp as the warehouse's operator. ([612 Wharf and Site 7](../Locations/612-Wharf-and-Site-7.md))
