## Overview

**HEMP Global Agronomics** is a CAS agro-corporation introduced through **Byron L. Cedar**, a corporate lawyer who hired Kilimanjaro for the completed Judge Belisarius rush job.

## Known Facts

- HEMP Global Agronomics specializes in genetically engineered, high-yield, low-quality protein crops.
- Its business footprint runs through the southeastern CAS / broader North American market.
- Byron L. Cedar works for the corporation as a lawyer.
- Cedar's emergency implies HEMP has a stake in a court case assigned to **Judge Hiram Belisarius**.
- The Belisarius court-delay contract was completed in Session 2026-09-10, and Cedar reacted surprisingly positively despite the public kidnapping and shootout.
- The exact case and HEMP's direct stake remain unknown.
