# HEMP Global Agronomics

## Overview

**HEMP Global Agronomics** is a CAS agro-corporation introduced through **Byron L. Cedar**, a corporate lawyer who hired Kilimanjaro for the active Judge Belisarius rush job.

## Known Facts

- HEMP Global Agronomics specializes in genetically engineered, high-yield, low-quality protein crops.
- Its business footprint runs through the southeastern CAS / broader North American market.
- Byron L. Cedar works for the corporation as a lawyer.
- Cedar's current emergency implies HEMP has a stake in a court case assigned to **Judge Hiram Belisarius**.
