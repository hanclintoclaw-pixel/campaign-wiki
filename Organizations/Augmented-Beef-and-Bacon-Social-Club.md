## Canon status (GM direction, 2026-11-16)

The Augmented Beef and Bacon Social Club is a campaign organization, not a printed SR3 organization. A search of the local SR3 corpus found no match for “Augmented Beef”; its established details come from GM direction, campaign reference material, and play.

## Overview

The Social Club sells high-end soy-based and real meat-based items through premium vending machines throughout the Nashville metroplex. Vending is one of its lines of business, not necessarily the whole enterprise; the GM has not defined the complete portfolio. (GM direction, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-16)

The branding is deliberately ridiculous. The customer profiling underneath it is not.

## Lines of business

- **Premium vending:** High-end soy and genuine meat-based items sold through premium machines across Nashville. Both categories are established; neither replaces the other. (GM direction, 2026-11-16)
- **Membership and consumer marketing:** The public host supports a loyalty club, subscription offers, influencer reels, and product marketing. Its private side includes subscription fulfillment, advertising plans, customer-preference analytics, and test-market research. (Matrix Host and host profile)
- **Other business:** The GM specified “among other lines of business.” The full portfolio remains open; the host’s files are not an exhaustive corporate inventory. (GM direction, 2026-11-16)

## Premium vending

“Premium” establishes the machines’ market positioning and high-end food offering. Specific hardware, payment methods, refrigeration, security, service arrangements, machine counts, and individual sites have not been established.

The host’s public menu advertises artificial beef, bacon-flavored lifestyle bundles, and novelty snacks. Whether every machine carries those exact products is unknown. A branded wrapper does not authenticate its contents: counterfeit club vending snacks have reached the crew. (Host profile; Session 2026-04-30)

## Brand and membership

The public Matrix door presents a lifestyle club, including a loyalty tier called the **Smoke Ring**. Influencer sizzle reels and engineered appetite cues sell aspiration, masculinity theater, novelty, and manufactured authenticity. (Matrix Host and host profile)

The host renders the brand as a private meat club crossed with a corporate theme park: mahogany smokehouse aesthetics, butcher-block counters, neon signs, and executive dining rooms where every surface is advertising. Beneath that presentation sits a serious consumer-data operation. The marketing pitch makes membership and repeat orders signs of belonging, particularly for augmentation enthusiasts. (Matrix Host; GM ruling, 2026-11-16)
