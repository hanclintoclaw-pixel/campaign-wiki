**Stuffer Shack** appears in the campaign both as a shopping stop and as the name imitated by a counterfeit operation. The counterfeit site is not established as a legitimate branch. ([Session 2026-09-03](../Sessions/2026-09-03.md); [Session 2023-11-30](../Sessions/2023-11-30.md))
