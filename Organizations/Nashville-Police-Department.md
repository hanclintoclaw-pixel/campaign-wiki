# Nashville Police Department

## Overview

Nashville PD is a recurring part of the crew's city business: police cordons, prisoners, negotiated access, and favors. The characters' working relationship with [Herrick](../NPCs/Herrick.md) has helped them stay out of trouble by doing occasional work for the department. (Session 2026-08-06; Session 2026-08-27; GM direction, 2026-11-17)

## Canon status

## Public face and actual function

The department establishes cordons and holds suspects; Nashville police have also circulated warnings about foreign shadowrunners and smuggled weapons entering the city. Its dealings with runners are not limited to arrests: surviving Pixel Sticks leaders in custody were being pressured into gray-hat work under Herrick's oversight. (Session 2026-08-06; Late-January Headlines; Session 2026-08-27; Pixel Sticks 21st Floor Cell)

## The Herrick arrangement

Herrick is a senior NPD figure and the department's shadowrunner expert. He coordinates with known or assumed shadowrunner contacts, including the PCs. His rank is not established. [Mevin](../PCs/Mevin-Kitnick.md) holds the crew's main relationship with him; older material calls the same contact Harac. (GM direction, 2026-11-17; Herrick; Mevin Kitnick)

Mevin secured prisoner access through etiquette and police-procedure framing. Herrick stayed mostly hands-off, listening for information that threatened the city. That latitude has limits: the crew later left a restaurant scene before police intervention rather than spend goodwill explaining it. (Session 2026-08-27; Session 2026-10-22)

## Magical sphere

[Glenn](../NPCs/Glenn.md)'s [Hidden Enforcers](../Factions/Hidden-Enforcers.md) association is one of the crew's established city ties. Glenn and the Enforcers were called in for Chunky Sparkles after the arkoblock fight, but lost containment. Their involvement did not guarantee control of the scene. (Session 2026-08-06)

## Where the department has touched play

- After the Vernon Winfrey arkoblock fight, an active-camouflage drone broke through the police cordon. Kurgan reached toward City Hall and police through Herrick rather than escalating to Neumann or Haggar. Herrick traced the drone south along old I-65, identified its ruthenium-polymer camouflage as beyond youth-gang resources, and kept matters quiet to avoid federal escalation. ([Session 2026-08-06](../Sessions/2026-08-06.md))
- NPD and Herrick stayed out of the [CAT](Cross-Applied-Technology.md) / [Princeps](../Factions/The-Princeps-Group.md) conflict because the city had been paid not to investigate unless it spilled into city business. The payer is unidentified. [Kne@zle](../NPCs/Kne-zle.md) also directed the crew to leave [Handy-Sandy](../NPCs/Lizzo-Wells-Handy-Sandy.md) in police custody. ([Session 2026-08-27](../Sessions/2026-08-27.md))
- Eric's working relationship and Mevin's limited police-network access were reaffirmed. Mevin's Police Procedures check exposed response timing around repeated PANIC! button use. ([Session 2026-10-22](../Sessions/2026-10-22.md))
