# Cross Applied Technology

## Overview

Cross Applied Technology (CAT, printed as Cross Applied Technologies and commonly CATCo in SR3 sources) is a corporation with a Nashville presence in biotech recovery and entertainment research. The crew has worked for its representative Imogene Viola and has become entangled in its conflict with the Princeps Group. This page brings organization and division material together; Lafayette Tower, its Matrix host, and the Seraphim handout remain separate for their distinct subject matter.

## Canon status and printed identity

**GM ruling, 2026-11-17:** Cross Applied Technology is a **separate organization from the Corporate Court of the American Alliance (CCMA)**. An older page note dated 2026-11-10 asserted that this organization was the CCMA; that was a drafting slip carried over from the Grid Overwatch ruling recorded the same day and is retired. CAT is the printed SR3 megacorporation Cross Applied Technologies (commonly CATCo). The CCMA is the Corporate Court that charters CAS corporations and licenses Matrix technology; Grid Overwatch is the CCMA's G-men arm, not a CAT body. (GM ruling, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-17; SR3 Corporate Download, pp.58, 60, 62, 134; SR3 Portfolio of a Dragon, p.60; SR3 Matrix, pp.155, 157, 159)

## What the campaign has established

- CAT wanted sensitive DNA-sequencing and cloning equipment recovered quickly. Imogene Viola represented it; the theft overlapped the fake-crew / impersonation thread tied to 6 Steel Strings. (Sessions 2024-02-01 and 2024-02-08)
- Imogene presented herself as a senior director in forward agrarian strategy for CAT's CAS division. That is her stated position; no separate confirmation of her title is recorded. (Session 2024-02-01; Imogene Viola page)
- CAT's North American Entertainment Division is headquartered at downtown Nashville's [Lafayette Tower](../Locations/Lafayette-Tower.md), which is also identified as Cross's CAS regional headquarters. (Session 2024-02-08; Session 2026-11-12)
- A basement Ultraviolet host at Lafayette Tower is used for brainstorming and prototyping entertainment. See its [dedicated Matrix host record](../Tech/Matrix/Lafayette-Tower-CAT-Entertainment-Host.md).
- Pixel Sticks utility attacks targeted CAT subsidiaries and affiliates. Imogene confirmed arranging the original takedown contract through the shadow pipeline, then paid the crew 10,000¥ for follow-up intelligence and utility samples. (Session 2026-08-27)
- The crew suspects [The Princeps Group](../Factions/The-Princeps-Group.md) sponsored the attacks; proof remains circumstantial. Their work has repeatedly put them between Cross and Princeps. (Sessions 2026-08-27 and 2026-11-12)
- GM direction 2026-11-17: one major local Cross project in Nashville is a shamanic studies program focused on Christian shamans, studying novel totems found only in the American Southeast. See the sub-page [Southeastern Novel Totems](Cross-Applied-Technology/Southeastern-Novel-Totems.md). (GM direction, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-17)

## Divisions and operating arms

### North American Entertainment Division — campaign

The division's Nashville headquarters, entertainment R&D and basement Ultraviolet host are at Lafayette Tower. The GM also identified the tower as Cross's CAS regional headquarters. Imogene is CAT's recorded local representative, but a specific appointment to this division, its staffing and its budget are not established. (Sessions 2024-02-08 and 2026-08-27; GM direction, 2026-11-12)

### Southeast Division — campaign handout only

The player-facing [Seraphim File 7-AUGUST](../NPCs/Claude-August-CAT-Dossier.md) is signed by Section Chief A. Ferreira, Corporate Security, Southeast Division, and distributed to Lafayette Tower for Imogene and two delegates. This establishes what the handout says, not independent confirmation of the division's existence, remit, or reporting line. Its assessment of Claude August is not campaign fact merely because the prop asserts it. (Seraphim File 7-AUGUST)

### Printed divisions and branches — SR3 reference

- **Matrix Technologies Division (MTD):** produces Cross's Matrix hardware and software lines. (SR3 Matrix, pp.155, 157)
- **Cross Biomedical:** works in cultured neuralware and is described as a contender in nanoware. (SR3 Man and Machine, pp.64, 89)
- **Cross Applied Electronics:** a named operating arm in *System Failure*. (SR3 System Failure, p.58)
- **The Seraphim:** CAT's intelligence branch, described as an advanced corporate intelligence organization and listed in the CAS alongside DSI and ERLA. (SR3 State of the Art 2064, pp.26, 33; SR3 Target: Smugglers Havens, p.50)

The handout's use of the Seraphim name is consistent with the printed branch, but does not authenticate its fictional author, security program, or claims. (Seraphim File 7-AUGUST; SR3 State of the Art 2064, p.26)

## Printed corporate background — SR3

- Cross Applied Technologies, commonly CATCo, is a megacorporation. Its predecessor appeared in 2032 after Damien Knight's nanosecond buyout of Ares; it reincorporated in 2044. (SR3 Corporate Download, p.58; SR3 Portfolio of a Dragon, p.60)
- The printed 2050-era profile names Dr. Lucien Cross as President/CEO and Montreal, Republic of Quebec, as headquarters. (SR3 Portfolio of a Dragon, p.60)
- Cross traditionally emphasized pure research and licensing, headware research, and Matrix technology. It runs several Quebec LTGs and, unusually among megacorporations, does not issue its own corporate scrip. (SR3 Man and Machine, pp.12, 89; SR3 Matrix, p.155; SR3 Corporate Download, p.62)
- Ares and Damien Knight are longstanding rivals. Leonard Aurelius left Ares for Cross and became Knight's visible nemesis; the printed corporate profile describes CATCo as surviving among larger Corporate Court powers through savvy executives. (SR3 Corporate Download, pp.39, 43, 134)
- The 2064-dated printed record describes executive indecision, Ares stripping assets, Lucien Cross's reported fatal aircraft crash, and Aurelius's effort to save the company. The campaign has not established whether those later events apply to this version of CAT. (SR3 System Failure, pp.35, 86, 106)

Printed background does not override what the table has established. (GM ruling, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-10)
