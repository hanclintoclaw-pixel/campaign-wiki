# Enigma Machine

## Overview

Enigma Machine is a Nashville otaku tribe described as representing [Grid Overwatch Division](Grid-Overwatch-Division.md) in this region of the CAS. [Kne@zle](../NPCs/Kne-zle.md) is its senior otaku and the local face of grid watch. Its stated responsibility is monitoring Matrix activity in Nashville and the surrounding cities, and the tribe lives and works in a secure location maintained by the city government. (wordpress-ingest NPC source; Grid-Overwatch-Division page)

## Printed otaku framework (SR3)

- Each major sprawl contains at least one otaku tribe, sometimes more; tribes have their own subcultures and are usually led by their oldest, most experienced members, with ranks of teens or younger. (SR3 Matrix, pp.135-136)
- Tribes combine communal living, street-survival skills, an exploratory hacker mindset, and near-fetishistic regard for cyber technology. (SR3 Matrix, pp.135-136)
- Members support the tribe by selling deckmeister, programming, tech-wiz and info-broker services to other street formations, taking subsistence plus favors and protection. This is printed framework, not proof of Enigma Machine's income. (SR3 Matrix, pp.135-136)
- Recruitment is described as intuitive, followed by a probationary period: recruits run tortoises, then cyberterminals, then cyberdecks, and finally decide whether to accept a datajack. The tribe supplies a member's datajack and ASIST converter at no character-creation cost. (SR3 Matrix, pp.135-136; see Otaku Tribes and Tribal Resources, p.142)
- Otaku run a living persona and complex forms, take a +1 to Detection Factor for being otaku, and are unusually exposed to biofeedback and simsense overload. (SR3 Matrix; complex forms p.139)
- Cyberadept and technoshaman are distinct otaku paths, not interchangeable labels; Lizzo Wells calls herself a technoshaman. (SR3 Matrix, paths p.136; Session 2026-08-27)
- Otaku come largely from dispossessed orphans and runaways, dispute the nature of the Deep Resonance among themselves, and lose their abilities to the Fading as they age. None of that establishes any particular member's history. (SR3 Matrix)

## Ties to Grid Overwatch

Enigma Machine is described as representing [Grid Overwatch Division](Grid-Overwatch-Division.md) locally, with Kne@zle as its local face. The exact institutional relationship - including how much command authority G.O.D. actually holds over the tribe - remains unresolved. (wordpress-ingest NPC source; Grid-Overwatch-Division page)

The GM has ruled that Nashville's G.O.D. is the printed CCMA "G-men" of *SR3 Matrix*, p.158: part Matrix marshals, part hot-shot deckers, policing the grid under self-declared Corporate Court authority. That authority is contested - nations and corps assert their own Matrix enforcement, including Lone Star's GridSec and Renraku's Securitech - and a CCMA warrant is not automatically welcome to the [Nashville Police Department](Nashville-Police-Department.md). (GM ruling, 2026-11-10; SR3 Matrix, p.158)
