# School for Gifted Youngsters

## Overview

Professor "Chuck" X runs the School for Gifted Youngsters outside Nashville. The school trains younger people who are gifted magically or otherwise.

## What the crew knows

- Professor X runs two initiate groups: one for casters and one for adepts.
- He has been scouting for an X-Force of cutthroat mercenaries to keep the school safe.
- The school is tied to Mucky, who sought Professor X with questions about his own origin and personhood.
