# Kyron

## Overview

Awakened mage and the crew's only character with reliable astral perception. Joined Kurgan at White Creek during [Session 2026-10-01](../Sessions/2026-10-01.md) and has since become the team's authority on spirits — mostly because he is the only one who can see them.

Player attribution in the Discord voice capture is **Kira Yamato**; the GM and table also address the character as **Chiron** and **Kairon** in transcription. Confirm the character's canonical spelling against the sheet.

## Known Sheet Details Established In Play

The GM has read or asked for the following at the table. These are transcript-reported values, not sheet-verified.

- **Sorcery pool: 5** ("my source was five"), used with up to spell-force dice for combat spells.
- **Initiative: roll 1D6 + 5.** Kyron rolled a 9 in the Session 2026-10-22 combat.
- **Known combat spells:** **Power Bolt**, **Fireball**, **Mana Ball**, and **Mana Bolt**. The GM identified Mana Bolt as the key anti-spirit option — it only works on spirits, has very low drain, and can be juiced hard.
- **Magic Background** is a real skill and the GM called it "a key skill for image," using it repeatedly for spirit identification.
- **Intelligence** supports assensing and astral scanning, rolled against target number 5 for standard assensing.
- **Astral perception** is active and is the reason the GM routes spirit-related checks to him.
- **Totem: Wolf.** The GM offered a Wolf Totem vision as a bounded research endpoint at his lodge.
- He has **not yet fully entered his skills and spells** into the Tabletop Simulator character token; the GM walked him through the edit process during Session 2026-10-22 pre-game.
- The GM explicitly noted Kyron has **not yet defined his contacts**, and suggested burning a contact on a magic researcher/expert as a reasonable player decision.

## Known Facts

- Kyron is awakened and perceives astrally. He identified two fire elementals and a great-form tainted spirit of man above White Creek, and was the only witness who could track the possessed zombies and the shedim.
- He joined the Humanis evidence retrieval through Kurgan and withdrew from the burning civic center with the crew.
- In [Session 2026-10-15](../Sessions/2026-10-15.md) his Force 3, Moderate fireball wrecked the armored vans and shattered Lula Bell Sandusky's physical form. He suffered a Moderate Stun wound.
- In [Session 2026-10-22](../Sessions/2026-10-22.md) his **Force 4, Moderate Mana Ball** rolled eight dice against target number 4 for six successes, tying the shedim's own six-success defense for a Moderate wound. His later Power Bolt destroyed the weak possessing spirit in the last zombie, dropping the corpse "like a puppet with its strings cut."
- **He is the one who identified the enemy as a shedim**, recognizing the banishment signature from weaker spirits and placing the type as something new to the world within the last few years, returning to a hostile metaplane. The GM credited three successes.
- **He knows the permanent-kill answer and the crew's clock.** Banishment holds one turn of the moon — roughly thirty days. The only reliable permanent kill is opening a portal to the shedim's home metaplane and destroying it there, a major undertaking he described as beyond current means. He also established that without a ritual link, the banished entity cannot identify them.
- **He chose to keep a possessed corpse**, at the GM's direct prompting, for future study and as a possible ritual link. It rides in Grandpa's under-floor storage and is now in the compound's deep freeze. Curtis gave permission with "you're the mage, so if that's something we need, then sure."
- **His identification rolls have been unlucky.** Across the session he failed multiple magic-background checks to determine whether Sandusky was ever a real person, including one that was technically a critical failure. The GM honored the failures rather than waving him through, so his strongest statements remain explicitly speculative.

## Current Status

Alive, unwounded in the Session 2026-10-22 fight, and paid **12,000¥** plus **8 Karma** for the closed-out evidence sale. He holds the crew's only magical assets: the frozen possessed corpse and the working theory about how to kill the shedim permanently. He is about to become the crew's most important character if the entity comes back in thirty days.
