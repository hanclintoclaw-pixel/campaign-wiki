## Balance maintenance

Each PC's **Current Karma and Nuyen** section near the top of their page is the current tracking summary. Dated ledger entries remain history, not competing current totals.

- During a session, at closeout, and for between-session adjustments, update the top summary and the dated ledger in the same change. Apply an award, expense or correction once; record the event and arithmetic. A correction is not another award.
- Keep Good Karma (advancement), Karma Pool dice, awards subtotals, cached money and spendable funds distinct. Do not turn a payout subtotal into an absolute balance or charge proposed/unpriced expenses.
- Preserve lower bounds, derived-value assumptions, unresolved spending and incomplete-history qualifications. An unknown or pending balance must not be silently changed to zero.
- The GM permits **0** as an explicitly dated tracking initialization when no prior value exists. Label it as initialized, not evidence of zero lifetime wealth; keep known awards, caches and historical events intact.
- Every summary update must refresh its As-of line, retain its block markers and labels, and re-review/rebuild the revision-bound voice projection before activation. A wiki publication alone does not update the voice reader. If the summary cannot be retrieved or is stale, Cindy must decline rather than use an older ledger subtotal.
