# Humanis Evidence Retrieval

## Overview

Contract introduced in [Session 2026-09-17](../Sessions/2026-09-17.md). An anonymous Mr. Johnson hired the crew to retrieve pre-Crash physical evidence from the old **[White Creek Civic Center](../Locations/White-Creek-Civic-Center.md)** north of Nashville. The target material concerns **[Lula Bell Sandusky](../NPCs/Lula-Bell-Sandusky.md)**, a late-60s moral crusader whose public anti-metahuman / anti-Haggar commentary has become politically inconvenient.

The building is controlled by **[Paul Hardcastle](../NPCs/Paul-Hardcastle.md)** and the **[Ring of Fire](../Factions/Ring-of-Fire.md)**, a **[Humanis](../Factions/Humanis.md)**-affiliated smuggling gang.

## Established Setup

- The job is a physical retrieval, not a Matrix theft. The crew expects paper records, a catalog, and possible physical evidence.
- The evidence likely dates to Sandusky's youth around the Return-of-Magic / Crash-era chaos roughly forty years earlier.
- The Johnson does not know exactly how much must be moved, but described less than two filing cabinets and more than a single folder.
- The crew must make a secure handoff inside Nashville via burner contact after recovery.
- The Ring of Fire may not know what is in the basement or why it matters.

## Site Read

- White Creek is north of Nashville, beyond the border and city drone/radio density.
- The old police station / town hall is a two-story fortified building with a fenced parking lot, patched old police cars, reinforced transports, and guard posts.
- The town's refugee-processing infrastructure remains partly in place from the early Sixth World panic.
- The Ring of Fire appears deliberately low-tech: no visible roof cameras, no detected local drone/radio net, and no satellite dish.
- People are active on the second floor, and at least some food and power are present.
- The intermittent Matrix signal was resolved in [Session 2026-09-24](../Sessions/2026-09-24.md) as a [thin logistics host](../Tech/Matrix/White-Creek-Waystation-Host.md), apparently tapped into a fiber trunk. Mevin recovered shipment/patrol information and references to secure holding, but found no connected security controls.
