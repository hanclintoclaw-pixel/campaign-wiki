# Humanis Evidence Retrieval

## Overview

Active contract introduced in [Session 2026-09-17](../Sessions/2026-09-17.md). An anonymous Mr. Johnson hired the crew to retrieve pre-Crash physical evidence from the old **[White Creek Civic Center](../Locations/White-Creek-Civic-Center.md)** north of Nashville. The target material concerns **[Lula Bell Sandusky](../NPCs/Lula-Bell-Sandusky.md)**, a late-60s moral crusader whose public anti-metahuman / anti-Haggar commentary has become politically inconvenient.

The building is controlled by **[Paul Hardcastle](../NPCs/Paul-Hardcastle.md)** and the **[Ring of Fire](../Factions/Ring-of-Fire.md)**, a **[Humanis](../Factions/Humanis.md)**-affiliated smuggling gang.

## Established Setup

- The job is a physical retrieval, not a Matrix theft. The crew expects paper records, a catalog, and possible physical evidence.
- The evidence likely dates to Sandusky's youth around the Return-of-Magic / Crash-era chaos roughly forty years earlier.
- The Johnson does not know exactly how much must be moved, but described less than two filing cabinets and more than a single folder.
- The crew must make a secure handoff inside Nashville via burner contact after recovery.
- The Ring of Fire may not know what is in the basement or why it matters.

## Site Read

- White Creek is north of Nashville, beyond the border and city drone/radio density.
- The old police station / town hall is a two-story fortified building with a fenced parking lot, patched old police cars, reinforced transports, and guard posts.
- The town's refugee-processing infrastructure remains partly in place from the early Sixth World panic.
- The Ring of Fire appears deliberately low-tech: no visible roof cameras, no detected local drone/radio net, and no satellite dish.
- People are active on the second floor, and at least some food and power are present.
- The intermittent Matrix signal was resolved in [Session 2026-09-24](../Sessions/2026-09-24.md) as a [thin logistics host](../Tech/Matrix/White-Creek-Waystation-Host.md), apparently tapped into a fiber trunk. Mevin recovered shipment/patrol information and references to secure holding, but found no connected security controls.

## Current Assets / State

- The crew recovered a large cache labeled with Sandusky's pre-Crash arrest-record information, additional concealed paydata, and an old terminal with attached monitor. The Sandusky files have since been reviewed enough to identify her youth involvement in violent proto-Humanis activity; their full evidentiary and political value is unassessed. The extra paydata and terminal remain unexamined, and no material has been handed off.
- Curtis, Mevin, Kilimanjaro, and Kurgan reached Nashville with the recovered material during [Session 2026-10-08](../Sessions/2026-10-08.md); their escape from the immediate White Creek site is established.
- The Sandusky files include photographs, VHS tapes, typed and witness reports, and DNA evidence. They show her youth involvement in violent proto-Humanis activity against refugee groups, compromising her otherwise clean public reputation. Their exact legal/political value remains unassessed.
- **Joseph Neumann** denied that the mayor's office issued the original contract, but offered **55,000¥** for private mayoral custody of the Sandusky material (the GM recalled the original contract's quoted payment as **60,000¥ total**). Kurgan left the offer open; no sale, copy/division plan, original-Johnson contact, handoff, or payout is confirmed.
- Kurgan called **Glenn**, who projected to investigate the powerful tainted spirit. Glenn later reported the Hidden Enforcers banished it; its summoner remains unidentified, and Glenn expects Kurgan to follow up about the response.
- Near City Hall, the crew encountered a Princeps-style knockoff drone and stripped-marking CAS military drop pod. Four unidentified armed occupants left after a disguise/bluff. The crew acquired four heavy security armor sets, a prototype shotgun/assault cannon, a grenade launcher, and ammunition; exact stats and allocation remain pending.
- Mevin forged a routine pickup order for one day earlier; his [thrift-store flunkies](../NPCs/Thrift-Store-Flunkies.md) delivered the disguised Neuro-Stun VII package, which gang members carried inside.
- The breach killed two exterior guards and incapacitated several occupants. One unidentified man fled on foot during the earlier breach. The civic center was engulfed when the crew withdrew; two fire elementals were seen accelerating the fire, and Kyron perceived a great-form tainted spirit of man above it. About half the gang was away beforehand; Hardcastle's current fate is unknown.
- Kilimanjaro used the roof hatch, which was unlocked on this visit despite the previous maglock observation. He passed through the upper-floor sleeping area and descended to the basement.
- Mevin retains the old-interface device and downloaded logistics records. The crew has gas masks and two hand trucks; carrying-case costs remain unreconciled.
- **No Karma, nuyen payout, or job-completion award; acquired gear is recorded in Session 2026-10-08.** The contract remains active pending the crew's decision about the evidence and a handoff. The active scene remains nighttime, provisionally late May 2066 or later; Grandpa's approved new-armor availability implies May 30 or later.
- During [Session 2026-10-15](../Sessions/2026-10-15.md), the crew contacted the original Johnson and attempted a public physical drop in Nashville. **Lula Bell Sandusky** appeared with armed security, magically compelled Curtis, and signaled an attack when he admitted examining the material. Kyron's fireball wrecked one armored van and ignited another; the GM reported two guards dead and one surviving among those caught. Sandusky's physical body was apparently killed, and the tainted greater spirit of man previously reported banished emerged from it. The evidence remains with the crew; no transfer, payment, or award occurred.
- Sandusky's apparent death, the spirit's reappearance, the Johnson's identity/fate, the ownership of the vans, and the crew's injuries / combat resolution remain unresolved.
- During [Session 2026-10-22](../Sessions/2026-10-22.md), the crew won the drop-site fight. Kyron's Mana Ball and Kurgan's shotgun bolts drove the shedim to nine of ten boxes of damage, and Kurgan's final pass banished it. Kyron's assensing identified the entity as a **shedim** — a spirit type new to the Sixth World within the last couple of years — banished for one turn of the moon (roughly thirty days), which is not permanent death. The only reliable permanent kill is a portal to its home metaplane, beyond the crew's current means.
- **The contract is now paid.** The crew sold the evidence package, including Buzz's footage of Sandusky exploding and the monster emerging, to Mayor Haggar's office through Joseph Neumann for **12,000¥ each and 8 Karma each**, against the GM's recollection of a **60,000¥** original contract figure. A competing bid to Princeps was refused hostilely. The **original Johnson was never contacted again** and remains unidentified.
- Surviving complications: a possessed corpse is frozen at Taco's compound as Kyron's prospective ritual link; **Tom Erlichman**, a captured Humanis guard, is chained and unconscious there and has not been questioned; two guards were executed by Curtis after fleeing; the McDonald's host was wiped by Cindy Lou after its panic button was pressed.

## Open Questions

- Who is the Johnson, and why target Sandusky now?
- What does the recovered Sandusky cache establish, and does it match the Johnson's request?
- What do the extra paydata and old terminal contain?
- Where is Hardcastle, and will absent patrols or the escaped ganger bring help?
- Who directed the fire elementals, and what is the great-form tainted spirit's connection to the scene?
- Will the genuine pickup order that Mevin altered expose the deception?
- Can Kurgan's Humanis past provide access, cover, or complications?
- Will the crew accept Neumann's 55,000¥ offer, retain copies, or complete the original Johnson's handoff? Who is the original Johnson, and how will multiple interested parties change the risk?
- Who deployed the stripped-marking pod and four armed personnel near City Hall, and what were they trying to accomplish?
- What does Glenn need from Kurgan after the Hidden Enforcers' intervention, and can they learn who summoned the spirit?
- **The thirty-day clock.** The shedim returns in roughly a month. Does the crew pursue a metaplane portal, build defenses, or accept the risk on the reasoning that it cannot identify them?
- **What does the crew still hold** after selling to the mayor — copies, the Buzz footage, the concealed paydata, Mevin's old terminal?
- **What does Tom Erlichman know**, including whether Sandusky's demeanor changed at some point, and what happens to an unconscious Humanis captive held by a crew on good terms with MetroPedy?
- How does the **original Johnson** react to learning the material went to the mayor instead of them?
