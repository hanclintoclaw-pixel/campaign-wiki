# Byron Cedar / Judge Belisarius Rush Job

## Overview

Active rush contract introduced in [Session 2026-09-03](../Sessions/2026-09-03.md). **Byron L. Cedar**, corporate lawyer for **HEMP Global Agronomics**, hired **Kilimanjaro** to make sure corrupt circuit judge **Hiram Belisarius** misses court the next day without being killed, permanently removed from the docket, or obviously targeted.

## Known Facts

- Cedar has previously hired Kilimanjaro for low-risk security, surveillance, and personal-protection work.
- The offered job budget is **60,000¥ total**, potentially **15,000¥ each** for Kilimanjaro, Kurgan, Curtis, and Mevin.
- Belisarius is a gambling-addicted older human judge who has accepted payoffs from Cedar's side before.
- Belisarius is protected after hours by **Renaud Dupree**, a heavily cybered Cajun troll bodyguard.
- Belisarius's court-assigned city security is **Officer Davis Holbrook**, who generally keeps more distance when Belisarius is off work.
- The crew's current strategy is to exploit Belisarius's gambling vice and make the missed court date look like self-inflicted overindulgence.
- The active scene is at **Mucky's gambling hall**, after Curtis convinced Belisarius to follow the crew toward a supposed better game.

## Current Position

- **Belisarius** and **Dupree** are outside Mucky's with the crew.
- **Kilimanjaro** has planted his personal comm unit on Belisarius as a tracking aid.
- **8th Street** has been scattered from its alley by Kilimanjaro's flash grenade.
- The team has not yet settled the final method for incapacitating Belisarius.

## Open Questions

- What case does Cedar need delayed?
- Why did Belisarius become unreliable despite prior payoffs?
- Can the crew avoid killing or maiming Dupree if the bodyguard resists?
- Will Mucky or Melchizedek treat the crew's use of the gambling hall as acceptable business, an insult, or an opportunity?
