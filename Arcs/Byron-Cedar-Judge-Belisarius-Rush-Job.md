# Byron Cedar / Judge Belisarius Rush Job

## Overview

Contract introduced in [Session 2026-09-03](../Sessions/2026-09-03.md) and **completed and paid** in [Session 2026-09-10](../Sessions/2026-09-10.md). **[Byron L. Cedar](../NPCs/Byron-Cedar.md)**, corporate lawyer for **[HEMP Global Agronomics](../Organizations/HEMP-Global-Agronomics.md)**, hired **[Kilimanjaro](../PCs/Kilimanjaro.md)** to keep corrupt circuit judge **[Hiram Belisarius](../NPCs/Judge-Hiram-Belisarius.md)** out of court the following day without killing him or permanently removing him from the docket. The intended discretion did not survive the extraction, but Cedar explicitly accepted the result.

## Established Setup

- Cedar previously employed Kilimanjaro for security and surveillance. The offered budget was **60,000¥**, originally discussed as four **15,000¥** shares.
- Belisarius owed gambling debts around town. **[Renaud Dupree](../NPCs/Renaud-Dupree.md)** handled his after-hours protection; **[Officer Davis Holbrook](../NPCs/Officer-Davis-Holbrook.md)** handled court security.
- The opening session scattered **[8th Street](../Factions/8th-Street.md)**, planted Kilimanjaro's comm unit on the judge, and lured the target pair toward **[Mucky's Place](../Locations/Muckys-Place.md)**.

## Resolution

- Curtis persuaded Belisarius to accept a gambling advantage through his cyber-eye, paired through Grandpa with Cindy Lou providing analysis. Mevin worried about a second local signal; Dupree was the likely tracking-feed recipient, not a proven offsite recording service.
- **[Mucky](../NPCs/Mucky.md)** knowingly agreed to supply alcohol and a distraction for **10% of the take**. He wanted the useful corrupt judge back at work after the missed day, not removed permanently.
- **[Big Lou / Big Blue](../NPCs/Big-Lou.md)** drew Dupree to a doorway. Mevin applied a tranq patch, Kilimanjaro carried the judge through the rear exit, and Curtis positioned Grandpa for extraction.
- The Finisher's attempt to shoot out the pursuer's tires failed. Dupree chased in an armored vehicle with a pop-up machine gun. Curtis made a sharp getaway turn while The Finisher covered him; Kilimanjaro's grenade struck the pursuing engine compartment and caused a crash. **Dupree survived.**
- Mevin secured the judge, blocked his cyber-eye view, and located the transmitter. Cindy's successful signal-spoofing support helped the crew stage a gambling-debt shakedown rather than a corporate hit.
- Curtis located an out-of-the-way holding place. The crew kept Belisarius incapacitated through the next court day and released him alive near **[8th Street Cafe](../Locations/8th-Street-Cafe.md)** with staged injuries and a drunken debt-collection story.
- The abduction generated a missing-person news story. The cover was accepted as the crew's resolved plan, not proof that all witnesses, records, or consequences disappeared.
- Cedar gave a **surprisingly positive confirmation** despite the public kidnapping and shootout. The GM explicitly left a mystery in that reaction.

## Rewards / Accounting

- **5 Karma to all PCs**, per the GM's closeout instruction, including absent/paused PCs without implying attendance.
- **Curtis: 20,000¥; Mevin: 20,000¥**, confirmed final payouts.
- Kilimanjaro volunteered his own share to cover Mucky's assistance so the other active players' payments were not reduced. His precise remaining net payout was not stated; do not subtract Mucky's cut again from Curtis or Mevin.
- Kilimanjaro lost several hundred nuyen at cards; exact losses and incidental expenses were not priced.

## Current Position

The contract is closed. The current endpoint is **the following evening after court closed**, provisionally **2066-05-21** based on the inherited **2066-05-20** start. The absolute calendar remains provisional; no exact in-world stop time was stated. No next mission was chosen.
