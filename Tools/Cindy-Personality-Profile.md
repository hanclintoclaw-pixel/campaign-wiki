## Refinement loop — your corrections are the input

Tell me naturally what fits or does not: “less stage sweetness,” “she would tease Mevin, not accuse him,” “she likes performing even with no audience,” or supply a scene and your preferred reaction. These are examples, **not feedback already attributed to the GM**.

1. Sol records the actual correction, its Discord source and the affected trait in a new versioned feedback file. Nothing is inferred as approval from silence.
2. Proposed feedback stays visible but does not alter the preview. Explicitly approved wording replaces only the targeted trait text, retaining original evidence plus a GM override receipt.
3. A new trait needs a new profile claim; contradictory campaign facts require explicit reconciliation, not an automatic text override that silently rewrites the wiki.
4. Qwen recompiles a new offline preview. Existing source snapshots, profiles and feedback remain available; exclusive output creation prevents accidental replacement through the CLI.
5. Compare the same scenes with the old and candidate profiles. Judge distinctiveness, enjoyment, humor, relationship specificity, concrete judgments, restraint and factual boundaries. The GM's taste beats an automatic personality score.
6. Only after review, make a compact approved projection and separately activate it in Initiative 1.001 during a coordinated idle change. Publication alone is not activation.

The compiler's “approved” flag is a **trusted-local operator annotation**, not Discord authentication, proof of canon agreement, or live deployment authority. There is no remote feedback-ingestion endpoint or automatic learning from transcripts.
