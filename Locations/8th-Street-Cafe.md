# 8th Street Cafe

## Overview

The named landmark near which the crew released **[Judge Hiram Belisarius](../NPCs/Judge-Hiram-Belisarius.md)** at the end of the **[Byron Cedar court-delay job](../Arcs/Byron-Cedar-Judge-Belisarius-Rush-Job.md)**.

## Known Facts

- The GM explicitly named the cafe during the resolved release plan in [Session 2026-09-10](../Sessions/2026-09-10.md).
- Belisarius was held elsewhere through the following court day, then left near the cafe after court closed under a staged drunken gambling-debt shakedown story.
- The story pointed toward his familiar 8th Street gambling area. **[8th Street](../Factions/8th-Street.md)** was not established as the actual kidnapper.
- This appears to be the diner / neighborhood from the opening session, but its exact identity with the earlier unnamed diner, street address, ownership, and layout remain unconfirmed.
