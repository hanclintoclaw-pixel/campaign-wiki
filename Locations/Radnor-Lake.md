# Radnor Lake

## Overview

**Radnor Lake** is an overgrown wilderness pocket south of Nashville. By 2066, it is no longer a simple public park: the water is suspiciously clear, the area feels maintained despite the lack of obvious caretakers, and the surrounding brush offers plenty of cover for animals, squatters, and awakened things.

**Map:** [Nashville Campaign Map](../Maps/Nashville-Campaign-Map.md) pin at `36.06093990746219, -86.80293496992576`.

The crew visited Radnor Lake during the [Session 2026-08-13](../Sessions/2026-08-13.md) player-driven follow-up to find **[Chunky Sparkles](../NPCs/Chunky-Sparkles.md)** for Valgaut's Wyrmwatch / Draco Foundation service.

## Known Features

- Overgrown lake shore south of Nashville.
- Suspiciously clear water compared to normal local expectations.
- Washed-out roads, gullies, wooded approaches, hiding places, and scattered structures around the lake.
- A few distant residents or occupied structures remain, but the immediate encounter area was mostly empty.
- The site carries enough loose mana to attract or sustain awakened creatures.
- Valgaut sensed a broader malevolent hostility around the lake during the overnight watch.

## Awakened Activity

- **Chunky Sparkles** bedded down under a collapsed tree near the lake after fleeing the Pixel Sticks / Happy Cat fallout.
- **Blood ticks** - oversized awakened carnivorous insects - were active around a clearing near the waterline.
- A gator-like aquatic awakened predator lurked just offshore and appeared to be watching or hunting near Chunky Sparkles.
- The gator-like predator had regeneration or a similar recovery trait; Kurgan brought it down and Valgaut destroyed it before it could recover.

## Session 2026-08-13 Outcome

The crew used Matrix research, Taco's old handling context, Herrick's prisoner information, and Valgaut's magical knowledge to find Chunky Sparkles here. Curtis provided drone overwatch from **Grandpa**, Kurgan and Valgaut approached with bait and command phrases, and the team intervened when the lake predator threatened Chunky Sparkles.

After the fight, Chunky Sparkles followed the crew out of the Radnor Lake area, allowing Valgaut to complete the Wyrmwatch follow-up.

## Open Questions

- Who or what keeps the water so clean?
- Was the hostile magical feeling only the dead gator-like predator, or is something deeper tied to the lake?
- Who are the distant residents or maintainers around the lake?
- Will the death of the lake predator destabilize the local awakened ecosystem?
