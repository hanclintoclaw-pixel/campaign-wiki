# Taco's Garage and Workshop

## Overview

Large vehicle repair, salvage, and workshop site run by **[Taco](../NPCs/Taco.md)**. It is now also the mapped location for **Curtis's Dolphin Rescue** planning.

## Address / Map

- **Address:** 216-226 Thompson Ln, Nashville, TN 37211
- **Map point:** `36.1109641, -86.7411965`
- **Confidence:** exact address range, mapped to the midpoint between the 216 and 226 Thompson Lane geocodes

## Known Facts

- Taco runs a large vehicle repair / salvage operation here.
- Curtis is an ally and student of Taco, making this one of Curtis's most important logistics locations.
- The site is the current player-safe mapped location for **Curtis's Dolphin Rescue** and related habitat preparation work.
- By 2026-05-28, Taco's property was being considered as possible support infrastructure for a future **Core 7** dolphin relocation/rescue plan.
- Taco previously kept **Chunky Sparkles** in an improvised field and planned to build a pen, but the creature flew away before he secured it properly.
- Behind the rundown gas station sits **Curtis's dolphin and hurricane-seal habitat** — a reinforced garage enclosure Curtis spent months and substantial in-game resources building, complete with storm pumps, storm drains, three-phase/220 power, a dispenser, and televisions that show tuna. The GM described it as "a functioning SeaWorld attraction." It has its own page: [Curtis Dolphin and Hurricane Seal Habitat](../PCs/Curtis-Dolphin-and-Hurricane-Seal-Habitat.md).
- The site functions as the crew's **safehouse and containment location**. In [Session 2026-10-22](../Sessions/2026-10-22.md) the crew withdrew here after the McDonald's fight and left two very different captives: **a possessed zombie in the deep freeze** for Kyron's study, and **[Tom Erlichman](../NPCs/Tom-Erlichman.md)**, then an unconscious restrained Humanis guard. **Update, [Session 2026-10-29](../Sessions/2026-10-29.md): Tom was released as Kurgan's Level-1 informant; he is no longer a captive on the property.** The frozen-corpse thread remains unresolved.
- The GM established on the record that the compound **has a deep-freeze setup** the crew has used before for a dead body, which the table half-remembered as a haunted horse. Curtis's stimpaks and painkillers are also kept here.
- The habitat was originally built by having **Cindy Lou walk the crew through the real-world engineering steps** — water baffles, storm protection — as one of the GM's AI-capability tests, then rendered as an in-world applet mini-game Curtis engaged with every morning.
