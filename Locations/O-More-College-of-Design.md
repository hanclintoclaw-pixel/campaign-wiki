# The O'More College of Design

## Overview

**The O'More College of Design** is an abandoned art and design museum / academy in Franklin. It was left to rot during the **Crash of 2029**, and by the time the shadowrunner crew investigated it, the place had become a derelict hostile-held site rather than a functioning school or gallery.

The crew cleared the location of hostiles. **Kurgan** is now planning to restore and fortify it as his wilderness headquarters.
