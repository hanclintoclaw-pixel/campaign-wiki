# Thrift Store Flophouse

## Overview

A hidden crew safehouse in East Nashville, held onto after a job that never made it into the written records.

## Address / Map

- **Address:** 699-615 Main St, Nashville, TN 37206
- **Map point:** `36.1745339, -86.7614195`
- **Confidence:** exact address range, mapped to the midpoint between the 699 and 615 Main Street geocodes

## Known Facts

- The team held onto this location after an undocumented job.
- It is used as a place for the crew to lie low.
- Nobody outside the group is known to know about it.
- Despite the name, its value is not comfort; its value is secrecy, deniability, and a place to disappear for a while.
- The bottom floor operates as a boring thrift-store cover, maintained by two low-profile flunkies who keep the storefront open and help the crew maintain the safehouse's plausible daily rhythm.

## Expanded Lifestyle Profile

Using the optional Detailed Lifestyles rules from *Sprawl Survival Guide* pp. 127-145, the flophouse is built as a **Middle-equivalent group-supported fallback safehouse**: uncomfortable and low-glamour, but with enough space, security, cover business, and internal support to function above a normal Low lifestyle.

### Category Ratings

- **Area:** B / Middle-equivalent (**3 points**) - Main Street frontage in East Nashville: public enough to hide in normal business traffic, not polished enough to attract serious scrutiny.
- **Comforts:** Low (**2 points**) - basic utilities, cheap food, backup supplies, and improvised sleeping arrangements; survivable, not pleasant.
- **Entertainment:** Low (**2 points**) - old trideo, basic Matrix/telecom access, thrift-store media, and whatever the crew brings.
- **Furnishings:** Middle (**3 points**) - the thrift-store cover keeps the place stocked with mismatched but functional beds, couches, shelves, curtains, and household junk.
- **Security:** High (**4 points**) - reinforced back rooms, controlled access, watcher habits from the flunkies, cameras/alarms hidden in store clutter, and crew-installed locks.
- **Space:** High (**4 points**) - storefront below plus concealed upstairs/back-room crash space, storage, and enough room for multiple runners to lie low.

**Base total:** 18 points, the same point total as an all-Middle lifestyle under the SSG category model. Treat base cost as **5,000¥/month** before lifestyle Edges/Flaws.

### Lifestyle Edges

- **Defensive Setup (+0.10):** cluttered shelves, blind corners, stock-room choke points, and back-room sight lines give prepared defenders useful cover.
- **Hasty Access (+0.05):** rear exit, service access, and shop-floor movement paths let people get in or out quickly when the safehouse is not fully compromised.
- **No More Neighbors (+0.15):** adjacent/upper spaces are kept empty, ignored, or controlled enough that nobody nearby asks useful questions.
- **Privacy Screen 2 (+0.20):** thrift-store cover, interior curtains, sound-damping junk, blocked sight lines, and boring public traffic impose **+2 TN** on outside surveillance attempts.

**Edge modifier subtotal:** +0.50, matching the SSG sample **Safehouse** package.

### Lifestyle Flaws

- **Cursed Amenities (-0.10):** hot water, heat, old wiring, and plumbing all work until the worst possible moment.
- **Bad Reputation (-0.05):** contractors and delivery people dislike the property; it reads as cheap, weird, and not worth extra attention.
- **Patchwork Engineering (-0.05):** renovations and upgrades take longer because everything is layered over old retail space, salvage repairs, and inherited problems.
- **This Isn't Sam's Pizza (-0.05):** the storefront's old LTG/listing history still produces wrong calls, bad directory data, and occasional retail confusion.

**Flaw modifier subtotal:** -0.25.

### Final Working Cost

- **Base lifestyle cost:** 5,000¥/month
- **Net Edge/Flaw modifier:** +0.25
- **Working monthly upkeep:** **6,250¥/month**

For campaign use, this cost represents keeping the site viable as a group safehouse: thrift-store front expenses, utilities, quiet repairs, flunky support, cheap supplies, and enough security maintenance that the place remains useful after runs.

### Resident / Staff Notes

- **Two flunkies** keep the thrift-store cover open, rotate stock, notice suspicious customers, and maintain the everyday pattern that makes the building boring.
- The flunkies are not assumed to be combatants. If threatened, their best use is warning, delay, plausible-deniability chatter, and keeping the front business normal.
- The site is a fallback and recovery location, not a full lifestyle replacement for every runner unless the crew intentionally moves in.
