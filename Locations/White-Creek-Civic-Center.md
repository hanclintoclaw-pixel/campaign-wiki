# White Creek Civic Center

## Overview

Old police station / town hall in **White Creek**, a small ruined hill-country community north of Nashville. During the early Return-of-Magic / Crash-era chaos, the site helped process refugees fleeing rural areas into Nashville. In the current campaign era, it is controlled by **[Paul Hardcastle](../NPCs/Paul-Hardcastle.md)** and the **[Ring of Fire](../Factions/Ring-of-Fire.md)**.

## Known Site Details

- Two-story municipal building with an attached fenced parking lot.
- Former police / civic infrastructure, including old checkpoint or refugee-processing remnants.
- Patched old police cars with Humanis iconography; some are functional, some are parts vehicles.
- Reinforced transports and trucks support the smuggling operation.
- Guard posts cover the exterior; at least two visible guards were present on either corner during the 2026-09-17 recon.
- Front door, side / maintenance or loading door, possible windows, and roof access were identified.
- A back roof hatch is protected by a maglock.
- The roof has old HVAC / vent equipment, including a food-smoke vent and dead roofing equipment where Buzz is now hidden.

## Security Read

- Deliberately low-tech posture: no visible cameras, no detected drone/radio security, and no satellite dish.
- People are active on the second floor.
- The building has working power and at least some cooking / living activity.
- There is an intermittent Matrix signal associated with the location.
- The crew estimates the Ring of Fire may have up to about twenty men associated with the site, though not all are necessarily present at once.

## Current Crew Assets

- **[Buzz](../Vehicles/Buzz.md)** is hidden on the roof as a passive listening device for several days.
- The crew has identified the site for a future breach or spoof/distraction plan.
