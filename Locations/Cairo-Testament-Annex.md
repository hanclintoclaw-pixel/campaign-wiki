# Cairo Testament Annex

## Overview

The **Cairo Testament Annex** is a small Draco Foundation facility in Cairo, Illinois, just inside the UCAS side of the river-border routes north of the CAS. It serves as the Wyrmwatch Circle's regional intake, archive, and initiation site for outside awakened associates who cannot be processed through more visible Foundation offices.

Publicly, the Annex is a preservation office and secure document-storage site tied to Foundation grants, estate records, and awakened-history research. Privately, it is a warded meeting house, ritual classroom, and oath chamber for candidates whose work touches draconic legacies or dangerous magical material.

## Exterior

The Annex occupies a restored brick insurance-and-title office near the old riverfront commercial district. Its sign is intentionally dull: **Draco Foundation Testamentary Records Annex - By Appointment Only**. The windows are narrow, the public lobby is over-lit, and the waiting room smells like paper, old rain, and industrial dehumidifiers.

Nothing about the building announces power. That is the point. Wyrmwatch wants candidates to understand that the Foundation's most important work often looks like custody, patience, and boring paperwork until the wrong file starts bleeding astral heat.

## Secure Areas

- **Public records lobby:** a mundane front office with locked cabinets, a receptionist window, and strict appointment logs.
- **The Dry Stacks:** climate-controlled archive shelves holding harmless grant files in front and sensitive magical custody records deeper inside.
- **Seven-Seal Classroom:** a small instruction room used for candidate interviews, magical ethics briefings, and oath rehearsal.
- **The Testament Chamber:** a warded initiation room below grade, built around a circular floor inlay of brass scale, ink, and black stone.
- **Custody Lockers:** individual lockers for sealed links, oath tokens, research theses, and temporarily held awakened objects.
- **River Door:** an emergency egress and discreet delivery route toward the riverfront service alleys.

## The Testament Chamber

The initiation chamber is deliberately restrained. It has no dragon statue, no corporate logo, and no theatrical hoard. The floor is a dark circle divided by thin brass lines. Around the wall are sealed document niches, each marked with a date rather than a name.

The Circle teaches that a secret kept for pride becomes poison, but a secret kept in custody can become service. Candidates are asked what they are preserving, who they are preserving it from, and what they will surrender if the answer is themselves.

## Wards and Security

The Annex mixes mundane and magical protection:

- controlled appointment access and paper sign-in logs;
- locked stacks and archive cages;
- low-profile watcher coverage during candidate visits;
- layered wards around the Testament Chamber and custody lockers;
- Foundation legal and diplomatic pressure if local authorities get curious.

The site is secure enough for initiation and sensitive briefings, but it is not a fortress. Its best defense is that almost everyone who matters benefits from pretending it is only a document annex.

## Use at the Table

Use the Annex for:

- Valgaut's Wyrmwatch admission and Grade 1 initiation;
- Draco Foundation briefings that should not happen inside the CAS;
- artifact custody, lore verification, and sealed-record scenes;
- quiet moral pressure from a patron that speaks softly but keeps receipts.
