# South Side Highwaymen

## Overview

The **South Side Highwaymen** are a go-gang operating in the old I-65 / 840 southern corridor outside Nashville. They are not a top-tier city power, but they appear established enough to mark territory, patrol roads, and function as a crooked form of local law enforcement for people living around tucked-away homesteads and back roads.

## Known Facts

- Their territory covers part of the southern route the damaged **[Pixel Sticks scorpion drone](../Vehicles/Pixel-Sticks-Scorpion-Drone.md)** used after fleeing the Vernon Winfrey arkoblock fight.
- Their tags use visible go-gang iconography meant to be read at speed.
- Their presence suggests local homesteads and small properties may pay protection or tolls.
- They likely fought or chased the damaged scorpion drone after it entered their territory.
- During the 2026-08-06 stakeout, three armed Highwaymen vehicles returned to the area near the disabled drone.
- Curtis's drones and the damaged scorpion drone wrecked or drove off the returning vehicles.

## Relationships

- Linked to the **[Pixel Sticks / Military-Grade Utilities Run](../Arcs/Pixel-Sticks-Utility-Run.md)** as fallout from the escaped scorpion drone.
- Potentially hostile toward **[Curtis](../PCs/Curtis.md)** and **[Kurgan](../PCs/Kurgan.md)** if they identify who interfered with the drone-site fight.
