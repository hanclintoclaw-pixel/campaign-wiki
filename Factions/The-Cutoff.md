# The Cutoff

## Overview

The Cutoff is a wildcat outcast community on Capshaw Mountain. Its host describes it as a mountain waystation for outcasts, travelers and border people, rather than a town, corporation or gang.

It presents itself as a rough but functional stop between wasteland roads and civilized supply lines: a place for trade, repairs, a meal, road warnings and deciding whether the city below is worth dealing with today.
