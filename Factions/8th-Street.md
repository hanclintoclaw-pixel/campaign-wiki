# 8th Street

## Overview

**8th Street** is a minor Nashville alley crew / dice-game group introduced during the Judge Belisarius rush job.

## Known Facts

- 8th Street is not a major gang; it appears to be a handful of toughs claiming an alley and running low-end gambling.
- **Judge Hiram Belisarius** regularly gambles with them and owes them money.
- Named or overheard figures include **Skeezix** and **Butternut Squash**.
- **Kilimanjaro** assessed them as street toughs rather than hardened professionals.
- Kilimanjaro scattered them with a flash grenade aimed near pallets, disorienting them and clearing the alley without lethal intent.

## Relationships

- Linked to [Judge Hiram Belisarius](../NPCs/Judge-Hiram-Belisarius.md) through gambling debts.
- Disrupted by [Kilimanjaro](../PCs/Kilimanjaro.md) during [Session 2026-09-03](../Sessions/2026-09-03.md).

## Relevant Sessions

- **2026-09-03** - challenged Kilimanjaro in the alley, then fled after the flash grenade.
