## One gang (GM ruling, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-10)

The **Mall Rats** and the **Mallrats** are **one gang** seen in two eras: the occupied-mall faction picture of 2024, and **Valgaut**'s background membership reported in 2026. One organization, two eras - [Mallrats](Mallrats.md) is this page, not a second gang.

## Overview

A lower-significance faction in the contested mall arc. The recovered archive treats them as present but less central than the Steel Backs.

## What the crew knows

- The Mall Rats are part of the faction picture at the occupied mall.
- They were explicitly described as less significant than the Steel Backs.
- Their importance may be local/social rather than strategic.

- **Valgaut** is a former member - the same gang reported in his 2026 background as the "Mallrats" (see [Mallrats](Mallrats.md)).
