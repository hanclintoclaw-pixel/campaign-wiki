# Humanis

## Overview

Anti-metahuman extremist faction with renewed active relevance through the **[Ring of Fire](Ring-of-Fire.md)** and the White Creek evidence-retrieval job.

## Known Facts

- **Kurgan** is explicitly noted as a former Humanis member.
- **[Paul Hardcastle](../NPCs/Paul-Hardcastle.md)** is a Humanis figure of note north of Nashville.
- **[Ring of Fire](Ring-of-Fire.md)** is a Humanis-affiliated smuggling gang whose members are called Torchmen.
- The Ring of Fire operates from **[White Creek Civic Center](../Locations/White-Creek-Civic-Center.md)**, an old police station / town hall north of Nashville.
- The gang appears to function as a smuggling affiliate or contractor rather than a formal public Humanis office.
- **[Lula Bell Sandusky](../NPCs/Lula-Bell-Sandusky.md)** is not confirmed as Humanis, but her public anti-metahuman / anti-Haggar commentary overlaps with Humanis talking points.

## Relationships

- Linked to **Kurgan** through former membership.
- Linked to the Ring of Fire through ideology, personnel, iconography, and smuggling-route support.
