# Ring of Fire

## Canon status (GM ruling, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-10)

No printed SR3 equivalent: **zero** matches across the 19-book local SR3 corpus. **Table-created** for this campaign - not a re-skin of a printed organization, and printed canon makes no claim about it.

Name-collision caution: the two "Ring of Fire" lines in the local SR3 corpus are the **volcanic** Ring of Fire - a seismology comparison in *System Failure* and a *Year of the Comet* disaster headline - not an organization. This gang is unrelated to either, and the shared name is coincidence.


## Overview

Humanis-affiliated smuggling gang based around **[White Creek Civic Center](../Locations/White-Creek-Civic-Center.md)** north of Nashville. Its members are called **Torchmen**.

## Known Facts

- Led or fronted by **[Paul Hardcastle](../NPCs/Paul-Hardcastle.md)**.
- Operates from an old police station / town hall in White Creek.
- Maintains a fortified low-tech base with old police vehicles, reinforced transports, guard posts, and makeshift barricades.
- Runs or protects overland smuggling routes from the UCAS / Kentucky side toward Nashville while avoiding federal attention.
- Keeps routes clear, patrols for traps or obstacles, and appears to manage or bribe border-guard relationships.
- Uses Humanis tattoos, iconography, and rally affiliation, but functions more like a smuggling affiliate / contractor than a formal public Humanis office.

## Current Relevance

The crew breached the base in [Session 2026-09-24](../Sessions/2026-09-24.md) after Mevin forged a pickup order in its [waystation host](../Tech/Matrix/White-Creek-Waystation-Host.md). Gang members unwittingly carried the disguised gas package inside. Two exterior guards were killed, several occupants incapacitated, exterior cladding ignited, and one unidentified man escaped on foot. The gang is **not confirmed destroyed**: about half its roughly twenty members were away before the assault, and Hardcastle's fate is unknown.

Recovered logistics and personal-message material showed discontent, long/overnight pickup patrols, and considerable operational independence, without resolving higher Humanis command links. The crew is now beginning the basement evidence search; no recovered Sandusky material or completed contract is recorded.
