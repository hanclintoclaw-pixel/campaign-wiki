# Draco Foundation

<!-- cindy-canon: printed-sr3 same-org -->

## Canon status (GM ruling, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-10)

Same organization as the printed SR3 body: the **Draco Foundation**, named in 11 of the 19 local SR3 books. Where this table's portrayal differs from print, that is our own characterisation of one organization, not a different organization.


## Overview

Major magical-research / intervention institution with a strong reputation for addressing dangerous magical problems.

## Known Facts

- The Draco Foundation is treated like a major NGO/research institution dealing with magical trouble.
- It has a generally positive reputation, though some people suspect hidden agendas.
- It does not have formal remit to operate openly in the CAS.
- Kari Wilkerson represented Foundation interests in Nashville as a private citizen.
- The Foundation maintains the UCAS-side [Wyrmwatch Circle](Draco-Foundation/Wyrmwatch-Circle.md), an initiatory circle for sponsored awakened associates whose work touches draconic lore, dangerous magical phenomena, or artifact custody.

## Relationships

- Linked to **Kari Wilkerson**.
- Linked to the toxic magical biomaterial / egg-fragment investigation.
- Linked to [Valgaut](../PCs/Valgaut.md) through his Draco Foundation connection and possible Wyrmwatch initiation path.
- Compared and contrasted with the **Hidden Enforcers** and federal authorities.
