# Mallrats

**Mallrats** is the preferred term. **Mall Rats** and **Mall-Rats** are aliases for this same gang, not a second group (GM ruling, New Cindy GM Interaction Thread, 2026-11-19). The former separate page **[Mall Rats](Mall-Rats.md)** is now a redirect stub.

## Overview

One gang seen in two eras: the lower-significance faction in the contested-mall picture of 2024, and the group **Valgaut** reports as a former member in his background. The recovered archive treats the Mallrats as present in the mall conflict but less central than the **Steel Backs**.

## What the Crew Knows

- The Mallrats are part of the faction picture at the occupied mall.
- They were explicitly described as less significant than the **Steel Backs**.
- Their importance may be local/social rather than strategic.
- **Valgaut** is explicitly noted as a former Mallrats member.
