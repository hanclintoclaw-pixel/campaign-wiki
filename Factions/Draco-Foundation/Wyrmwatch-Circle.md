# Draco Foundation - Wyrmwatch Circle

## Overview

The **Wyrmwatch Circle** is a UCAS-side Draco Foundation initiatory circle that cultivates awakened field assets, occult researchers, and trusted draconic-lore specialists who may be useful to the Foundation's long work. It operates legally inside the UCAS border and does not claim public authority in the CAS.

For Nashville purposes, the Circle is reachable because it maintains the [Cairo Testament Annex](../../Locations/Cairo-Testament-Annex.md) near the UCAS/CAS river-border routes. Candidates from the CAS are not treated as open Foundation employees; they are sponsored as outside associates whose skills and loyalties align with the Foundation's mission.

## Public Cover

Publicly, Wyrmwatch is a small Foundation-affiliated research and stewardship program focused on:

- post-Dunkelzahn testamentary scholarship;
- awakened artifacts and hazardous magical material custody;
- draconic folklore, language, and historical pattern analysis;
- grants and field support for vetted awakened researchers.

Its boring paperwork says preservation, education, and risk review. Its real value is that it gives the Foundation a disciplined way to train and bind useful awakened specialists without making them full Foundation staff.

## Initiatory Function

Rules basis: **Shadowrun Third Edition** and **Magic in the Shadows** magical group and initiation rules.

Wyrmwatch is an **initiatory magical group** with strong dedicated and research-oriented traits. It is open to different magical paths when the candidate's work serves the Foundation's goals, especially draconic lore, dangerous awakened phenomena, artifact custody, and field investigation.

### Working Strictures

- **Oath:** members swear to preserve dangerous magical knowledge from abuse and to treat draconic legacies as obligations, not trophies.
- **Limited Membership:** membership is by sponsorship only; suitable candidates must be Awakened and useful to Foundation-aligned work.
- **Secrecy:** members may acknowledge Foundation contact, but not Wyrmwatch membership, internal rites, archives, or secured sites.
- **Deed:** each member owes field service, research, recovery work, or containment assistance when called.
- **Fraternity:** members assist other Circle members with magical threats, safe custody, and lore verification.
- **Material Link:** full initiates may be asked to place a sealed symbolic link, research thesis, blood spot, or oath token in Foundation custody.

### Resources

Wyrmwatch has **High Resources** for a small circle because it can draw on Draco Foundation facilities, archives, legal support, and grant channels. Ordinary dues are often waived or converted into service obligations for sponsored assets. The Foundation would rather collect reliable work, vetted reports, and recoverable artifacts than small monthly payments.

## Valgaut Path

Valgaut is a good candidate because he is a physical adept with a documented interest in dragons, awakened creatures, and magical theory, plus an existing campaign connection to the Draco Foundation.

Recommended table path:

1. **Sponsorship:** Valgaut is sponsored as an outside awakened field associate, not an employee.
2. **Admission cost:** pay **3 Karma** to join the magical group. First-month dues are waived in exchange for the Deed stricture.
3. **Compatibility test:** roll Valgaut's Magic 6 against **TN 5**, representing a five-member active intake circle. One success admits him.
4. **Initiation choice:** if admitted, Valgaut may pursue Grade 1 group initiation.
5. **Cost:** Grade 1 group initiation is **12 Karma**, or **9 Karma with a qualifying ordeal**.
6. **Recommended ordeal:** Oath or Deed. Oath binds him to the Circle's secrecy and stewardship; Deed requires a Foundation-approved field service.
7. **Payoff:** if he completes Grade 1 normally, Magic rises from 6 to 7 and, as an adept, he gains **+1 Power Point**.

## Campaign Use

Use Wyrmwatch when the campaign needs:

- a Draco Foundation path for Valgaut's initiation;
- an occult patron that values dragon lore without handing out simple answers;
- jobs involving awakened artifacts, sealed records, dangerous specimens, or old testamentary obligations;
- a UCAS-side contact point that can help without operating openly in Nashville;
- a competing model to the Hidden Enforcers: scholarly, draconic, philanthropic, and still quietly coercive.
