# Campaign wiki maintenance

## PC current balances

For any confirmed Karma or nuyen adjustment during play, session closeout or between sessions, update the PC page's top `## Current Karma and Nuyen` block AND its dated ledger in the same edit. Apply each event once and record before/delta/after arithmetic or an explicit correction; check for an earlier application first. Preserve the `current-pc-balances:v1` markers and field labels. Do not confuse advancement Good Karma with Karma Pool, or current spendable nuyen with an earned subtotal or cached money. Preserve lower bounds, assumptions, unknown amounts and unresolved expenses. Initialize a missing tracking value to zero only under the GM's standing permission, labeled and dated as initialization, never as reconstructed history. Update the As-of line.

These pages are pinned to the live voice registry. Stage edits before touching the live checkout, rebind all surviving approved text, explicitly review the changed balance block, and rebuild/test a new generation. Publication alone is not voice activation; preserve fail-closed source/revision checks and coordinate any idle component restart. Follow the voice-bridge operations and campaign registry re-review skills.

## Machine-readable table-reference maintenance

For player-requested edits, between-session changes and voice-session closeout, update any existing `table_reference` together with supporting prose/history/ledgers in the same change. Preserve all qualifications, status, units, references and As-of date; apply events once, never turn missing data into zero/none. PC current-balance blocks remain canonical for tracked totals, referenced rather than duplicated. Run `scripts/validate_table_reference.py --require` on migrated changed pages (PyYAML required), then review source agreement; formatting is not truth or permission. At closeout list changed PC/NPC/asset pages as validated, unmigrated or unresolved. Absent blocks are unmigrated, not a pass: do not start a bulk conversion or invent a schema during an unrelated edit. Stage pinned edits and rebind/review the voice projection before separately approved activation. The proposed v1 contract, pilot boundary and full workflow are in [Table Reference Standard](Tools/Table-Reference-Standard.md). No new voice capability is implied by publishing these instructions.
